# Yoruba — Nursery 2 · First Term · term paper

class: Nursery 2
term: 1st Term

[objective]
"Yàrá ìkáwé" in English is the ___ || classroom || dining room || reading room || sleeping room

"Good morning" in Yoruba is ___ || Ẹ̀ káàárọ̀ || Ẹ̀ káàlẹ̀ || Ẹ̀ káàṣánṣá || Ẹ̀ máàdọ́rùn
"Good afternoon" is said as ___ || Ẹ̀ káàṣánṣá || Ẹ̀ káàárọ̀ || Ọ́ dàbọ̀ || Ẹ ṣé
We greet an elder at night with ___ || Ẹ̀ máàdọ́rùn || Ẹ̀ káàárọ̀ || Ọ́ nàṣẹ́ || Àlàáfíà ni
A boy greets an elder by ___ || ìdòbì (prostration) || ìkúnlẹ̀ (kneeling) || standing and waving || clapping the hands twice

The reply to "Ọ́ dàbọ̀" is ___ || "Ọ́ dábọ̀, ààbọ̀" || "Ẹ ṣé gan-an" || "Bákan náà" || "Ààbọ̀ ni ọla"
Numbers: the Yoruba word for 5 is ___ || àárún || ẹ̀fà || ẹ̀sán || ẹ̀jọ
"Ẹ̀wá" is the number ___ || ten || eight || four || nine
Counting backwards from ẹ̀wá, the next word is ___ || ẹ̀sán || ẹ̀ta || àárún || ọ̀kan
"Mẹ́ta ni mo ní" means ___ || I have three || I am three years old || I saw three || I want three
"Ìwà lẹ́wàá" teaches that ___ || good character is the true beauty || a fine face is everything || money is the greatest wealth || speed wins the race
An orin ìkìlọ̀ is a song that ___ || warns children away from bad things || welcomes a newborn baby || praises a chief at his gate || counts the days of the week
The Yoruba alphabet has letters ___ || twenty-five in all: 18 consonants and 7 vowels || twenty-six in all: 20 consonants and 6 vowels || twenty-four in all: 17 consonants and 7 vowels || thirty letters in all
The seven vowels of Yoruba are ___ || a, e, ẹ, i, o, ọ, u || a, e, i, o, u, w, y || a, b, c, d, e, f, g || e, ẹ, i, o, ọ, u, n
Which letter is NOT in the Yoruba alphabet? || c || b || d || ń
The two letters that are written as pairs are ___ || gb and ṣ || kp and q || sh and th || ch and ph
"Ọjọ́ Àìkú" is the day we call ___ || Sunday || Friday || Wednesday || Saturday
Tuesday in Yoruba is ___ || Ọjọ́ Ìṣẹ́gun || Ọjọ́ Bọ̀ṣẹ̀ || Ọjọ́ Ẹtì || Ọjọ́ Ajé
The seven days of the week end with ___ || Ọjọ́ Àbámẹ́ta || Ọjọ́-rúkú-ọ̀jọ́ || Ọjọ́ Àìkú || Ọjọ́ Ìṣẹ́gun
The first eight letters of the Yoruba alphabet, in order, are ___ || a, b, d, e, ẹ, f, g, gb || a, b, c, d, e, f, g, h || a, e, ẹ, i, o, ọ, u, b || b, d, f, g, gb, h, j, k
Action words: "jẹ" means ___ || eat || drink || sleep || come
Do not mix them up: "mu" is used for ___ || drink || food || book || water only
"Kí ni o ń ṣe?" is asked when we want to know || what somebody is doing || where somebody lives || how old somebody is || who somebody greeted
The sentence pattern we learnt is ___ || Mo + action + thing || Thing + action + Mo || Action + Mo + thing || Mo + thing + action
"Ọ́ nàṣẹ́" is a greeting for a person who is ___ || at work || at rest || at school || just born
The Yoruba word for a farmer is ___ || agbẹ̀ || ọ̀dẹ̀ || akọ́ || alagbe
