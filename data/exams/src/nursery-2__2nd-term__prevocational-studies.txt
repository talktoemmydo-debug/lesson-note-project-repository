# Prevocational Studies (PVS) — Nursery 2 · Second Term · term paper
subject: Prevocational Studies (PVS)
class: Nursery 2
term: 2nd Term

[objective]
A farmer is a person who ___ || grows crops or keeps animals for food || sells cloth inside the market || drives a big bus to the town || mends our shoes at the roadside
The farmer who keeps birds for eggs and meat is a ___ || poultry farmer || fish farmer || tree farmer || miner
Work that begins a new farm, before any seed is planted, is ___ || clearing the land || harvesting the crop || selling the produce || drying the grain
After planting, the farmer removes the ___ || weeds || stones from the market || birds from the sky || roof from the shed
The last work in growing a crop, before the money comes, is ___ || harvesting and selling || watering the weeds || burning the fence || plucking the roof
A fish farmer feeds his fish at set times and keeps the ___ || water clean and moving || birds away from the roof || fire in the shed || tools in the sun
Why does a farmer begin his work very early in the morning? || because the body is cooler and weeds are soft || because the market is shut down later || because the sun is brighter at noon || because the tools are heavier in rain
A farm visit is when the class goes to the farm to ___ || see with the eyes what the class was told || buy plenty of food for the house || have a long sleep on the grass || play football with the bigger boys
Before we start a farm visit we should ___ || shut our shoes and fill the water flask || leave our books on the ground || carry our lunch in an open bag || run to the gate first
On the farm we must not ___ || pick the fruit or chase the birds away || look at the plants in the rows || ask the farmer any good question || wash our hands when we come back
An animal that sits apart from the others in the house is probably ___ || sick || happy || the newest || sleeping well
A good question to ask the farmer on a visit is ___ || when did you plant this crop? || what is your father's name? || where do you sleep? || how old is your gate?
A farm tool is a thing we use to ___ || make farm work easier || cook the family food || sew the school uniform || wash our plates
The tool with a broad blade used for tilling and making ridges is the ___ || hoe || rake || basket || watering can
A cutlass is used mainly for ___ || clearing the grass and small sticks || watering the young seedlings daily || carrying the harvest to the market || sowing the small seeds in rows
A tool with teeth for gathering leaves and leveling a seedbed is the ___ || rake || cutlass || spade || basket
A watering can has a spout with small holes called a ___ || rose || leaf || root || handle
The tool with a flat stiff blade that is pushed with the foot into the ground is the ___ || spade || shovel || basket || sickle
Which tool is better for lifting and moving loose sand? || a shovel || a rake || a watering can || a hoe
A wheelbarrow carries a big load because it has a ___ || wheel, a tray and two handles || very long rope for pulling it || sharp edge under the front || net and a heavy wooden lid
In loading a barrow, the heavy things are put ___ || near the wheel and over it || at the back by the handles || on top of everything || under the wheel
A wheelbarrow must never be used for carrying ___ || people or children || sand or soil || a bag of manure || the harvest
A woven carrier used for the harvest and put on the head is a ___ || basket || shovel || watering can || sprayer
Before we swing a cutlass we must first ___ || clear the people and the stones away || water the grass around it || count the leaves on the bush || hide our tools
A tool is kept after work by ___ || cleaning off the mud, drying and hanging it || leaving it there on the wet ground || burying it deep in the planting bed || pouring cold water on the blade
The tool used to fan chaff from grain is the ___ || winnowing basket || watering can || cutlass || spade
A knapsack sprayer differs from a watering can because it sends a ___ || fine mist of medicine on to the leaf || big stream of water on the root || heavy heap of farm manure || whole load of clean soil
A sickle is a curved one-hand blade used for ___ || reaping grass and maize || digging a big pit || carrying water || tying the yam
The farmers' saying about the two tools that look alike is: a spade digs, a shovel ___ || moves || sleeps || sings || dries
Tools must not be left where a child can step on them, so they are ___ || racked or hung with the edge covered || thrown down inside the gutter || hidden under the young crop || stood up out in the hot sun

[subobjective]
Name the four kinds of farmer we learnt and what each keeps or grows. :: crop farmer | poultry keeper | fish farmer | tree crop farmer
Mention five works of a crop farmer in order. :: clearing | tilling | planting | weeding | harvesting
Name four things to look for on a crop farm during a visit. :: plant height | soil | rows | weeds | holes in leaves
Draw and name the parts of a tool: the working metal and the part in the hand. :: blade | handle
Mention the parts of a watering can. :: body | handle | spout | rose
Name four tools used for carrying on the farm. :: wheelbarrow | basket | head pan | sack
Sort these into digging and clearing: hoe, cutlass, spade, rake. :: digging: hoe, spade | clearing: cutlass, rake
Mention the parts of a wheelbarrow. :: wheel | tyre | frame | tray | handles | legs
Name two pairs of tools that look alike and the one difference in each. :: spade/shovel — digging/moving | hoe/spade — pulling/pushing
Give four rules for storing farm tools well. :: clean off mud | dry them | cover the edge | keep out of the rain
