[subobjective]
Name the three things that kept changing as computers grew older to newer. :: smaller size | faster work | cheaper cost
Mention the four parts found inside a computer box. :: CPU | RAM | hard drive | motherboard
Sort these into input and output: keyboard, monitor, mouse, printer, microphone, speakers. :: input: keyboard, mouse, microphone | output: monitor, printer, speakers
Mention four things we can do with the mouse. :: click | double-click | right-click | scroll | drag and drop
Name four peripherals that can be joined to a computer. :: printer | scanner | flash drive | webcam | speakers
Mention four rules of the computer lab. :: walk, do not run | no food or drink | clean dry hands | switch off after use
Name the parts of a printer we work with. :: paper tray | ink or toner cartridge | cover | output slot
Mention three ways a monitor can be spoiled. :: water sprayed on it | knocking or pressing the glass | leaving it in bright sun
Give three rules for caring for headphones. :: do not share them | do not wind the cable tight | keep the holes clean
Mention four things we must do before printing. :: look at the preview | put paper in flat | check the ink | switch it on
