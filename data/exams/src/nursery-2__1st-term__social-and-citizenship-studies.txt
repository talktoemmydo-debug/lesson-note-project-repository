# Social and Citizenship Studies — Nursery 2 · First Term · term paper
subject: Social and Citizenship Studies
class: Nursery 2
term: 1st Term

[objective]
The leader of my class in school is the ___ || teacher, with the class captain || the watchman at the gate || the cleaner of the corridor || a visitor from town
The leader of a street or community is called the ___ || baale || prefect || monitor || pupil
The leader of a state is the ___ || governor || chairman || king only || mayor of another town
We need leaders so that people will be ___ || guided, with order kept and work finished || left alone, with nothing at all to say || paid money on every market day || sent away to another town
The leaders of my home are ___ || father and mother || the neighbours only || the traders at the gate || the pupils of the class
A duty of a parent is to give food, clothes, education, love and ___ || protection || punishment only || money for games || a motor car
When father and mother are away, the smaller children are led by ___ || an elder brother or sister || the family dog || the neighbour's child || no one at all
Respect at home begins with small things: answering "yes ma/sir", carrying a bag and ___ || not slamming doors || dropping plates loudly || running upstairs || leaving shoes in the passage
A community is people living together in ___ || one area — a street, a village or a town || one house with many storeys only || one class in the school building || one market stall on a table
The name of the place I live is said like this: ___ || "I live in ______" || "I sleep in ______" || "I eat at ______" || "I run to ______"
An example of community mobilization is ___ || the clean-up exercise on the last Saturday of the month || the long sleep taken in the afternoon sun || the heavy rain that falls in the month of June || the closing of the market at high noon
The leaders who meet to plan for the people include the chief, the imam, the pastor and the ___ || chairman of the local government area || driver of the bus at the park || trader that sells us food || pupil of the school near the market
When a neighbour's house falls down, the community brings ___ || sand, blocks and hands || a new television set || music and dancing shoes || a full pot of soup
A strong community gives its people ___ || safety, unity and progress || noise, dust and fear || homework and tests || hunger and thirst
A child's part in the community includes ___ || not littering and keeping the gutters free || throwing papers on the wide road || blocking the gutter with soft mud || frightening the old people at the gate
A responsibility is a thing we ___ || must do, and not a thing we may choose to do || may forget when we are very busy || can buy for ourselves at the market || give to another child in the class
If I lose or spoil a borrowed book, the consequence is that ___ || it must be replaced or paid for || nothing at all happens || the book returns itself || the owner buys two
Respect for elders is shown by greeting them, listening, obeying and ___ || using their respectful title, such as ma or sir || calling them by a nick name at the gate || answering them back with a loud voice || walking away without saying anything
Polite words we use are please, thank you, sorry and ___ || excuse me || over to you || mind the gap || good night
Nigeria's neighbours include Benin Republic, Niger, Chad and ___ || Cameroon || Egypt || Ghana || Kenya
The capital of Nigeria is ___ || Abuja || Lagos || Kano || Ibadan
The white on the Nigerian flag stands for ___ || peace and unity || water || milk || morning
Nigeria became independent on the ___ || 1st of October 1960 || 29th of May 1999 || 1st of January 1960 || 27th of October 1958
The flag is flown at half a mast when ___ || a great person dies || the market is closed || school begins in January || it is raining hard
The national pledge is said at school to ___ || be faithful to the country and to honour Nigeria || greet the visitors that come to the school || sing before the match on Friday || open the market for the traders
The Y on the shield stands for ___ || the rivers Niger and Benue || the shape of the flag || the two horses || the capital city
Our money is the Naira; its small part is the ___ || kobo, and one hundred kobo make a Naira || pesewa, and ten pesewa make a cedi || sen, and one hundred sen make a naira || bit, and one thousand bits make a naira
A sacred place like the church or the mosque must be met with ___ || quiet behaviour and proper dress || loud talking and running || food and drink in the hall || shoes on the altar table
Objects we respect include the flag, religious books, the elders' belongings and ___ || national monuments || the refuse gutter || other people's slippers || the broken chair
Writing my name on a monument is wrong because it ___ || damages a thing that belongs to the whole country || will cost me some of my money for the paint || takes a very long time in the afternoon || makes the old wall look very colourful

[subobjective]

State four qualities of a good leader. :: kind | fair | honest | helpful | goes first in what is right
Give four duties of a parent in the home. :: food | shelter | clothes | education | love and protection
Name the four things that make up the community around me. :: neighbours | shopkeepers | drivers | teachers | nurses | farmers | worshippers
Mention four community activities that need mobilization. :: clean-up exercise | festivals | town hall meeting | building a bridge or a borehole | protecting children
State four responsibilities of a pupil at school. :: come on time | do homework | keep the classroom clean | bring books | respect the bell
Give three ways we respect elders and three ways we respect younger ones. :: greet, listen, obey, use titles | do not push them, help them when they fall, no bullying
Name the colours of the flag and the meaning of each. :: green for agriculture and forests | white for peace and unity
Mention four things that identify Nigeria as my country. :: flag | anthem | coat of arms | Naira | independence day | English as a link language
State four objects or places that must be respected. :: the flag | Bible and Qur'an | church and mosque | school, hospital, market | monuments
