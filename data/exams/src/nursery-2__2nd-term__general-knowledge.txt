[objective]
The helper who treats people when they are sick is the ___ || doctor || tailor || farmer || driver
A nurse works mainly at the ___ || hospital or clinic || farm || market || filling station
The helper who sews our school cloth is the ___ || tailor || mason || barber || pilot
Where does a police officer work? He works at the ___ || police station || poultry house || school kitchen || motor park
The farmer is important to the town because he ___ || grows the food we eat || sews our uniforms || drives the bus || keeps the gate
Our money in Nigeria is called the ___ || Naira || cedi || franc || shilling
The sign written for the Naira is ___ || ₦ || $ || £ || ¢
The colours of the Nigerian flag are ___ || green, white and green || red, white and black || green and gold || white and blue only
The white colour on our flag stands for ___ || peace || water || farms || market
The green colour on our flag is for our ___ || farms and land || cars and roads || planes and birds || books and pens
Our capital city is ___ || Abuja || Lagos || Ibadan || Sokoto
On a simple map, our country Nigeria is found in ___ Africa. || West || East || North only || South only
The eagle is important to us because it is our ___ || national bird || farm animal || pet in the house || fish of the river
The animal called the king of animals is the ___ || lion || goat || rabbit || dog
The home of a dog is called a ___ || kennel || coop || hutch || stable
A chicken is kept in a ___ || coop or a poultry house || sty or a pig hole || stable for a horse || nest on a tall tree
The language used for our lessons in school is ___ || English || Arabic only || French || Latin
A day with clouds and water falling from the sky is a ___ || rainy day || sunny day || dusty day || windy day
The harmattan season in Nigeria brings air that is ___ || dry, dusty and cool || wet and muddy || hot and rainy || dark all day
On a rainy morning the thing we carry to keep dry is an ___ || umbrella || fan || broom || plate
The part of a plant that drinks water from the soil is the ___ || root || flower || fruit || leaf
A flower on a plant later becomes the ___ || fruit || stone || branch || shadow
The things a plant gives us for our home are ___ || food, shade and fresh air || only fire and ash || books and pens || iron and tin
Which of these is a vegetable and not a fruit? || ugu || mango || banana || orange
We hold a grown-up's ___ when we cross a busy road. || hand || finger only || bag || shoe
Before crossing the road a child should first ___ || stop and look left, right, then left || run across as fast as possible || close both eyes and then jump || shout at all of the cars
The sense we use for smelling food is the ___ || nose || ear || skin || tongue
A dangerous thing at home that must never be touched is ___ || a bottle of medicine on the shelf || a folded towel on the bed || a wooden spoon for the pot || a school shoe by the door
The tortoise in our folktales is known for being clever and ___ || greedy || fast always || strong like a horse || big as an elephant
In the story of the wisdom gourd, the tortoise learnt that wisdom must be ___ || shared with everybody || hidden in a tree || sold at the market || buried in the farm
