[objective]

"Ìyan" is food made from ___ || pounded yam || roasted maize || fried beans || boiled rice

"Ọmọlúàbí" means a child who is ___ || well brought up || very rich || always last || never taught

The opposite of "ńlá" (big) in Yoruba is ___ || kékeré || gígùn || tútù || òkè
