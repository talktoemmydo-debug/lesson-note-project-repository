# Basic Digital Literacy — Nursery 2 · First Term · term paper
subject: Basic Digital Literacy
class: Nursery 2
term: 1st Term

[objective]
A digital device is a machine that works with ___ || electricity and shows things on a screen || fire and shows things on a wall || water and keeps things in a pot || rope and pulls things up a hill
This one is NOT a digital device ___ || a wooden broom || a tablet || a smart watch || a camera
The device used for taking pictures is a ___ || camera || kettle || fan || broom
A tablet is different from a desktop because it is ___ || flat, light and touched with the finger || big, heavy and fixed on a table || sold only inside a bank || made of wood and of cloth
A computer is big and has a screen, keys and a ___ || mouse || spoon || plate || broom
A machine that opens and shuts like a book, with a battery, is a ___ || laptop || desktop || typewriter || television
The smallest and lightest computer to carry is the ___ || tablet || desktop || printer || CPU box
The part that shows pictures, letters and numbers is the ___ || monitor || keyboard || mouse || speaker
We press keys to put information into the computer with the ___ || keyboard || monitor || printer || CPU
The part held in the hand that moves the arrow on the screen is the ___ || mouse || keyboard || CPU box || speaker
The machine that puts the picture or letter out on paper is a ___ || printer || scanner || mouse || monitor
A computer in the bank is used to ___ || count money and print a receipt || cook food for the workers || water the plants outside || sweep the floor of the hall
The price of goods in a shop is read by a ___ || machine that reads the price || pencil and paper only || radio || fan
Time on a device should be shared between learning and ___ || play, with a limit on play || sleeping, with no limit || fighting, with a limit || cooking, with no limit
Water inside a device ___ || spoils it for good || makes it faster || cleans the screen || charges the battery
A laptop should be carried ___ || closed in two hands inside a padded bag || open in only one small hand || by its long wire, dragged along || with the screen facing downward
The cable is taken out of the socket by holding ___ || the plug itself || the wire, pulled hard || the screen || the chair
Before we switch off, we save the work and ___ || shut the programme, then kill the power || pull the cable out in a hurry || take the keyboard right away || shake the monitor once or twice
The room in the school where many computers are kept is the ___ || computer lab || computer kitchen || computer store || computer chapel
In the lab we move about by ___ || walking carefully || running fast || sliding on the floor || climbing the desks
If a machine gives smoke or a bad smell, we ___ || leave it and tell the teacher at once || pour cold water on the machine || press every key on the keyboard || hide it under the desk
Long ago, people counted with stones and with a frame of sliding beads called a(n) ___ || abacus || laptop || monitor || printer
The first machines used parts that glowed and grew hot called ___ || vacuum tubes || microchips || tablets || printers
The tiny part that replaced the hot vacuum tube was the ___ || transistor || abacus bead || candle || keyboard
Computers became ___ || smaller, faster and cheaper || bigger, slower and dearer || smaller, slower and dearer || bigger, faster and cheaper
The machine built by Babbage to work out tables of numbers without mistakes was the ___ || Difference Engine || Printing press || Steam engine || Spinning jenny
Babbage's plans could not be built fully because the ___ || workshop of his day could not make such parts || the money he had for the metal parts || the plans he drew on his paper only || the numbers he wrote in his log book
A set of written instructions for a machine is called a ___ || programme || pillow || parcel || poster
A computer language was named after ___ || Ada Lovelace || the monitor || Babbage's town || the abacus
"A device is a servant, not a ___ || master || toy || book || chair"

[subobjective]
Mention five digital devices you know. :: computer | tablet | smartphone | smart watch | camera
State three things a smartphone does. :: calls | photographs | plays music
Name the four basic parts of a computer. :: monitor | keyboard | mouse | CPU
Say what each of these is used for: monitor and CPU. :: the | monitor | shows | work; | the | CPU | does | the | thinking
Mention three places where a computer is used. :: bank | hospital | school | shop | home
Give four rules for caring for a device. :: keep food and drink away | wipe with a soft dry cloth | carry with two hands | do not drop or hit it
State three safety rules of the computer lab. :: walk, do not run | no food or drink on the desk | do not touch cables or sockets
Name the three kinds of computer we learnt. :: desktop | laptop | tablet
Mention three uses of the abacus or old counting things. :: counting in markets | adding beads up | taking away beads down | school counting
Say two things about Babbage and Ada Lovelace. :: Babbage designed the Analytical Engine and the Difference Engine | Ada wrote the first programme
