# Basic Science — Nursery 2 · Third Term · term paper
subject: Basic Science
class: Nursery 2
term: 3rd Term

[objective]
Water keeps best in a container that is ___ || clean and covered || bright in colour || made of paper || left in the sun
Which of these is used to store water at home? || a jerry can || a fishing net || a writing slate || a broom
A lid on a water bucket keeps out ___ || dirt and insects || the cold wind || the smell of smoke || the light of day
Before water is put into a container, the container is ___ || washed out with clean water || rubbed with dry sand || left where it is || warmed in the sun
Water kept in an open bucket becomes dirty because ___ || dust and insects fall in || the bucket is too heavy || the stand is too high || the water is too cold
A sickness that comes to us from dirty water is called ___ || a water-borne disease || a bone disease || an ear disease || a skin disease
Which of these comes from dirty water? || cholera || ring worm || measles || tooth decay
Passing loose water many times in one day is ___ || diarrhoea || cough || rash || sneezing
To make doubtful water safe for drinking we can ___ || boil it || keep it in the shade || add palm oil to it || leave it open
After using the toilet, a child must wash the ___ || hands with soap || ears with sand || feet with chalk || bag in the sun
Keeping people and property safe is called ___ || security || cooking || farming || sleeping
Which gadget makes a loud sound when somebody forces a door? || a burglar alarm || a water tank || a dustbin || a hand broom
A CCTV camera is put up to ___ || record what happens || pound the yam || sweep the gate || cool the room
The gadget that warns a house when there is smoke is a ___ || smoke alarm || door bell || hand fan || cutlass
When an alarm sounds at night, we should ___ || leave the house at once || hide under the bed quietly || run back inside for our toys || open the door and look out well
The home of a chicken is called a ___ || coop || den || hive || burrow
A spider lives in a ___ || web || nest || stable || kennel
The home of a cow is a ___ || shed || coop || hive || web
Which of these animals lives in an anthill? || the ant || the fish || the goat || the hen
The young one of a goat is called a ___ || kid || lamb || cub || foal
A puppy is the young one of a ___ || dog || hen || cow || bee
The young one of a butterfly is called a ___ || caterpillar || tadpole || chick || duckling
Which of these animals hatches from an egg? || the duck || the goat || the horse || the dog
A machine is something that makes work ___ || easier || longer || wetter || darker
Which of these is used for sweeping the floor? || a broom || a spoon || a plate || a cup
A hammer is used to ___ || knock in a nail || cut the cloth || drink the water || iron the shirt
In the class, two sheets of paper are joined with a ___ || stapler || duster || ruler || bell
Which machine makes a round hole in a page? || a hole puncher || an eraser || a brush || a stick of chalk
Everything around me that I can see, hear and smell is my ___ || environment || exercise book || lunch box || school bell
Which sense tells me that the water from the tap is cold? || touching || seeing || hearing || smelling
