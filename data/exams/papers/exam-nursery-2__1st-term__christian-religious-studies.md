**Time:** 1 hour · **Total: 50 marks**

## Section B — Sub-objective (10 marks)
Answer briefly.

31. Name the six days of creation in order.
32. Say what God made on Day 3 and Day 4.
33. Give three reasons why God rested on the seventh day.
34. Mention four gifts of God listed in our lesson.
35. State three ways we thank God for His gifts.
36. Name three things we must NOT do to God's gifts.
37. Who are the four people in the story of the birth of Jesus?
38. Mention the three gifts of the wise men.
39. Give two reasons why we celebrate Christmas.
40. State two things that happened after Jesus died.

## Section C — Theory (10 marks)
Answer in full sentences.

41. Write on the work of creation in six days (2 marks)
42. Why do we call God the Creator? Give three reasons (2 marks)
43. Explain how we show thankfulness for the gifts of God (2 marks)
44. Tell the story of the birth of Jesus in four sentences (2 marks)
45. Why is Jesus called the greatest gift of God? Write two reasons (2 marks)
