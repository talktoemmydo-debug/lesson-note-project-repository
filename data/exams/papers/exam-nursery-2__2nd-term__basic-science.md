# EXAMINATION PRACTICE — BASIC SCIENCE (Nursery 2 · 2nd Term)

**Time:** 1 hour · **Total: 50 marks**

## Section A — Objective (30 marks)
Choose the correct option and ring the letter.

1. Food is anything we eat or drink that gives the body ___
   A) work for our hands only
   B) a bad smell and colour
   C) energy and help to grow
   D) only a full belly and nothing else
2. The meal we take in the morning before coming to school is ___
   A) lunch
   B) breakfast
   C) dinner
   D) supper
3. The meal we take in the middle of the day is called ___
   A) supper
   B) breakfast
   C) tea
   D) lunch
4. Yam, rice, beans and plantain are foods that come from ___
   A) plants
   B) stones
   C) water only
   D) animals
5. Eggs, milk, meat and fish are foods that come from ___
   A) plants
   B) trees
   C) animals
   D) rivers only
6. Living things are things that ___
   A) never move or feed
   B) have life in them
   C) are made in a factory
   D) cannot grow at all
7. Which of these is a non-living thing?
   A) a mango tree
   B) a catfish
   C) a snail
   D) a piece of chalk
8. Which is NOT one of the signs of life?
   A) feeding and breathing
   B) having young ones
   C) shining brightly in the dark
   D) moving and growing
9. A fish breathes inside water with its ___
   A) ears
   B) gills
   C) nose
   D) scales
10. A snail goes back into its shell when you touch it. This shows that living things ___
   A) answer what happens around them
   B) never move at all by themselves
   C) do not need any air at all
   D) make their own houses of clay
11. The young one of a dog is called a ___
   A) kitten
   B) calf
   C) puppy
   D) chick
12. A frog begins its life in water as a ___
   A) chrysalis
   B) tadpole
   C) caterpillar
   D) puppy
13. Between the caterpillar and the butterfly comes the stage called the ___
   A) chrysalis
   B) tadpole
   C) young fowl
   D) seedling
14. A baby animal that takes milk from its mother is called a ___
   A) fish
   B) bird
   C) reptile
   D) mammal
15. The first growth of a seed, when the root comes out, is called ___
   A) pollination
   B) germination
   C) boiling
   D) harvesting
16. The part of a young plant that grows down into the soil is the ___
   A) fruit
   B) leaf
   C) root
   D) flower
17. Which of these is NOT needed for a plant to grow?
   A) air
   B) space to spread
   C) sunlight
   D) a basket of sweets
18. Water that is clean and safe for drinking is ___
   A) clear and without bad smell
   B) full of floating dirt
   C) warm like soup
   D) thick with red colour
19. A canoe and a ferry are means of transport on ___
   A) sand
   B) road
   C) water
   D) rail
20. The thing that keeps a passenger safe inside a moving car is the ___
   A) big shoe
   B) seat belt
   C) school bag
   D) sun cap
21. Our bones give the body ___
   A) shape, support and protection
   B) colour and smell
   C) nothing at all at all
   D) only a bad hardness
22. The bone of the head that protects the brain is called the ___
   A) spine
   B) kneecap
   C) rib
   D) skull
23. The bones of the chest that keep the heart and the lungs safe are the ___
   A) skulls
   B) ribs
   C) fingers
   D) ankles
24. The bone of the back that lets us bend and keeps us upright is the ___
   A) spine
   B) skull
   C) wrist
   D) elbow
25. To keep our bones strong we should take ___
   A) bread with no butter
   B) sweets and chalk
   C) only cold water
   D) milk, eggs, fish and beans
26. Technology means the ___
   A) songs that we sing in the class
   B) stories that the elders tell at night
   C) tools and machines people make to work
   D) things that only grow on the farm
27. A mobile phone is useful in an emergency because it can ___
   A) carry a load to the market
   B) call for help for a sick person
   C) cook food for the family
   D) keep our bones straight
28. Making water that is dirty safe for drinking is called ___
   A) pollution
   B) germination
   C) transportation
   D) purification
29. Boiling water is done mainly to ___
   A) kill the germs inside it
   B) make the water heavier
   C) cool the kettle down
   D) add more dirt to it
30. After water has been boiled it should be ___
   A) drunk while it is still hot
   B) kept open in the sun
   C) left to cool in a clean covered cup
   D) poured back in the bucket

## Section B — Sub-objective (10 marks)
Answer briefly.

31. Give three reasons why we eat food.
32. Name the three main meals of the day.
33. Mention four foods that come from plants and two that come from animals.
34. Name the seven signs of life in living things.
35. Mention four things animals need in order to grow well.
36. Write the stages in the life of a hen in order.
37. Write the stages of a plant's life in order.
38. Name the parts of the body described: head bone, chest bones, back bone.
39. Mention four ways of making water safe to drink.
40. Name the sources of water you know.

## Section C — Theory (10 marks)
Answer in full sentences.

41. What is food? Give four reasons why living things need food. (2 marks)
42. Explain the difference between living things and non-living things, and give three examples of each. (2 marks)
43. Describe how an animal grows from its young one to adult, using any two animals you know. (2 marks)
44. What is transportation? Name means of transport on land, on water and in the air, and two rules for travelling safely. (2 marks)
45. Why must dirty water be made clean before we drink it? Explain the four ways of doing it. (2 marks)
