# EXAMINATION PRACTICE — GENERAL KNOWLEDGE ( · )

**Time:** 1 hour · **Total: 50 marks**

## Section A — Objective (30 marks)
Choose the correct option and ring the letter.

1. When somebody asks who you are, the first thing you say is your ___
   A) shoe size
   B) class only
   C) name
   D) father's work
2. We see the pictures on the board with our ___
   A) eyes
   B) ears
   C) elbows
   D) toenails
3. We hear the bell of the school with our ___
   A) nose
   B) hair
   C) fingers
   D) ears
4. If you are four years old you say: I am ___
   A) one year old
   B) ten years old
   C) four years old
   D) forty years old
5. Your father's father is your ___
   A) cousin
   B) grandpa
   C) nephew
   D) brother
6. When a teacher gives you a book you say ___
   A) thank you
   B) sorry only
   C) go away
   D) excuse me
7. In my family, the work of the house is done by ___
   A) a visitor who comes from the town
   B) the father alone in the house
   C) only the smallest child in the house
   D) everybody helping one another
8. Where can we borrow and read story books in school?
   A) the toilet
   B) the playground
   C) the library
   D) the kitchen
9. The head of the school is the ___
   A) school nurse
   B) head teacher

   D) pupil in Jss 1

   A) a cooking pot
   B) a pencil

   A) run to the board
   B) shout the answer

   A) bag of a friend
   B) cupboard shelf

   A) kitchen
   B) bedroom

   A) kitchen

   A) near
   B) tall

   A) red, blue and yellow
   B) green, purple and pink

17. The shape of a plate or a ball is a ___
   A) square

   A) circle
   B) triangle

   D) line

   A) green and white
   B) red and yellow

   A) goat

   C) dog
   D) cow

   A) moo
