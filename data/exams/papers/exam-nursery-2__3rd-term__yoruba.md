# EXAMINATION PRACTICE — YORUBA (Nursery 2 · 3rd Term)

**Time:** 1 hour · **Total: 54 marks**

## Section A — Objective (30 marks)
Choose the correct option and ring the letter.

1. "Ẹ jó!" tells the class to ____
   A) sit down
   B) begin reading
   C) stand up
   D) wash the hand
2. To make a command polite we put in the word ____
   A) ṣé
   B) jọ̀wọ́
   C) àáṣẹ̀
   D) ṣeé
3. "Ẹ káàlẹ́" is the greeting said in ____
   A) the morning
   B) the class
   C) the evening
   D) the market
4. When an elder is greeted, a girl ____ and a boy lies full length.
   A) kneels
   B) jumps
   C) runs
   D) shouts
5. "à-kà-rá" said together gives the word ____
   A) kàrà
   B) àrá
   C) àkà
   D) àkàrà
6. How many sounds is the word "bàtà" made of? ____
   A) one
   B) two
   C) four
   D) six
7. The fruit of the palm that is pressed for red oil is ____
   A) ọ̀pẹ̀
   B) áńbà
   C) àmùtì
   D) bílíbilí
8. An àlọ́ àpamọ́ is the kind of story that ____
   A) ends in a lie
   B) counts the days
   C) is only a song
   D) hides a lesson
9. The call "Àlọ́ o!" takes the reply ____
   A) À ń ṣé!
   B) Àpamọ́!
   C) Ẹ ṣé gan!
   D) Báwo ni o!
10. When a child wants to reply to the teacher he says ____
   A) Ó dára
   B) Moò ṣé
   C) Mi ò
   D) Lọ kúrò
11. If a word is too fast for us we say "Ẹ jọ̀wọ́, ____"
   A) lọ kúrò
   B) wò ó
   C) fi sí i
   D) tún un sọ
12. The body is washed at òwúrọ̀ and again ____
   A) in the evening
   B) on Monday
   C) twice a year
   D) in a month
13. Hands wash with soap before food and after ____
   A) the song
   B) the toilet
   C) the drum
   D) the market gate
14. Twenty in Yoruba is ____
   A) àrún
   B) ogótà
   C) ogún
   D) ẹ́wàá
15. "Ogún àti ẹ̀ta" is the number ____
   A) 32
   B) 40
   C) 13
   D) 23
16. Forty is said in Yoruba as ____
   A) ogótà
   B) ogún mẹ́rin
   C) ọ̀rúnmọ́lẹ́dógún
   D) ọ̀ọ́dúnrún
17. Ogún àti ọ̀kan plus ọ̀kan is ____
   A) ogún àti ọ̀kan
   B) ogún nìkan
   C) ogún àti èjì
   D) ogún àti ẹ̀ta
18. The day of thanks and rest is called ____
   A) Ọjọ́rú
   B) Ọjọ́ Àìkú
   C) Ọjọ́ Ayé
   D) Ọjọ́bọ̀
19. On Ọjọ́ Àbámẹ́ta the children ____
   A) sleep all day
   B) work on the farm
   C) count the pots
   D) sweep and play
20. "Àwọ̀ ewé" is the colour of ____
   A) a stone
   B) new milk
   C) a leaf
   D) the sky
21. The Yoruba for red is ____
   A) púpà
   B) funfun
   C) àwọ̀ ilẹ̀
   D) dúdú
22. "Iṣẹ́ ni òògùn ìẹ́" says the medicine for shame is ____
   A) play
   B) work
   C) sleep
   D) wealth
23. The elder sibling, who leads by the hand, is the ____
   A) ọmọ ìyá
   B) àbúrò
   C) ẹ̀gbọ́n
   D) àgbàlagbà
24. Before she takes a thing, a younger child always ____
   A) runs off
   B) hides it
   C) laughs loud
   D) asks first
25. "Kí ni n ń fi ọ̀lẹ̀ ṣe?" is the question inside a ____
   A) market call
   B) character song
   C) number card
   D) lullaby
26. Before the pot goes on the fire the wet flour is pressed so that the ____ goes out.
   A) water
   B) oil
   C) sand
   D) smoke
27. "Fa kọ́kọ́ yọ" in the akọ̀sọ̀rọ̀ drill means to ____
   A) sweep the yard
   B) fetch the water
   C) eat the food
   D) pull out the knot
28. "Ahọ́n" is the ____
   A) neck
   B) nose
   C) tongue
   D) ear
29. The beaded crown worn by the Òba is his ____
   A) àdé
   B) ẹ̀kẹ́
   C) ìka
   D) asọ̀
30. Over a village, under the Òba, is the ____
   A) Aláàfin
   B) Baale
   C) Ọọni
   D) Olúbadan

## Section B — Sub-objective (10 marks)
Answer briefly.

31. Say four actions the teacher names in Yoruba
32. Name three greetings and the time each one belongs to
33. Join the sounds and write the words
34. Mention four fruits of our land
35. Say the two kinds of àlọ́ and what each one does
36. Write the numbers 21 to 24 in Yoruba
37. Mention six colours in Yoruba
38. Say two duties of ẹ̀gbọ́n and two of àbúrò
39. Name the work on ọ̀gẹ̀ in order, four steps
40. Mention five parts of the body in Yoruba

## Section C — Theory (10 marks)
Answer in full sentences.

41. Write two sentences on how a play song is sung and what the class does when it stops. (2 marks)
42. Join the sounds and write the words: à-kà-rá, bà-tà, ọ-kọ́. (3 marks)
43. Say what "Iṣẹ́ ni òògùn ìẹ́" means and write one thing you do at home that proves it. (4 marks)
44. Write two sentences on the duties of ẹ̀gbọ́n and àbúrò in one house. (3 marks)
45. Describe the crown of the Òba and say how a man and a woman greet him. (2 marks)
