# EXAMINATION PRACTICE — SOCIAL AND CITIZENSHIP STUDIES (Primary 1 · 1st Term)

**Time:** 1 hour · **Total: 50 marks**

## Section A — Objective (30 marks)
Choose the correct option and ring the letter.

1. Who I am is my ___
   A) identity
   B) hunger
   C) noise
   D) dust
2. I am a ___ or a girl
   A) tree
   B) stone
   C) boy
   D) car

   B) storm
   C) sky
   D) community

   A) Nigerian
   B) stone
   C) fish only
   D) bird

   A) lost
   B) related
   C) far
   D) angry

   A) tools
   B) strangers
   C) parents
   D) pets
7. My brothers and sisters are my ___
   A) siblings
   B) shoes
   C) books
   D) roads
8. A big family has parents, children, uncles and ___
   A) trees
   B) aunts
   C) stones
   D) cars
9. A community is a place where people ___
   A) fight
   B) sleep only
   C) live together
   D) hide

   A) stranger
   B) tool
   C) pet
   D) neighbour

   A) leader
   B) tree
   C) stone
   D) cloud

   A) hunger

   C) dust
   D) noise

   A) weather

   D) culture

   D) storm

   A) noise

   D) anger

   A) hiding

   D) respect

   A) thank you
   B) noise

   D) bad words

   A) stealing
   B) crying
   C) sharing
   D) hiding

   A) anger
