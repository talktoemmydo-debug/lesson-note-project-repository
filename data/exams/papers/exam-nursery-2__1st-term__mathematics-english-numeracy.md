## Section B — Sub-objective (10 marks)
Answer briefly.

31. Write the tens and ones in the number 58.
32. Write 147 in expanded form and say what each digit stands for.
33. Write these in words: 20, 40 and 100.
34. Add: 20 + 30 and 28 + 7.
35. Subtract: 57 − 23 and 42 − 15.
36. Mention two ways to check an addition sum.
37. Say which is greater in each pair: 96 and 102; 189 and 198.

39. Count from 95 to 105 and say which numbers are odd.
40. Say what the answer of an addition is called and what the answer of a subtraction is called.

41. Write the numbers 101 to 120 in figures and any five in words (2 marks)
