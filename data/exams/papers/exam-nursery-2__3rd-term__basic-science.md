# EXAMINATION PRACTICE — BASIC SCIENCE (Nursery 2 · 3rd Term)

**Time:** 1 hour · **Total: 50 marks**

## Section A — Objective (30 marks)
Choose the correct option and ring the letter.

1. Water keeps best in a container that is ___
   A) clean and covered
   B) left in the sun
   C) made of paper
   D) bright in colour
2. Which of these is used to store water at home?
   A) a fishing net
   B) a writing slate
   C) a broom
   D) a jerry can
3. A lid on a water bucket keeps out ___
   A) the cold wind
   B) the light of day
   C) dirt and insects
   D) the smell of smoke
4. Before water is put into a container, the container is ___
   A) left where it is
   B) washed out with clean water
   C) warmed in the sun
   D) rubbed with dry sand
5. Water kept in an open bucket becomes dirty because ___
   A) the stand is too high
   B) the water is too cold
   C) the bucket is too heavy
   D) dust and insects fall in
6. A sickness that comes to us from dirty water is called ___
   A) a water-borne disease
   B) an ear disease
   C) a bone disease
   D) a skin disease
7. Which of these comes from dirty water?
   A) ring worm
   B) cholera
   C) measles
   D) tooth decay
8. Passing loose water many times in one day is ___
   A) cough
   B) sneezing
   C) rash
   D) diarrhoea
9. To make doubtful water safe for drinking we can ___
   A) boil it
   B) leave it open
   C) keep it in the shade
   D) add palm oil to it
10. After using the toilet, a child must wash the ___
   A) ears with sand
   B) bag in the sun
   C) hands with soap
   D) feet with chalk
11. Keeping people and property safe is called ___
   A) sleeping
   B) farming
   C) cooking
   D) security
12. Which gadget makes a loud sound when somebody forces a door?
   A) a dustbin
   B) a burglar alarm
   C) a hand broom
   D) a water tank
13. A CCTV camera is put up to ___
   A) record what happens
   B) cool the room
   C) pound the yam
   D) sweep the gate
14. The gadget that warns a house when there is smoke is a ___
   A) door bell
   B) hand fan
   C) smoke alarm
   D) cutlass
15. When an alarm sounds at night, we should ___
   A) open the door and look out well
   B) leave the house at once
   C) hide under the bed quietly
   D) run back inside for our toys
16. The home of a chicken is called a ___
   A) coop
   B) burrow
   C) den
   D) hive
17. A spider lives in a ___
   A) kennel
   B) stable
   C) nest
   D) web
18. The home of a cow is a ___
   A) hive
   B) web
   C) shed
   D) coop
19. Which of these animals lives in an anthill?
   A) the hen
   B) the ant
   C) the fish
   D) the goat
20. The young one of a goat is called a ___
   A) kid
   B) cub
   C) foal
   D) lamb
21. A puppy is the young one of a ___
   A) bee
   B) cow
   C) dog
   D) hen
22. The young one of a butterfly is called a ___
   A) chick
   B) tadpole
   C) duckling
   D) caterpillar
23. Which of these animals hatches from an egg?
   A) the horse
   B) the duck
   C) the dog
   D) the goat
24. A machine is something that makes work ___
   A) darker
   B) longer
   C) easier
   D) wetter
25. Which of these is used for sweeping the floor?
   A) a broom
   B) a cup
   C) a plate
   D) a spoon
26. A hammer is used to ___
   A) cut the cloth
   B) drink the water
   C) iron the shirt
   D) knock in a nail
27. In the class, two sheets of paper are joined with a ___
   A) bell
   B) duster
   C) stapler
   D) ruler
28. Which machine makes a round hole in a page?
   A) a hole puncher
   B) a brush
   C) an eraser
   D) a stick of chalk
29. Everything around me that I can see, hear and smell is my ___
   A) lunch box

   A) seeing
   B) hearing

## Section C — Theory (10 marks)
Answer in full sentences.

41. What is a water-borne disease? Name four of them and state two ways of keeping it away. (2 marks)
42. Describe how water is kept clean at home. Name the containers that may be used for it. (2 marks)
43. What is security? Name four security gadgets and say the work each one does. (2 marks)
44. Write the home and the young one of each of these animals: the dog, the hen, the cow, the goat and the lion. (2 marks)
