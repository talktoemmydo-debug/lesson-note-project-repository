# EXAMINATION PRACTICE — SOCIAL AND CITIZENSHIP STUDIES (SCS) (Nursery 2 · 2nd Term)

**Time:** 1 hour · **Total: 50 marks**

## Section A — Objective (30 marks)
Choose the correct option and ring the letter.

1. Marriage is the day when a man and a woman are joined as ___
   A) buyer and seller
   B) neighbours only
   C) husband and wife
   D) classmates and friends
2. Marriage is important to us because it is how a ___ begins.
   A) family
   B) school
   C) town
   D) market
3. The marriage done with signed papers before a registrar is a ___
   A) traditional marriage
   B) church marriage
   C) naming rite
   D) court marriage
4. A marriage ceremony done in a mosque or a church is called a ___
   A) court marriage
   B) naming marriage
   C) religious marriage
   D) market marriage
5. The woman on her wedding day is called the ___
   A) aunt
   B) bride
   C) daughter only
   D) groom
6. Two duties that hold a marriage together are love and ___
   A) respect
   B) sleeping
   C) fighting
   D) selling
7. Parenting means ___
   A) building new houses for the rich
   B) selling our goods in the market
   C) feeding, teaching and caring for the children
   D) driving to school every single morning
8. Which of these is NOT a duty of parents?
   A) making sure the child is clothed well
   B) doing the child's own school homework for him
   C) paying the school fees every term
   D) giving the children their daily food
9. A grown-up who keeps a child that is not his own is called a ___
   A) guardian
   B) visitor
   C) stranger
   D) classmate
10. A good parent teaches the child ___
   A) how to run away
   B) how to lie
   C) how to hide things
   D) right from wrong
11. In the family, providing for the home and protecting it is the role of the ___
   A) neighbour
   B) baby
   C) father
   D) driver
12. Keeping the home in order, cooking and caring for the little ones is the role of the ___
   A) mother
   B) watchman
   C) visitor
   D) cousin
13. A child's role in the family includes obeying, helping and ___
   A) spending the money
   B) studying well
   C) closing the gate
   D) selling the food
14. Grandparents help the family by giving advice, stories and ___
   A) punishment
   B) new clothes
   C) school fees always
   D) wisdom
15. Uncles, aunties and cousins who lend a hand are called the ___
   A) age grade
   B) nuclear class
   C) extended family
   D) market group
16. Culture is best described as ___
   A) the way of life of a people
   B) a kind of food only
   C) the name of a street
   D) a school rule
17. Which of these is NOT an element of culture?
   A) festivals and greetings
   B) the price of fuel
   C) dressing
   D) language
18. The cloth tied on a Yoruba woman's head is the ___
   A) buba
   B) fila
   C) hula
   D) gele
19. The head tie or cap worn by men in the north is called a ___
   A) fila
   B) iro
   C) gele
   D) wrapper
20. Amele, eba and pounded yam belong to Nigerian ___
   A) music
   B) food
   C) dressing
   D) names
21. The Yoruba way of greeting an elder by a boy is to ___
   A) shout
   B) run
   C) clap only
   D) prostrate
22. A kneeling greeting for an elder is done by a Yoruba ___
   A) stranger
   B) dog
   C) girl
   D) driver
23. Nigeria is found in the ___ part of Africa.
   A) western
   B) northern only
   C) eastern
   D) southern only
24. The capital city of Nigeria is ___
   A) Lagos
   B) Abuja
   C) Ibadan
   D) Kano
25. Nigeria has ___ states and one Federal Capital Territory.
   A) fifty
   B) twenty
   C) thirty-six
   D) twelve
26. The official language used in our schools in Nigeria is ___
   A) Hindi only
   B) French
   C) Arabic
   D) English
27. Our national motto says "Unity and Faith, ___ and Progress."
   A) Prayer
   B) Peace
   C) Plenty
   D) Power
28. Telling the one in charge about an open pit you saw is called ___
   A) making a noise
   B) telling a story
   C) reporting a danger
   D) hiding a matter
29. An attitude is best described as ___
   A) how we think and feel about a thing
   B) a thing we buy at the market
   C) a long journey
   D) a school song
30. Doing the right thing when nobody is looking is called ___
   A) speed
   B) pride
   C) luck
   D) integrity

## Section B — Sub-objective (10 marks)

31. Mention the three kinds of marriage ceremony we have in Nigeria.
32. State four duties of parents to their children.
33. Give four roles of children in the family.
34. Name four elements of culture.
35. Mention four foods and two clothes of Nigeria.
36. Name the three big ethnic groups of Nigeria.
37. State four core values of Nigeria.
38. Mention four dangers that can harm a child at home.
39. Name the people we can run to when we are in danger.
40. Give four things that make a place secure.

## Section C — Theory (10 marks)
Answer in full sentences.

41. What is marriage? Describe the three kinds of ceremony found in Nigeria. (2 marks)
42. Explain four duties of parents and four duties of children in a family. (2 marks)
43. What is culture? Write on the food, dressing and greetings of our people. (2 marks)
44. Write on Nigeria: where it is, its states and capital, its languages and its flag. (2 marks)
45. What is danger? Explain how a child finds help and keeps safe from strangers. (2 marks)
