# EXAMINATION PRACTICE — BASIC SCIENCE (Primary 1 · 1st Term)

**Time:** 1 hour · **Total: 50 marks**

## Section A — Objective (30 marks)
Choose the correct option and ring the letter.

1. A road that is smooth and black is called a ___
   A) bush path
   B) tarred road
   C) muddy road
   D) water way
2. The small road that people use for walking is a ___
   A) bridge
   B) flyover
   C) pathway
   D) highway
3. The traffic light that tells us to stop is ___
   A) yellow
   B) green
   C) blue
   D) red
4. Everything around us at home and at school is the ___
   A) calendar
   B) environment
   C) market
   D) notebook
5. Red, blue and yellow are the ___ colours
   A) secondary
   B) pale
   C) primary
   D) dark
6. A round shape with no corners is a ___
   A) circle
   B) triangle
   C) rectangle
   D) square
7. The shape with three sides and three corners is a ___
   A) oval
   B) circle
   C) square
   D) triangle
8. A shape with four equal sides and four corners is a ___
   A) star
   B) oval
   C) square
   D) rectangle
9. The shape of an egg is an ___
   A) oval
   B) square
   C) heart
   D) circle
10. A tin of milk is the solid shape called a ___
   A) cone
   B) cylinder
   C) sphere
   D) cube
11. A ball is the solid shape called a ___
   A) cylinder
   B) cone
   C) cube
   D) sphere
12. Things that have life like humans, animals and plants are ___ things
   A) living
   B) man-made
   C) stone
   D) non-living

   A) non-living things
   B) living things
   C) toys
   D) tools

   A) melt
   B) rust
   C) move
   D) break
15. A plant needs water, air and ___ to grow well
   A) sunlight
   B) money
   C) shoes
   D) paper
16. Things that do not eat, grow or breathe are ___
   A) living things
   B) plants
   C) animals
   D) non-living things
17. A stone, a chair and a book are ___
   A) pets
   B) crops
   C) non-living things
   D) living things
18. Rocks, soil, water and sand are ___ non-living things
   A) living
   B) natural
   C) growing
   D) man-made
19. Cars, houses, chairs and toys are ___ non-living things
   A) natural
   B) living
   C) breathing
   D) man-made

   A) cloud

   D) rain

   A) green

   A) school
