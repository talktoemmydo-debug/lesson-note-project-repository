# EXAMINATION PRACTICE — GENERAL KNOWLEDGE (Nursery 2 · 3rd Term)

**Time:** 1 hour · **Total: 54 marks**

## Section A — Objective (30 marks)
Choose the correct option and ring the letter.

1. Our state, where the land meets the sea, is ____
   A) Lagos
   B) Sokoto
   C) Borno
   D) Kano
2. The big city of our state is called ____
   A) Calabar
   B) Lokoja
   C) Ikeja
   D) Zaria
3. On the map of Nigeria, the sea is at the ____
   A) top
   B) bottom
   C) right side
   D) middle
4. The market, the motor park, the school, a church or mosque and the ____ are the five places of a town
   A) bus station
   B) football field
   C) cinema hall
   D) water point
5. The festival when masked people walk in white through the streets of our town is ____
   A) Eyo
   B) Durbar
   C) Argungu
   D) New Yam
6. Water for drinking in the house is first ____
   A) buried
   B) painted
   C) boiled
   D) frozen
7. Wind is really ____ that is moving
   A) sand
   B) air
   C) smoke
   D) dust
8. In the float-and-sink test, the thing that goes down is ____
   A) a stone
   B) a feather
   C) a dry leaf
   D) a cork
9. We waste water when we leave the ____ running
   A) fan
   B) lamp
   C) tap
   D) door
10. The part of the day when the sun is highest and hottest is ____
   A) morning
   B) evening
   C) night
   D) midday
11. At night we see the moon and the ____ in the sky
   A) kites
   B) stars
   C) boats
   D) planes
12. Rain comes down out of the ____
   A) ground
   B) market
   C) sky
   D) well
13. A wheel makes a heavy load easy to move because it ____
   A) rolls
   B) floats
   C) rusts
   D) melts
14. A plank laid from the ground up to a lorry is a ____
   A) keg
   B) bucket
   C) lever
   D) ramp
15. To lift a big stone with a strong stick is to use a ____
   A) rake
   B) lever
   C) broom
   D) ruler
16. A pair of scissors is passed to a friend by the ____
   A) spring
   B) blade
   C) handle
   D) point
17. Nigeria took its name from the ____
   A) river Niger
   B) river Benue
   C) long mountain
   D) big lake
18. The colours of our flag, read in order, are ____
   A) red, green, red
   B) green, red, white
   C) white, green, white
   D) green, white, green
19. The Niger and the Benue, our two big rivers, meet ____
   A) at the top of the map
   B) in the middle
   C) only in the north
   D) far at the sea
20. A child who is different from me is treated ____
   A) the same
   B) last
   C) never
   D) first
21. Twenty bottle tops put in two equal lines make lines of ____
   A) five
   B) fifteen
   C) eight
   D) ten
22. The day that comes right after Friday is ____
   A) Sunday
   B) Wednesday
   C) Saturday
   D) Monday
23. A book is like which of these shapes? ____
   A) a ball
   B) a square
   C) a circle
   D) a cone
24. Which of these is a vegetable and not a fruit? ____
   A) ugu
   B) mango
   C) orange
   D) banana
25. The worker whose labour makes sure the whole town eats is the ____
   A) barber
   B) guard
   C) driver
   D) farmer
26. In the stories of our people, the small one that out-thinks the whole bush is ____
   A) the horse
   B) the elephant
   C) the tortoise
   D) the lion
27. Queen Amina of Zaria is remembered in story as ____
   A) a boat man
   B) a warrior queen
   C) a drum maker
   D) a market seller
28. A hero is a person who ____
   A) takes the big share
   B) runs from trouble
   C) hides his mistake
   D) finishes the work begun
29. In the game "Where does it live?", a hen lives ____
   A) in the forest
   B) in the river
   C) in the house yard
   D) in the sky
30. "I come out in the day and give the world light and warmth. Who am I?" — ____
   A) the sun
   B) the star
   C) the lamp
   D) the moon

## Section B — Sub-objective (10 marks)
Answer briefly.

31. Name the five places every town has
32. Say four places water comes from
33. Name four uses of water in the house
34. Say the five parts of the day in order
35. Name three things that take work off our hands
36. Mention four tools we use at school
37. Say three peoples of Nigeria
38. Name the colours of our flag in order
39. Write the seven days of the week
40. Say the three lines of a show and tell

## Section C — Theory (10 marks)
Answer in full sentences.

41. Write two sentences on your town and name the five places in it. (2 marks)
42. Say four things water is used for and one way we keep it clean. (3 marks)
43. Name the parts of the day in order and say what the sun gives us. (4 marks)
44. Write two sentences on one worker who keeps the town going and what he does. (3 marks)
45. Say what you want to be when you grow up and give two reasons. (2 marks)
