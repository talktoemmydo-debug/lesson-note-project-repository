# EXAMINATION PRACTICE — MATHEMATICS & ENGLISH · LITERACY (LANGUAGE DOMAIN) (Nursery 2 · 2nd Term)

**Time:** 1 hour · **Total: 50 marks**

## Section A — Objective (30 marks)
Choose the correct option and ring the letter.

1. A capital letter is used at ___
   A) the end of a word
   B) before every small letter
   C) the middle of a name
   D) the start of a sentence
2. A sentence is written correctly when it begins with ___
   A) capitals on every word
   B) a small letter and ends with a comma
   C) a capital letter and ends with a full stop
   D) a full stop and no spaces
3. The word that is always a capital, wherever it stands, is ___
   A) I
   B) and
   C) a
   D) the
4. The first letter of the name of a month must be ___
   A) crossed
   B) a small letter
   C) a dot
   D) a capital
5. A double consonant in a word means ___
   A) a silent letter
   B) three letters, one sound
   C) the same consonant written twice
   D) two vowels together
6. Which word has a double consonant?
   A) spoon
   B) rabbit
   C) table
   D) chair
7. The sound in "egg" is written with ___
   A) gg
   B) cc
   C) dd
   D) ck
8. English writes "comb" with a b because ___
   A) it came from a Yoruba root word
   B) b is always written at word end
   C) the letter b is sounded twice over
   D) the b was silent, the spelling stayed
9. A silent letter is a letter that is ___
   A) spoken twice
   B) written but not spoken
   C) always a vowel
   D) used to begin a sentence
10. The long a sound in "rain" is written ___
   A) ay
   B) ea
   C) ai
   D) ee
11. The long a sound at the end of a word is usually written ___
   A) ee
   B) ai
   C) ea
   D) ay
12. Which spelling is correct?
   A) train
   B) trane
   C) traine
   D) trayn
13. The words "bee", "tree" and "see" use the sound ___
   A) long a with ai
   B) short i
   C) long e with ee
   D) silent e
14. "Sea" and "see" sound the same but are ___
   A) rhyming words
   B) spelled differently
   C) both names
   D) both numbers
15. The long i sound in "night" is written ___
   A) igh
   B) eye
   C) ie
   D) y
16. "Boy" and "toy" end with the sound written ___
   A) ou
   B) oi
   C) oy
   D) ow
17. The word "coin" is spelled with ___
   A) oy at the end
   B) ie
   C) igh
   D) oi in the middle
18. "Out", "shout" and "house" use the sound written ___
   A) oi
   B) ou
   C) ai
   D) ow
19. "Cow", "how" and "now" end with the sound written ___
   A) igh
   B) ou
   C) ow
   D) oi
20. A trigraph is ___
   A) three letters making one sound
   B) two letters making one sound
   C) three separate sounds
   D) a silent letter
21. "Care" and "chair" show that ___
   A) care has a silent letter c
   B) are and air make the same sound
   C) air has two vowel sounds
   D) are is longer than air is
22. The trigraph tch is written after ___
   A) a full stop
   B) a capital
   C) a long vowel
   D) a short vowel
23. Which word carries the trigraph ure?
   A) picture
   B) centre
   C) little
   D) proper
24. We use "an" before a word that begins with ___
   A) any letter
   B) a consonant sound
   C) a vowel sound
   D) a capital only
25. Which phrase is correct?
   A) some honest mans
   B) an honest man
   C) a honest man
   D) an house of egg
26. "Give me ____ water" — the correct word is ___
   A) one
   B) a
   C) an
   D) some
27. Which is correct: "I have ____ (4) pens"?
   A) four
   B) four's
   C) fore
   D) for
28. "There" and "their" are different because ___
   A) both of them are number words
   B) there is a place, their is belonging
   C) their is a place to sit down
   D) they sound nothing at all alike
29. The word "was" tells us something that ___
   A) is a colour
   B) is a question
   C) already happened
   D) will happen tomorrow
30. "Like" and "hide" both have ___
   A) two full stops
   B) a silent k
   C) a short i sound
   D) a long i sound

## Section B — Sub-objective (10 marks)
Answer briefly.

31. Write your own name, your town and your country, each with the right first letter.
32. Write the seven days of the week, each with a capital first letter.
33. Find the double consonants in: ladder, summer, buzz, muffin.
34. Write the four ways the long a sound is spelled, with one word each.
35. Write four words with ee and four with ea.
36. Write three words with igh and three with y for the same sound.
37. Choose a or an: apple, book, egg, orange, pen, hour.
38. Use some, a or an: water, boys, one pen, many eggs.
39. Write the six sight words of this term.
40. Correct this sentence: "bola and ada went to abuja on monday."

## Section C — Theory (10 marks)
Answer in full sentences.

41. Write the three rules for using a capital letter and give an example of each. (2 marks)
42. What is a silent letter? Explain with "comb", "knee" and "write". (2 marks)
43. Explain alternative spellings: write four words for the long a sound and four for the long e sound. (2 marks)
44. What is a trigraph? Give words with are, air, tch and ure. (2 marks)
45. When do we use a, an and some? Write five examples and explain the one with a silent letter. (2 marks)
