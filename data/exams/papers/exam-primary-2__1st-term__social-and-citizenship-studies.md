# EXAMINATION PRACTICE — SOCIAL AND CITIZENSHIP STUDIES (Primary 2 · 1st Term)

**Time:** 1 hour · **Total: 50 marks**

## Section A — Objective (30 marks)
Choose the correct option and ring the letter.

1. A group of people related to one another is a ___
   A) tree
   B) family
   C) river
   D) stone
2. The ___ group we belong to is the family
   A) biggest
   B) smallest
   C) first
   D) last
3. Our brothers and sisters are ___ members
   A) family
   B) farm
   C) school
   D) market
4. The family ___ for us and teaches us
   A) sleeps
   B) runs
   C) fights
   D) cares
5. A group of people living in one place is a ___
   A) stone
   B) community
   C) tree
   D) river
6. People in our community have different ___
   A) clouds
   B) trees
   C) works
   D) stones
7. We have farmers, traders, teachers and ___
   A) doctors
   B) stones
   C) clouds
   D) trees
8. We keep our community clean and ___
   A) loud
   B) safe
   C) dirty
   D) dark
9. What we must do or not do is a ___
   A) stone
   B) song
   C) dream
   D) rule
10. Rules help us live ___ together
   A) noisily
   B) rudely
   C) well
   D) badly
11. At school we ___ our teachers
   A) shout at
   B) obey
   C) ignore
   D) fight
12. Rules keep order and ___
   A) noise
   B) war
   C) anger
   D) peace
13. Something we are free to have or do is a ___
   A) right
   B) noise
   C) stone
   D) dream
14. We have the right to ___ and go to school
   A) fight
   B) sleep
   C) learn
   D) steal
15. Something we must do is a ___
   A) game
   B) lie
   C) song
   D) duty
16. It is our duty to ___ rules at school
   A) obey
   B) break
   C) burn
   D) hide
17. Showing honour to others is ___
   A) anger
   B) sleep
   C) respect
   D) noise
18. Honesty means telling the ___
   A) lie
   B) truth
   C) joke
   D) story
19. An honest person does not steal or ___
   A) run
   B) jump
   C) sing
   D) lie
20. Honest people are ___
   A) feared
   B) hated
   C) trusted
   D) ignored
21. Living without fighting is ___
   A) peace
   B) anger
   C) war
   D) noise
22. We ___ our things with others
   A) hide
   B) share
   C) burn
   D) break
23. When we quarrel, we say ___ and make peace
   A) nothing
   B) lies
   C) bad words
   D) sorry
24. We use ___ words, not angry words
   A) harsh
   B) loud
   C) kind
   D) rude
25. The way of life of a people is ___
   A) culture
   B) rain
   C) sleep
   D) noise
26. Our culture includes language, food, dress and ___
   A) dust
   B) dance
   C) anger
   D) fire
27. We greet our ___ in our own way
   A) babies
   B) rivers
   C) stones
   D) elders
28. We eat our ___ food like rice and yam
   A) dirty
   B) bad
   C) local
   D) foreign
29. The people and place we live with are our ___
   A) community
   B) dream
   C) body
   D) song
30. A person who loves and helps their country is a good ___
   A) stranger
   B) citizen
   C) thief
   D) baby
