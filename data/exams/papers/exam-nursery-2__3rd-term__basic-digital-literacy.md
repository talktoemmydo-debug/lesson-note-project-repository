# EXAMINATION PRACTICE — BASIC DIGITAL LITERACY (Nursery 2 · 3rd Term)

**Time:** 1 hour · **Total: 54 marks**

## Section A — Objective (30 marks)

   B) shoes
   C) ears
   D) hair

   D) speakers

   D) sweep the floor

   B) umbrella
   C) water tank
   D) shoe box

   B) spoon
   C) cap
   D) mouse

   A) dust

   D) light

   A) writing

   D) music

   A) bucket

   D) monitor

   A) under

   D) behind

   A) bag
   B) leg
   C) face
   D) shoe

   A) hard drive
