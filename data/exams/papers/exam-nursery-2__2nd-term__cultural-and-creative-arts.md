# EXAMINATION PRACTICE — CULTURAL AND CREATIVE ARTS (CCA) (Nursery 2 · 2nd Term)

**Time:** 1 hour · **Total: 50 marks**

## Section A — Objective (30 marks)
Choose the correct option and ring the letter.

1. A hand fan is used to ___
   A) keep us cool by moving air
   B) cut paper into pieces
   C) carry books to class
   D) dry our wet clothes in the rain
2. The two kinds of fan we can make in class are the paddle fan and the ___
   A) ceiling fan
   B) round fan
   C) folding fan
   D) window fan
3. The stick we hold a fan by is called its ___
   A) spine
   B) handle
   C) wing
   D) cover
4. Thick paper used for a paddle fan is ___
   A) cardboard
   B) a wet napkin
   C) a thin polythene bag
   D) a soft handkerchief
5. A design that repeats itself is called a ___
   A) story
   B) picture
   C) pattern
   D) letter
6. Colour splash art is made by ___
   A) cutting the paper with scissors
   B) sewing two pieces of cloth
   C) drawing only straight ruler lines
   D) dropping and flicking paint on paper
7. Before splashing paint we should first ___
   A) sweep the whole of the floor
   B) put on an apron and cover the table
   C) wash our hands with the soap
   D) eat our lunch inside the room
8. Blue and yellow mixed together will give the colour ___
   A) brown
   B) black
   C) green
   D) orange
9. Art that shows a feeling instead of a thing is called ___
   A) a poster
   B) a copy book
   C) a portrait
   D) abstract art
10. A hedgehog is covered on its back with ___
   A) spikes
   B) feathers
   C) long hair
   D) scales
11. The round part of a hedgehog's body ends in front in a ___
   A) flat shell
   B) long tail
   C) pointed nose
   D) big ear
12. To make spikes on a paper hedgehog we can use ___
   A) a comb and water
   B) a fork dipped in paint
   C) chalk and a duster
   D) a spoon and a plate
13. Using paint, paper and twigs together in one picture is called ___
   A) mixed media art
   B) only drawing
   C) flat printing
   D) a sand collage
14. How a thing would feel is called its ___
   A) number
   B) colour
   C) name
   D) texture
15. A picture made by sticking torn and cut paper on a surface is a ___
   A) mural
   B) collage
   C) model
   D) mosaic
16. Tearing paper gives an edge that is ___
   A) hard and straight
   B) wet and heavy
   C) soft and feathered
   D) hot and sharp
17. On the friendship tree, one leaf carries ___
   A) the name of a friend
   B) the price of the tree
   C) a drawing of a bird
   D) only our class number
18. A paper bead is made by rolling paper and then ___
   A) boiling the paper in water
   B) putting it in a cold tin
   C) sewing it on a piece of cloth
   D) gluing the tip and letting it dry
19. Jewellery worn round the wrist is called a ___
   A) necklace
   B) anklet
   C) bracelet
   D) ring
20. When we thread beads in an order that repeats, we are making a ___
   A) pattern
   B) poem
   C) picture
   D) promise
21. A foam collage differs from a paper collage because it ___
   A) needs no glue at all
   B) must be painted with water
   C) lies perfectly flat always
   D) rises up from the paper in layers
22. Putting one piece of foam above another to make a picture look thick is ___
   A) shading
   B) layering
   C) tearing
   D) counting
23. In a sand collage, sand is sprinkled on the picture ___
   A) before any drawing is made
   B) instead of using paper
   C) after glue is put on that part
   D) only on the table edge
24. Sand, clay and shells used in art are called ___
   A) factory materials
   B) painted materials
   C) old materials
   D) natural materials
25. Extra sand on a sand picture is ___
   A) shaken off back into the tin
   B) left to cover the drawing
   C) blown on the floor
   D) watered with the plants
26. A cone hat is rolled from paper and held with ___
   A) only our hands
   B) glue, tape or staples
   C) a wet piece of cloth
   D) a heavy stone
27. An elastic is put round the hat to ___
   A) make it heavier
   B) hide the colour
   C) keep it on the head
   D) shorten the cone
28. Walking round the class wearing the hats we made is called a ___
   A) hat parade
   B) hat market
   C) hat counting
   D) hat lesson
29. Glued work should be tested only after it has ___
   A) been wetted again
   B) been left in the sun
   C) been folded
   D) dried well
30. A craft foam sheet is a material that feels ___
   A) thin like a handkerchief
   B) soft and thick
   C) hard like a stone
   D) wet and cold

## Section B — Sub-objective (10 marks)
Answer briefly.

31. Name three materials used for making a hand fan.
32. Mention four ways of decorating a fan or a hat.
33. Give four things needed for colour splash art.
34. Name the parts of a hedgehog we draw in our picture.
35. Mention four materials for making jewellery in class.
36. Name four things we do to make a foam or paper collage.
37. Give the four steps of a sand collage in order.
38. Write the four stages of making a cone hat in order.
39. Mention three rules for using scissors and glue in art.
40. Say the two kinds of fan we can make and what a fan is for.

## Section C — Theory (10 marks)
Answer in full sentences.

41. Describe how you made your hand fan, from the material to the testing of it. (2 marks)
42. What is a collage? Explain how you made your paper collage or your friendship tree. (2 marks)
43. Write on the hedgehog you made: its parts, how you gave it spikes and what texture means. (2 marks)
44. Describe how you made jewellery in class and what you must be careful with. (2 marks)
45. Write on any two of the art work of this term: what you used and what you learnt from it. (2 marks)
