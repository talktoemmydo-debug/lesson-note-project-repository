# EXAMINATION PRACTICE — CULTURAL AND CREATIVE ARTS (Nursery 2 · 1st Term)

**Time:** 1 hour · **Total: 50 marks**

## Section A — Objective (30 marks)
Choose the correct option and ring the letter.

1. The drawing of a car has these four parts ___
   A) petals, roots, a stem and seeds
   B) pages, a cover, a spine and words
   C) body, windows, two wheels and a headlight
   D) wings, feathers, a beak and a tail
2. What do wheels do?
   A) They help the car to move
   B) They keep the car warm
   C) They light the car inside
   D) They help the car to sing
3. We use crayons to ___
   A) cook rice in the pot
   B) cut our hair into shapes
   C) wash the grease off our hands
   D) colour inside the lines of a drawing
4. Paint is kept in a ___
   A) school shoe
   B) soft pillow
   C) small tin or can
   D) big cooking pot
5. Before we paint, the surface must be ___
   A) wet and muddy
   B) clean and dry
   C) hot from the sun
   D) covered in dust and sand
6. Thumb-printing is done with the ___
   A) thumb of the hand
   B) point of the elbow
   C) tip of the ear
   D) knee of the leg
7. The pattern made by pressing a thumb on paper is called a ___
   A) hand shake drawing
   B) footprint rubbing
   C) wall shadow
   D) thumb print
8. A Barney is a character we ___
   A) wear on the head
   B) eat
   C) draw and colour
   D) keep in water
9. The lines that show where a drawing ends are called ___
   A) outlines
   B) story verses
   C) rhyming lines
   D) deep shadows
10. In "Barney's environment" we colour the things ___
   A) only the inside of Barney's big mouth
   B) around Barney — his home and his friends
   C) only what is hidden under Barney's bed
   D) the moon and all the far away stars
11. A pig has a short tail that is ___
   A) long like a rope
   B) feathered like a bird
   C) flat and square
   D) curly like a spring
12. The foot of a pig is called a ___
   A) paw with claws
   B) fin
   C) trotter
   D) hoof of a horse
13. The pig, goat and cow are drawn in our lesson because they are ___
   A) animals kept at home
   B) wild animals of the bush
   C) toys made of plastic
   D) animals of the deep sea
14. Strands of dried grass or wheat used for making jewellery are ___
   A) leaves
   B) straw
   C) bones
   D) stones
15. A craft pattern that goes red, blue, red, blue is called a ___
   A) straight line
   B) song pattern
   C) repeating pattern
   D) one-off pattern
16. Which of these can be made out of straw?
   A) A bracelet, a ring and a small cap
   B) A bed made of iron
   C) A cup filled with cold water
   D) A pencil carved from hard wax
17. An umbrella protects us from ___
   A) the noise of the market
   B) our own homework
   C) the snake in the bush
   D) falling rain and hot sunshine
18. The strong cloth stretched over an umbrella is called the ___
   A) carrying handle
   B) canopy
   C) rubber tip
   D) box it came in
19. The frame of wire that holds the canopy is called the ___
   A) skeleton
   B) market basket
   C) soft pillow
   D) car wheel
20. Vegetable printing uses ___
   A) a wooden eating spoon
   B) a long sewing needle
   C) the cut surface of an okra
   D) only a painting brush
21. Before printing with a vegetable, we spread paint on a ___
   A) bucket of water
   B) flat tray or plate
   C) wall mirror
   D) school shoe
22. The print left by an okra looks like ___
   A) a tall storey building
   B) a plain round ball
   C) a long straight road
   D) a little star or a flower
23. Plasticine, which we mould with our hands, is a soft ___
   A) coloured clay
   B) sweet red fruit
   C) clean cotton cloth
   D) running tap water
24. A small ball and a rolled snake-like piece are the first ___
   A) dances for jumping
   B) sums for adding
   C) shapes for modelling
   D) songs for singing
25. To join two pieces of plasticine we ___
   A) drop them twice
   B) press them hard together
   C) sew them with thread
   D) blow air on them
26. Keeping paint off your uniform while you work is part of ___
   A) breaking the tools in anger
   B) hiding the materials from friends
   C) wasting the paint and materials
   D) taking good care of materials and tools
27. A drawing that shows a thing as it really looks is made with ___
   A) patience and careful shading
   B) only a long ruler
   C) a clean soft rubber
   D) two eyes kept closed
28. The noise dance we do with simple instruments keeps the body ___
   A) making no sound at all
   B) sitting completely still on the chair
   C) moving the feet and hands in time with the beat
   D) quietly asleep on the mat
29. Which of these is NOT a way of making a pattern?
   A) Repeating the same colours
   B) Alternating shapes and lines
   C) Printing with a leaf
   D) Shouting loudly in class
30. A good artist at the end of an art lesson ___
   A) hides the crayons under a bench
   B) washes the brush and lays the work out to dry
   C) tears the finished drawing in two
   D) leaves the wet paint pot on the floor

## Section B — Sub-objective (10 marks)
Answer briefly.

31. Name the four parts of a car we draw.
32. Mention three things we colour a car drawing with.
33. Give two rules of thumb-printing.
34. Name two places we print a thumb pattern.
35. Draw or name two things we colour in Barney's environment.
36. Name two things about a pig's body.
37. Mention two things we make from straw.
38. State two rules for caring for an umbrella.
39. Name two vegetables we can print with.
40. Give two things you can model with plasticine.

## Section C — Theory (10 marks)
Answer in full sentences.

41. Draw a car and colour it (2 marks)
42. Write about your own thumb print: how you made it and what you turned it into (2 marks)
43. Draw Barney and colour his environment (2 marks)
44. Describe how you made an umbrella in class, naming its three parts (2 marks)
45. Write two sentences on what you moulded with plasticine and how you joined the pieces (2 marks)
