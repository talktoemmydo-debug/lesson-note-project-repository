# EXAMINATION PRACTICE — NIGERIAN HISTORY (Nursery 2 · 2nd Term)

**Time:** 1 hour · **Total: 50 marks**

## Section A — Objective (30 marks)
Choose the correct option and ring the letter.

1. A hero is a person we ___
   A) admire for the way he helps others
   B) fear because he is very strong
   C) pay money to come and see
   D) avoid whenever he is kind
2. A woman who is a hero is called a ___
   A) queen mother
   B) market woman
   C) heroine
   D) princess only
3. Doing the right thing when your heart is beating fast with fear is ___
   A) pride
   B) rest
   C) play
   D) bravery
4. Selfless service means helping others ___
   A) only when you are paid
   B) without asking for a reward
   C) once and never again
   D) for a big prize
5. Which of these is NOT a quality of a hero?
   A) being kind to the sorrowful
   B) keeping one's own good word
   C) taking things from the weak
   D) always telling people the truth
6. People who keep our class clean and sweep the market before dawn are ___
   A) everyday heroes
   B) only workmen
   C) not important
   D) strangers to us
7. Teachers are heroes in our community because they ___
   A) sell books to us
   B) give us knowledge every day
   C) close the school gate
   D) drive us home
8. The people who run into smoke to put out a fire are ___
   A) farmers
   B) teachers
   C) traders
   D) firefighters
9. A police officer is a hero because he ___
   A) cooks for the street
   B) sells fuel
   C) keeps us safe from thieves
   D) sweeps the gutter
10. An everyday hero at home is ___
   A) a father or mother who works for us
   B) a man we only see on TV
   C) the driver of a big bus
   D) a stranger at the market gate
11. A national hero is a person whose work was for ___
   A) one market
   B) his own family
   C) one shop only
   D) the whole country
12. Herbert Macaulay was born in Lagos in the year ___
   A) 1999
   B) 1864
   C) 1960
   D) 2006
13. Herbert Macaulay was trained as a ___
   A) surveyor and an engineer
   B) soldier with a gun
   C) town doctor and healer
   D) motor bus driver
14. Macaulay formed the first political ___ in Nigeria.
   A) farm
   B) school
   C) party
   D) army
15. Macaulay is remembered because he used the newspaper, the court and ___ to fight for the people
   A) fighting with guns
   B) hiding in the bush
   C) leaving the country
   D) speaking and protesting
16. Nnamdi Azikiwe was called Zik of ___
   A) Africa
   B) the school
   C) Lagos
   D) Europe
17. Azikiwe used a newspaper called The West African ___ to teach the people.
   A) Paper
   B) Pilot
   C) Pilot Bird
   D) Press
18. Azikiwe became the first ____ of Nigeria.
   A) captain
   B) farmer
   C) president
   D) chief
19. Azikiwe was born in the year ___
   A) 1904
   B) 2015
   C) 1960
   D) 1999
20. Queen Amina ruled the land of ___
   A) Benin only
   B) Warri
   C) Kano
   D) Zazzau, now Zaria
21. Queen Amina is remembered for the ___
   A) ships she built
   B) school she opened
   C) city walls round the towns
   D) bridges over the sea
22. The walls built in Queen Amina's time are called ___
   A) gele
   B) ganuwa
   C) agbada
   D) iro
23. Funmilayo Ransome-Kuti came from the town of ___
   A) Sokoto
   B) Jos
   C) Onitsha
   D) Abeokuta
24. She led the market women against ___
   A) free schooling
   B) the price of cloth
   C) an unfair tax
   D) the new road
25. Margaret Ekpo organised the market women of ___
   A) Badagry
   B) Aba
   C) Calabar only
   D) Zaria
26. Volunteering means ___
   A) offering to help before you are asked
   B) telling another child to work
   C) refusing a duty
   D) waiting until you are paid
27. Which of these is a small act of service at school?
   A) hiding his book
   B) pushing him in the queue
   C) carrying a friend's heavy bag
   D) taking his chalk
28. Cleaning the gutter of the school helps the community because ___
   A) nobody will ever see us do it
   B) it looks very busy to the visitors
   C) we can skip our own class today
   D) the water will flow and sickness falls
29. Keeping our environment clean and helping the old are part of ___
   A) keeping a secret
   B) being a good citizen

   D) making a market
30. A desk in the classroom belongs to everybody, so it is called ___
   A) public property
   B) a teacher's bag
   C) a stranger's good
   D) my own property

## Section C — Theory (10 marks)
Answer in full sentences.

41. Who is a hero? Explain the three qualities of a hero with examples. (2 marks)
42. Write on the work of Herbert Macaulay and why he is called the father of Nigerian nationalism. (2 marks)
43. Give an account of Nnamdi Azikiwe: his praise-name, his work and the office he held. (2 marks)
44. Write on any two Nigerian heroines and what they did for their people. (2 marks)
45. What is selfless service? Explain four ways we can serve our school and community. (2 marks)
