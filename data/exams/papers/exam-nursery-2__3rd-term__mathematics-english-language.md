# EXAMINATION PRACTICE — MATHEMATICS & ENGLISH · LITERACY (LANGUAGE DOMAIN) (Nursery 2 · 3rd Term)

**Time:** 1 hour · **Total: 54 marks**

## Section A — Objective (30 marks)
Choose the correct option and ring the letter.

1. Two consonants that keep their own sounds and run together are called a ____
   A) blend
   B) sight word
   C) trigraph
   D) digraph
2. The two letters sh in "shirt" make ____
   A) no sound at all
   B) two sounds apart
   C) a long line
   D) one sound
3. The three letters held to one sound in "catch" are ____
   A) cat
   B) atc
   C) tch
   D) ach
4. Which of these words carries the air sound?
   A) dress
   B) chair
   C) shirt
   D) clock
5. In "clock" the two letters at the end are a ____
   A) digraph
   B) naming word
   C) plural mark
   D) blend
6. A noun is a word that ____
   A) shows a size
   B) ends a line
   C) tells the time
   D) names a thing
7. Which of these is a proper noun?
   A) school
   B) Bola
   C) river
   D) chair
8. "One child, two ____"
   A) children
   B) childs
   C) childes
   D) childrens
9. A big letter is put on ____
   A) a full stop
   B) a colour word
   C) a name like Lagos
   D) a doing word
10. Which pair of words are opposites?
   A) big and tall
   B) run and jump
   C) there and cat
   D) old and young
11. A verb tells us what somebody ____
   A) owns
   B) does
   C) is called
   D) looks like
12. We say "I sing" but we say "she ____"
   A) sing
   B) singed
   C) sings
   D) singing
13. Which asking word opens the line "____ do you do?"
   A) What
   B) Where
   C) Who
   D) When
14. Which of these is a doing word?
   A) table
   B) green
   C) small
   D) clap
15. Every sentence must have a naming word and a ____ word
   A) colour
   B) doing
   C) long
   D) small
16. Which of these is NOT yet a sentence?
   A) The happy child.
   B) Fish swim.
   C) Dogs run.
   D) The girl jumps.
17. The doing word that belongs to a fish is ____
   A) teaches
   B) drives
   C) flies
   D) swims
18. "____ sun hat is on the chair" — the word that shows it is yours is ____
   A) Then
   B) They
   C) Your
   D) There
19. "The boy kicks the ball" has a naming word, a doing word and ____
   A) no other word
   B) another naming word
   C) a plural mark
   D) a big letter only
20. The word that brings a reason is ____
   A) because
   B) there
   C) them
   D) and
21. "I see the boys" can be said as "I see ____"
   A) they're
   B) there
   C) them
   D) then
22. Which line is joined with the word "and"?
   A) I run now.
   B) Why do I run?
   C) I run fast.
   D) I run and I jump.
23. The person a page is mostly about is its ____ character
   A) quiet
   B) main
   C) small
   D) last
24. Which of these questions asks for the place?
   A) Who?
   B) When?
   C) Where?
   D) Why?
25. Before we turn the page we can ____ what happens next
   A) forget
   B) count
   C) erase
   D) guess
26. Which word opens the old tale line "____ upon a time"?
   A) Once
   B) One
   C) Only
   D) Over
27. "The ball is ____" — the word that tells a place is ____
   A) they
   B) their
   C) there
   D) then
28. "They want ____ toys" — the toys belong to them, so we write ____
   A) us
   B) them
   C) there
   D) their
29. Proofreading your own line means reading it ____
   A) slowly with your finger
   B) only to yourself
   C) very fast aloud
   D) with your eyes shut
30. A name of a person or a place begins with a ____ letter
   A) quiet
   B) big
   C) round
   D) small
