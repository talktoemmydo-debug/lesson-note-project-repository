# EXAMINATION PRACTICE — CHRISTIAN RELIGIOUS STUDIES (CRS) (Nursery 2 · 2nd Term)

**Time:** 1 hour · **Total: 50 marks**

## Section A — Objective (30 marks)
Choose the correct option and ring the letter.

1. God is called our Father in ___
   A) our school only
   B) heaven
   C) the river
   D) the market
2. A father at home is different from God because God ___
   A) is a spirit and never dies
   B) eats our food
   C) sleeps in the afternoon
   D) lives in another house
3. God's love for us is said to be perfect and ___
   A) small
   B) far away
   C) everlasting
   D) changing
4. God gives us food, rain and the people who keep us. This shows that God ___
   A) forgets small children
   B) cares and provides
   C) needs our help
   D) only watches from far
5. Prayer is best described as ___
   A) talking to God
   B) singing to a friend
   C) sleeping quietly
   D) reading a book aloud
6. We can pray ___
   A) once in the whole week
   B) only when we are in trouble
   C) only inside a church building
   D) at any time and in any place
7. When we do wrong and say sorry to God, He ___
   A) punishes every child
   B) forgives us
   C) forgets us
   D) leaves us alone
8. The prayer Jesus taught His disciples is called ___
   A) the morning song
   B) the harvest prayer
   C) the Lord's Prayer
   D) the school pledge
9. "God, thank You for today. Forgive my wrong. Help me tomorrow." This short talk with God is a ___
   A) prayer
   B) picture
   C) lesson
   D) song
10. Children of God are told to call God ___
   A) Master only
   B) Stranger
   C) Neighbour
   D) Father
11. God knows my name, my face and my ___
   A) desk number
   B) bag colour
   C) thoughts
   D) shoes only
12. Because God is with us, we are never ___
   A) alone
   B) late
   C) hungry
   D) small
13. Trusting God is like leaning on ___
   A) a tall ladder
   B) a strong wall
   C) a wet cloth
   D) a weak rope
14. The Bible says, "Children, ____ your parents."
   A) forget
   B) follow nowhere
   C) fear only
   D) obey
15. Times we are told to pray every day are ___
   A) Saturday morning only
   B) when it is raining hard
   C) morning, table and night
   D) once in the whole month
16. Before we eat, we ___ God for the food.
   A) owe
   B) thank
   C) blame
   D) forget
17. At home a child shows respect to his father and mother by ___
   A) answering them politely
   B) shouting at them
   C) hiding from them
   D) ignoring them
18. Which of these is NOT the behaviour of a child of God?
   A) sharing things
   B) saying sorry
   C) being friendly
   D) telling lies always
19. When somebody hurts us and says sorry, a child of God should ___
   A) take his bag
   B) fight him twice
   C) forgive him
   D) never speak to him
20. On the Lord's day, Christians go to ___
   A) the market to sell
   B) church to worship God
   C) the river to swim
   D) the farm to plough
21. Offerings in church should be given with a ___
   A) cheerful heart
   B) sleepy head
   C) angry look
   D) crying face
22. Taking care of the animals and trees God made is part of ___
   A) keeping a shop
   B) making a market
   C) winning a prize
   D) caring for creation
23. Which of these spoils the environment God made?
   A) watering the plants
   B) pouring refuse in the gutter
   C) keeping the drain open
   D) sweeping the yard
24. Jesus is called the ____ of God.
   A) brother
   B) cousin
   C) Son
   D) servant only
25. Jesus was born in the town of ___.
   A) Bethlehem
   B) Kano
   C) Jericho
   D) Ibadan
26. A miracle is a work that ___
   A) needs big machines
   B) any child can do
   C) a doctor always does
   D) only God can do
27. The Bible books that first tell us about Jesus are Matthew, Mark, Luke and ___.
   A) Joshua
   B) John
   C) Acts
   D) Romans
28. Jesus taught the people mostly by ___
   A) stories called parables
   B) singing songs alone
   C) drawing on the wall
   D) long written letters
29. Jesus said, "Suffer the little ____ to come unto me."
   A) kings
   B) birds
   C) children
   D) dogs
30. In the story of the lost son, the father ___ when his son came back.
   A) took his clothes
   B) shut the door on him
   C) sent him away
   D) ran out to embrace him

## Section B — Sub-objective (10 marks)
Answer briefly.

31. Mention four things God does for us as a Father.
32. Name four times or ways we pray every day.
33. Mention five things we must do as children of God.
34. Name four things we do in church on the Lord's day.
35. Mention four ways of taking care of what God created.
36. Name four miracles of Jesus.
37. Mention four people Jesus was kind to.
38. Name the four books that tell us about Jesus.
39. Give two lessons from the parable of the sower and one from the lost son.
40. State two things Jesus said are the greatest commands.

## Section C — Theory (10 marks)
Answer in full sentences.

41. Who is God as our Father? Explain four things He does for His children. (2 marks)
42. What is prayer? Explain when we should pray and what we can say to God. (2 marks)
43. Mention and explain five things children of God must do at home and in church. (2 marks)
44. How can we take care of the things God created? Give four ways. (2 marks)
45. Write on what you learnt about Jesus Christ: who He is, how He taught, and how He treated children. (2 marks)
