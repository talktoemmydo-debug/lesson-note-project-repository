## Section B — Sub-objective (10 marks)
Answer briefly.

31. Write the first six letters of the alphabet in small letters.
32. Match four letters with their pictures: A, B, C, D.
33. Name the six letters reviewed in Week 3 and one word for each.
34. Write the -at family — any four words.
35. Write the -en and the -in families, two words each.
36. Name three letters that go below the line when we write them.
37. Read this sentence and say the CVC words in it: "The big bug sat on the log."
38. Answer the four questions on our story: who, what, where, how it ended.
39. Write the last eight letters of the alphabet.
40. Say the sound of each of these: sh, ch, th, ph, wh, ck.

## Section C — Theory (10 marks)
Answer in full sentences.

41. Write the whole alphabet in small letters (2 marks)
42. Make six words from the -at and the -un families and use two of them in sentences (2 marks)
43. Retell our class story in three sentences: beginning, middle and end (2 marks)
44. Match and write: for each of five letters, one word that begins with it (2 marks)
45. Draw one thing from the story and write the letter its name begins with, with two words that begin alike (2 marks)
