# EXAMINATION PRACTICE — SOCIAL AND CITIZENSHIP STUDIES (Nursery 2 · 3rd Term)

**Time:** 1 hour · **Total: 54 marks**

## Section A — Objective (30 marks)
Choose the correct option and ring the letter.

1. Waste like paper and peels should be put ____
   A) under a big leaf
   B) in the long gutter
   C) on the open ground
   D) into a covered bin
2. When the gutter is choked with litter, rain water ___
   A) has no way out
   B) goes away faster
   C) dries up at once
   D) turns clean again
3. The weather a place has over many years is its ___
   A) chart
   B) climate
   C) class
   D) cloth
4. A long time without any rain at all is called a ___
   A) flood
   B) storm
   C) shower
   D) drought
5. Animals that live in nature by themselves are ___
   A) wildlife
   B) market goods
   C) houseplants
   D) farm crops
6. A monkey makes its home in ___
   A) the town market shed
   B) the holes in the path
   C) the trees of the forest
   D) the long dry grass
7. Keeping nature and its animals safe is called ___
   A) collection
   B) decoration
   C) pollution
   D) conservation
8. Some animals are nearly gone because their ___
   A) food is brought in
   B) homes are cut down
   C) tails grow long
   D) names are lost out
9. The money of Nigeria is called the ___
   A) naira
   B) pesewa
   C) kobo
   D) cedi
10. Round money made of metal is a ___
   A) card
   B) note
   C) coin
   D) cup
11. Money made of paper is called a ___
   A) coin
   B) note
   C) mat
   D) plate
12. People are paid money because they ___
   A) work
   B) wait
   C) play
   D) sleep
13. Garri, palm oil and a school uniform are things we ___
   A) want only
   B) hide away
   C) need
   D) break often
14. Money that is not ours is ___
   A) kept in a pocket
   B) quickly spent
   C) shared with good friends
   D) never taken
15. If we find money on the floor, we ___
   A) put it in a pocket
   B) give it back
   C) hide it well
   D) keep it very quiet
16. A box with a slit for keeping money for later is a ___ bank
   A) piggy
   B) muddy
   C) sleepy
   D) tidy
17. A person who belongs to a country is a ___
   A) visitor
   B) leader
   C) baby
   D) citizen
18. One right of a child is the right to be ___
   A) tired
   B) loud
   C) safe
   D) last
19. Giving a girl and a boy the same turn is being ___
   A) late
   B) fair
   C) cross
   D) cold
20. When a small child is being bullied, we ___
   A) keep very quiet
   B) look away
   C) laugh out loud
   D) speak up
21. People living and working together in one place are a ___
   A) classroom
   B) family name
   C) community
   D) market day
22. A busy place with many buildings and big markets is a ___
   A) town
   B) village
   C) bush
   D) farm
23. A village sends ___ to the town
   A) street lights
   B) baskets of food
   C) tall buildings
   D) long new buses
24. A good community shares, helps and works ___
   A) apart later
   B) quietly alone
   C) only on Sunday
   D) together
25. A leader is a person who ___ people
   A) hides from
   B) runs away from
   C) guides and serves
   D) looks down on
26. A traditional leader in our land is a ___
   A) chief
   B) pupil
   C) seller
   D) driver
27. One quality of a good leader is being a good ___
   A) eater
   B) listener
   C) sleeper
   D) runner
28. Nigeria has ___ tongues and cultures
   A) two
   B) no
   C) one
   D) many
29. Amala and efo, garri and pounded yam are ___ of our people
   A) foods
   B) tools
   C) games
   D) songs
30. A child in another country ___
   A) has no tongue at all
   B) eats nothing at all
   C) goes to school and plays
   D) stays from school
