# EXAMINATION PRACTICE — BASIC SCIENCE (Nursery 2 · First Term)

**Time:** 1 hour · **Total: 50 marks**

## Section A — Objective (30 marks)
Choose the correct option and ring the letter.

1. Our body has a head, a neck, a trunk, and the ___
   A) arms and legs
   B) ears and nose
   C) tail and wings
   D) knees and sandals
2. The part that joins the head to the body is the ___
   A) ankle
   B) chest
   C) neck
   D) waist
3. We use our ___ to hear sounds
   A) nose
   B) elbows
   C) toes
   D) ears
4. How many fingers do we have on both hands?
   A) ten
   B) five
   C) eight
   D) twelve
5. The part of the trunk at the back of us is the ___
   A) waist
   B) chin
   C) back
   D) chest
6. Which of these is found on the arm?
   A) knee
   B) wrist
   C) hip
   D) ankle
7. We brush our teeth ___ a day
   A) five times
   B) once
   C) three times
   D) twice
8. Before eating we must ___
   A) wash our hands
   B) sleep on the mat
   C) run about the room
   D) watch the television
9. Our nails should be kept ___
   A) painted with chalk
   B) short and clean
   C) wet with water
   D) long and full of dirt
10. Which one do we use on our hair?
   A) a spoon
   B) a plaster
   C) a comb
   D) a cup
11. Every day we should wear ___ clothes
   A) torn
   B) wet
   C) someone else's
   D) clean
12. For the body to be strong we must ___
   A) never play outside at all
   B) eat good food and sleep well
   C) eat only sweets every day
   D) drink only cold soft drinks
13. First aid is the ___ help given to a hurt person
   A) first
   B) slowest
   C) secret
   D) last
14. Which of these is kept in the first aid box?
   A) cutlass
   B) broom
   C) plaster
   D) chalk
15. Which one is NOT a first aid box item?
   A) a roll of cotton wool
   B) a clean bandage
   C) a small thermometer
   D) chewing stick
16. A thermometer is used to ___
   A) tell if the body is hot
   B) count the pupils in class
   C) wash the school chairs
   D) cut the bandage in two
17. A bandage is used to ___
   A) carry books to the office
   B) brush the school gate
   C) wrap up a wound
   D) cook the family food
18. Who should use the things in the first aid box?
   A) a baby
   B) a grown-up
   C) the class goat
   D) any pupil
19. A wound is an injury that ___
   A) breaks the skin
   B) paints the skin
   C) cleans the skin
   D) warms the skin
20. A blue-black mark from bumping, with the skin not open, is a ___
   A) cut
   B) burn
   C) plaster
   D) bruise
21. Which kind of wound comes from a thorn or a nail going deep into the skin?
   A) A soft round bump
   B) Puncture wound
   C) A clean dry graze
   D) A small blue bruise
22. Which one can cause a burn?
   A) a cold fan
   B) a soft pillow
   C) a hot pot
   D) a school bag
23. What is the first thing to do before caring for a wound?
   A) run and shout
   B) put mud on it
   C) blow the dust in
   D) wash the hands
24. We clean a small wound with ___
   A) clean water
   B) chalk dust from the board
   C) some kerosene
   D) a cup of dirty water
25. On a healing wound there is a scab. We must ___
   A) pick it every day
   B) leave it alone
   C) rub sand on it
   D) cut it with a blade
26. Sickness means the body ___
   A) is growing too fast
   B) is working quite well
   C) is not working properly
   D) is always clean and neat
27. Which one is a sign of sickness?
   A) high temperature
   B) a brand new school shoe
   C) very clean nails
   D) a loud happy laugh
28. Malaria is brought to us by the bite of a ___
   A) bird
   B) goat
   C) rat
   D) mosquito
29. A sick person should drink plenty of ___
   A) kerosene
   B) soda
   C) water
   D) palm oil
30. The top layer of the earth where plants grow is called ___
   A) rock
   B) soil
   C) sand only
   D) water

## Section B — Sub-objective (10 marks)
Answer briefly.

31. Name the five big parts of the body.
32. Mention four parts found on the head.
33. State three daily practices that keep the body clean.
34. Name four things inside a first aid box.
35. Give the use of each: (a) plaster (b) thermometer (c) bandage.
36. Mention three things that can cause wounds at home.
37. Write the steps for caring for a small wound in order.
38. Name four signs (symptoms) of sickness.
39. State three ways of preventing sickness.
40. Name the types of soil and mark the one best for plants.

## Section C — Theory (10 marks)
Answer in full sentences.

41. Name four parts of your body and say what each one does. (2 marks)
42. Why must we keep our body clean? Give three reasons. (2 marks)
43. Your friend falls in the compound and his knee is bleeding. What should you do? (2 marks)
44. What is sickness? Name four causes and three ways of preventing it. (2 marks)
45. What are rocks? Mention three uses of rocks and three types of soil. (2 marks)
