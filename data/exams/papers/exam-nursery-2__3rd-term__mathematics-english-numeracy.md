**Time:** 1 hour · **Total: 54 marks**

## Section A — Objective (30 marks)
Choose the correct option and ring the letter.

   C) quarter

   D) corners

   D) five

   D) long

## Section C — Theory (10 marks)
Answer in full sentences.

41. Shade half of a square and say how many equal parts you made. (2 marks)
42. Name three things you measured with your hand span and say how many spans each one was. (3 marks)
43. Write two sentences on one big thing that is light and one small thing that is heavy. (4 marks)
44. Say what the clock looks like at 7 o'clock and what you do at that time. (3 marks)
45. Ask five friends their favourite colour and record the answers with strokes. (2 marks)
