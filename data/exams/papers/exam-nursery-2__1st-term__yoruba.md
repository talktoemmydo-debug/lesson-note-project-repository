# EXAMINATION PRACTICE — YORUBA (Nursery 2 · 1st Term)

**Time:** 1 hour · **Total: 50 marks**

## Section A — Objective (30 marks)
Choose the correct option and ring the letter.

1. "Yàrá ìkáwé" in English is the ___
   A) sleeping room
   B) dining room
   C) reading room
   D) classroom
2. The Yoruba name for the table is ___
   A) tébèèlì
   B) ẹlẹ́dọ̀tí
   C) ìgáòkó
   D) pátákó
3. The board in the class is called ___
   A) tébèèlì
   B) ìwé
   C) pátákó
   D) bẹ́ẹ̀dì
4. "Ẹlẹ́dọ̀tí" is the thing we use to ___
   A) clean the floor
   B) write a letter
   C) cut a page
   D) rub out a mistake
5. "Good morning" in Yoruba is ___
   A) Ẹ̀ káàárọ̀
   B) Ẹ̀ káàlẹ̀
   C) Ẹ̀ máàdọ́rùn
   D) Ẹ̀ káàṣánṣá
6. "Good afternoon" is said as ___
   A) Ọ́ dàbọ̀
   B) Ẹ̀ káàṣánṣá
   C) Ẹ ṣé
   D) Ẹ̀ káàárọ̀
7. We greet an elder at night with ___
   A) Ẹ̀ káàárọ̀
   B) Àlàáfíà ni
   C) Ọ́ nàṣẹ́
   D) Ẹ̀ máàdọ́rùn
8. A boy greets an elder by ___
   A) ìkúnlẹ̀ (kneeling)
   B) clapping the hands twice
   C) ìdòbì (prostration)
   D) standing and waving
9. A girl greets an elder by ___
   A) ìkúnlẹ̀ (kneeling)
   B) ìdòbì (prostration)
   C) jumping once
   D) running to the door
10. The reply to "Ọ́ dàbọ̀" is ___
   A) "Ààbọ̀ ni ọla"
   B) "Ọ́ dábọ̀, ààbọ̀"
   C) "Bákan náà"
   D) "Ẹ ṣé gan-an"
11. Numbers: the Yoruba word for 5 is ___
   A) ẹ̀fà
   B) ẹ̀jọ
   C) ẹ̀sán
   D) àárún
12. "Ẹ̀wá" is the number ___
   A) nine
   B) eight
   C) ten
   D) four
13. Counting backwards from ẹ̀wá, the next word is ___
   A) àárún
   B) ẹ̀sán
   C) ọ̀kan
   D) ẹ̀ta
14. "Mẹ́ta ni mo ní" means ___
   A) I have three
   B) I am three years old
   C) I want three
   D) I saw three
15. "Ìwà lẹ́wàá" teaches that ___
   A) money is the greatest wealth
   B) a fine face is everything
   C) speed wins the race
   D) good character is the true beauty
16. An orin ìkìlọ̀ is a song that ___
   A) welcomes a newborn baby
   B) counts the days of the week
   C) warns children away from bad things
   D) praises a chief at his gate
17. The Yoruba alphabet has letters ___
   A) twenty-five in all: 18 consonants and 7 vowels
   B) twenty-four in all: 17 consonants and 7 vowels
   C) thirty letters in all
   D) twenty-six in all: 20 consonants and 6 vowels
18. The seven vowels of Yoruba are ___
   A) a, e, i, o, u, w, y
   B) a, e, ẹ, i, o, ọ, u
   C) a, b, c, d, e, f, g
   D) e, ẹ, i, o, ọ, u, n
19. Which letter is NOT in the Yoruba alphabet?
   A) d
   B) b
   C) ń
   D) c
20. The two letters that are written as pairs are ___
   A) gb and ṣ
   B) ch and ph
   C) kp and q
   D) sh and th
21. "Ọjọ́ Àìkú" is the day we call ___
   A) Wednesday
   B) Sunday
   C) Saturday
   D) Friday
22. Tuesday in Yoruba is ___
   A) Ọjọ́ Bọ̀ṣẹ̀
   B) Ọjọ́ Ajé
   C) Ọjọ́ Ìṣẹ́gun
   D) Ọjọ́ Ẹtì
23. The seven days of the week end with ___
   A) Ọjọ́ Àbámẹ́ta
   B) Ọjọ́-rúkú-ọ̀jọ́
   C) Ọjọ́ Ìṣẹ́gun
   D) Ọjọ́ Àìkú
24. The first eight letters of the Yoruba alphabet, in order, are ___
   A) b, d, f, g, gb, h, j, k
   B) a, b, c, d, e, f, g, h
   C) a, e, ẹ, i, o, ọ, u, b
   D) a, b, d, e, ẹ, f, g, gb
25. Action words: "jẹ" means ___
   A) sleep
   B) eat
   C) come
   D) drink
26. Do not mix them up: "mu" is used for ___
   A) water only
   B) food
   C) drink
   D) book
27. "Kí ni o ń ṣe?" is asked when we want to know
   A) what somebody is doing
   B) how old somebody is
   C) where somebody lives
   D) who somebody greeted
28. The sentence pattern we learnt is ___
   A) Mo + thing + action
   B) Action + Mo + thing
   C) Thing + action + Mo
   D) Mo + action + thing
29. "Ọ́ nàṣẹ́" is a greeting for a person who is ___
   A) at rest
   B) at school
   C) at work
   D) just born
30. The Yoruba word for a farmer is ___
   A) ọ̀dẹ̀
   B) agbẹ̀
   C) akọ́
   D) alagbe

## Section B — Sub-objective (10 marks)
Answer briefly.

31. Name five things in the classroom in Yoruba.
32. Say the three greetings for morning, afternoon and evening.
33. Mention four ways we greet at the right time or place.
34. Count from ọ̀kan to ẹ̀wá.
35. State three marks of a child of good character.
36. Name the seven vowels of the Yoruba alphabet.
37. Mention the seven days of the week in Yoruba.
38. Give four action words and their meanings.
39. State the duties of the father and the elder sibling.
40. Name two moonlight games and one rule of safety.

## Section C — Theory (10 marks)
Answer in full sentences.

41. Write the names of six things in your classroom in Yoruba and say what each is used for (2 marks)
42. Describe how you greet an elder, a friend and a person at work, and give the reply to each (2 marks)
43. Write on "Ìwà lẹ́wàá": say what it teaches and give three things a child of good character does (2 marks)
44. Name the parts of the body or of the house you keep clean and say how you clean them (2 marks)
45. Write two sentences with action words and explain the meaning of the ẹ̀wì "Iṣẹ́, iṣẹ́ ni ọ̀rẹ́ ẹni" (2 marks)
