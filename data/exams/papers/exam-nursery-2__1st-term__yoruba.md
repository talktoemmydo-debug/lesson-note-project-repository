# EXAMINATION PRACTICE — YORUBA (Nursery 2 · 1st Term)

**Time:** 1 hour · **Total: 50 marks**

## Section A — Objective (30 marks)
Choose the correct option and ring the letter.

1. "Yàrá ìkáwé" in English is the ___
   A) sleeping room
   B) dining room
   C) reading room
   D) classroom
2. The Yoruba name for the table is ___
   A) tébèèlì
   B) ẹlẹ́dọ̀tí
   C) ìgáòkó
   D) pátákó
3. The board in the class is called ___
   A) tébèèlì
   B) ìwé
   C) pátákó
   D) bẹ́ẹ̀dì
4. When I point at a thing and name it, I say ___
   A) "Ẹ́ ṣé o"
   B) "Kí ni orúkọ rẹ?"
   C) "Báwo ni?"
   D) "Èyí ni ______"
5. "Good morning" in Yoruba is ___
   A) Ẹ̀ káàárọ̀
   B) Ẹ̀ káàlẹ̀
   C) Ẹ̀ máàdọ́rùn
   D) Ẹ̀ káàṣánṣá
6. "Good afternoon" is said as ___
   A) Ọ́ dàbọ̀
   B) Ẹ̀ káàṣánṣá
   C) Ẹ ṣé
   D) Ẹ̀ káàárọ̀
7. A boy greets an elder by ___
   A) ìkúnlẹ̀ (kneeling)
   B) clapping the hands twice
   C) standing and waving
   D) ìdòbì (prostration)
8. A girl greets an elder by ___
   A) ìdòbì (prostration)
   B) running to the door
   C) ìkúnlẹ̀ (kneeling)
   D) jumping once
9. When a friend comes back home we say ___
   A) Ọ́ dàbọ̀
   B) Ẹ̀ káàlẹ̀
   C) Ẹ̀ jẹun ni
   D) Ọ́ nàṣẹ́
10. "Thank you" in Yoruba is ___
   A) Kí ni o ń ṣe
   B) Ẹ ṣé
   C) Ì jọ̀ọ́ ẹ̀
   D) Ẹ̀ jọ̀ọ́
11. Numbers: the Yoruba word for 5 is ___
   A) ẹ̀fà
   B) ẹ̀jọ
   C) ẹ̀sán
   D) àárún
12. "Ẹ̀wá" is the number ___
   A) nine
   B) eight
   C) ten
   D) four
13. "Meloo ni o ní?" is a question that means
   A) where do you live
   B) how many do you have
   C) when did you come
   D) what is your name
14. "Mẹ́ta ni mo ní" means ___
   A) I have three
   B) I am three years old
   C) I want three
   D) I saw three
15. "Ìwà lẹ́wàá" teaches that ___
   A) money is the greatest wealth
   B) a fine face is everything
   C) speed wins the race
   D) good character is the true beauty
16. One rule in the warning song is ___
   A) do not sleep early, so that you will work
   B) do not run, so that you will not fall
   C) do not be idle, so that your money will not sleep
   D) do not sing, so that you will learn
17. The Yoruba alphabet has letters ___
   A) twenty-five in all: 18 consonants and 7 vowels
   B) twenty-four in all: 17 consonants and 7 vowels
   C) thirty letters in all
   D) twenty-six in all: 20 consonants and 6 vowels
18. The seven vowels of Yoruba are ___
   A) a, e, i, o, u, w, y
   B) a, e, ẹ, i, o, ọ, u
   C) a, b, c, d, e, f, g
   D) e, ẹ, i, o, ọ, u, n
19. The two letters that are written as pairs are ___
   A) sh and th
   B) kp and q
   C) ch and ph
   D) gb and ṣ
20. The dot under ẹ and ọ ___
   A) changes the sound of the letter
   B) shows a long pause
   C) makes the letter silent
   D) doubles the letter
21. "Ọjọ́ Àìkú" is the day we call ___
   A) Wednesday
   B) Sunday
   C) Saturday
   D) Friday
22. The seven days of the week end with ___
   A) Ọjọ́-rúkú-ọ̀jọ́
   B) Ọjọ́ Ìṣẹ́gun
   C) Ọjọ́ Àbámẹ́ta
   D) Ọjọ́ Àìkú
23. The first eight letters of the Yoruba alphabet, in order, are ___
   A) a, b, d, e, ẹ, f, g, gb
   B) a, b, c, d, e, f, g, h
   C) b, d, f, g, gb, h, j, k
   D) a, e, ẹ, i, o, ọ, u, b
24. The three tones that mark the voice are ___
   A) high only, with no other tone
   B) dài, gún and kúkú
   C) è, é and ẹ
   D) à (low), á (high) and ā (middle)
25. Do not mix them up: "mu" is used for ___
   A) book
   B) drink
   C) water only
   D) food
26. "Kí ni o ń ṣe?" is asked when we want to know
   A) who somebody greeted
   B) where somebody lives
   C) what somebody is doing
   D) how old somebody is
27. The sentence pattern we learnt is ___
   A) Mo + action + thing
   B) Action + Mo + thing
   C) Thing + action + Mo
   D) Mo + thing + action
28. "Ọ́ nàṣẹ́" is a greeting for a person who is ___
   A) just born
   B) at school
   C) at rest
   D) at work
29. The Yoruba word for a farmer is ___
   A) ọ̀dẹ̀
   B) akọ́
   C) agbẹ̀
   D) alagbe
30. A ẹ̀wì is different from orin because it is ___
   A) danced, not spoken
   B) spoken, not sung
   C) written, not read
   D) sung twice, never once

## Section B — Sub-objective (10 marks)
Answer briefly.

31. Name five things in the classroom in Yoruba.
32. Say the three greetings for morning, afternoon and evening.
33. Mention four ways we greet at the right time or place.
34. Count from ọ̀kan to ẹ̀wá.
35. State three marks of a child of good character.
36. Name the seven vowels of the Yoruba alphabet.
37. Mention the seven days of the week in Yoruba.
38. Give four action words and their meanings.
39. State the duties of the father and the elder sibling.
40. Name two moonlight games and one rule of safety.

## Section C — Theory (10 marks)
Answer in full sentences.

41. Write the names of six things in your classroom in Yoruba and say what each is used for (2 marks)
42. Describe how you greet an elder, a friend and a person at work, and give the reply to each (2 marks)
43. Write on "Ìwà lẹ́wàá": say what it teaches and give three things a child of good character does (2 marks)
44. Name the parts of the body or of the house you keep clean and say how you clean them (2 marks)
45. Write two sentences with action words and explain the meaning of the ẹ̀wì "Iṣẹ́, iṣẹ́ ni ọ̀rẹ́ ẹni" (2 marks)
