# EXAMINATION PRACTICE — PHYSICAL AND HEALTH EDUCATION (PHE) (Nursery 2 · 2nd Term)

**Time:** 1 hour · **Total: 50 marks**

## Section A — Objective (30 marks)
Choose the correct option and ring the letter.

1. Feelings of the heart such as joy, fear and anger are called ___
   A) muscles
   B) emotions
   C) games
   D) rules
2. The feeling of being sure that you can do a thing is called ___
   A) sleep
   B) anger
   C) confidence
   D) pride only
3. When we lose a game and do not fight or cry wildly, we show good ___
   A) sportsmanship
   B) noise
   C) dress
   D) speed
4. Nervousness before a race is best beaten by ___
   A) skipping breakfast
   B) shouting at friends
   C) running away from it
   D) practising the race more
5. A hard feeling some children get when they lose is ___
   A) patience
   B) excitement
   C) disappointment
   D) contentment
6. Working together with your team toward one goal is called ___
   A) sleeping
   B) teamwork
   C) hiding
   D) counting
7. Welcoming a new child who does not know the rules of the game is ___
   A) hospitality
   B) fear
   C) pride
   D) anger
8. Accepting another child's mistake without laughing at him is ___
   A) cheating
   B) speed
   C) noise
   D) tolerance
9. Playing by the rules and taking what the one who judges says is ___
   A) shouting
   B) fairness
   C) luck
   D) pride
10. Sometimes leading and sometimes following in a game teaches us ___
   A) singing
   B) reading
   C) leadership
   D) drawing
11. Marching is best described as ___
   A) organised walking in rhythm and formation
   B) running about in any way you like
   C) hopping on only one leg around
   D) lying down quietly on the mat
12. The foot that starts when we march forward is the ___
   A) back foot
   B) left foot
   C) right foot
   D) both at once
13. The command that makes everybody stand straight and keep quiet is ___
   A) Play
   B) Halt
   C) Attention
   D) Rest
14. Keeping an arm's length between us in the line is done to ___
   A) look taller
   B) reach the gate first
   C) hear the drum
   D) avoid kicking another child
15. Marching is good for the body because it develops ___
   A) a loud and a shouting voice
   B) posture, discipline and coordination
   C) having a very big and round belly
   D) strong nails on both of the hands
16. Swinging and twisting the body before running are done to ___
   A) waste some time
   B) cool the class down
   C) warm the muscles first
   D) finish the lesson early
17. A jump should be landed with the knees ___
   A) lifted high up to the chest
   B) crossed behind the other leg
   C) straight and locked at all times
   D) bent, so that the landing is soft
18. Bending sideways with one arm over the head stretches the ___
   A) waist and the sides of the trunk
   B) hair and the top of the head
   C) toes and the nails only
   D) ears and the small fingers
19. Loud, jerky movements in exercise are dangerous because they ___
   A) make us run much more faster
   B) keep the whole class quite cool
   C) can pull a muscle or hurt a joint
   D) help us to breathe better
20. A race run on a marked path is called a ___
   A) field day
   B) marching drill
   C) board game
   D) track event
21. After crossing the finishing line we should ___
   A) keep going a little and then walk
   B) sit right down there at once
   C) lie flat down on the track
   D) turn and run back quickly
22. The first thing to do with a machine that gives smoke is to ___
   A) blow the smoke away with hands
   B) leave it and tell the one in charge
   C) pull the cable out very hard
   D) pour some cold water on it
23. Running with a partner side by side to the line is called ___
   A) relay singing
   B) hopping
   C) marching
   D) running in pairs
24. Climbing is safest when ___
   A) you climb fast with your eyes shut
   B) two children climb one above the other
   C) three points stay on the ladder while one moves
   D) you carry a bag in your hands
25. Looking up before climbing to choose which rung to use is a way of ___
   A) solving the problem
   B) warming the feet
   C) resting the arms
   D) cooling the head
26. Posture means the way we ___
   A) write our names in the book
   B) hold our body when we sit or stand
   C) choose the shoes for the day
   D) eat our food in the morning
27. While sitting to write, the feet should be ___
   A) up on the chair
   B) hanging in the air
   C) crossed behind
   D) flat on the floor
28. The position we should avoid while sleeping, because it twists the neck, is lying ___
   A) on the tummy
   B) flat on the back
   C) on the side
   D) half sitting up
29. A school bag is best carried on ___
   A) one shoulder only
   B) both shoulders
   C) the head always
   D) the fingers
30. Indoor games are mostly good for training the ___
   A) legs only
   B) lungs only
   C) mind and patience
   D) voice

## Section B — Sub-objective (10 marks)

31. Mention three good feelings that come from playing games.

34. Give four marching commands we use in class.
35. Mention four kinds of body exercise learnt this term.
36. Name four safety rules for climbing.
37. Mention two kinds of start in a short race and the rule about the lane.
38. State four rules for good sitting and standing posture.
39. Name five indoor games we can play in a room.
40. Give two reasons for balancing indoor games with outdoor play.

## Section C — Theory (10 marks)
Answer in full sentences.

41. Write on the feelings that come from games and how a child can manage them. (2 marks)
42. Explain what marching is and give four of its benefits to a pupil. (2 marks)
43. Describe how you swing, twist, jump and bend in exercise, and say why we warm up first. (2 marks)
44. Write on climbing: how to do it safely and what it does for the body. (2 marks)
45. What is good posture? Explain how to sit, stand, read and sleep well. (2 marks)
