# EXAMINATION PRACTICE — PREVOCATIONAL STUDIES (Nursery 2 · 3rd Term)

**Time:** 1 hour · **Total: 54 marks**

## Section A — Objective (30 marks)
Choose the correct option and ring the letter.

1. A tool used to pull dry leaves together and smooth soil is a ___
   A) hoe
   B) spade
   C) rake
   D) basket
2. The tool with a flat wide blade for digging and lifting soil is a ___
   A) rake
   B) spade
   C) drum
   D) cup
3. A cutlass or a hoe is carried by ___
   A) anybody who likes
   B) the smallest child
   C) a grown-up at work
   D) a child alone
4. After work a tool is cleaned, dried and ___
   A) hung on the rack
   B) buried in soil
   C) put in the water
   D) left in the grass
5. Safety means keeping people from being ___
   A) paid
   B) counted
   C) hurt
   D) called
6. Before we work on the ground we ___
   A) sit down and sing on it
   B) look for stones and holes
   C) run all over it first
   D) pour water on it first
7. A tool is carried with its sharp end ___
   A) towards a friend
   B) pointing down
   C) in a pocket
   D) up in the air
8. A wasps' nest near the fence is ___
   A) reported to a grown-up
   B) hidden under a leaf
   C) kicked with a hoe
   D) waited for till next year
9. A farm where a worker hurts his hand will ___
   A) plant twice
   B) earn more money
   C) lose days of work
   D) rest and prosper
10. Being quick and careless is the ___ way to do a work
   A) cleanest
   B) slowest
   C) quietest
   D) fastest
11. A child who works carefully is ___
   A) given the biggest hoe
   B) forgotten at once
   C) trusted with more work
   D) sent to bed
12. A hoe swings and hurts the person standing ___
   A) on the roof
   B) in the house
   C) far away
   D) behind
13. A cracked handle is dangerous because it may ___
   A) work faster
   B) shine brightly
   C) slip off while swinging
   D) stay clean longer
14. A hole in the ground we cannot fill today is ___
   A) jumped by everybody
   B) filled with leaves
   C) left for the goats
   D) marked with stones
15. A chipped blade cuts badly because it ___
   A) costs more
   B) is heavier
   C) is sharper
   D) slips more
16. The three steps for a small cut are clean water, a clean cloth and ___
   A) a leaf pressed in
   B) dust from the floor
   C) a grown-up told
   D) the finger hidden
17. The room where food is kept, washed and cooked is the ___
   A) kitchen
   B) shop
   C) shed
   D) farm
18. A pot handle on the fire is turned ___ so a sleeve will not catch it
   A) out
   B) in
   C) up
   D) down
19. Boiling, cutting and lifting a hot pot are jobs for ___
   A) the visitor
   B) a class friend
   C) a grown-up
   D) the smallest child
20. A wet floor in the kitchen is wiped ___
   A) later
   B) never
   C) with oil
   D) at once
21. Medicine for insects is kept shut, high and ___
   A) poured in a drink bottle
   B) marked
   C) given to a child
   D) left on the floor
22. Two tools must never be swung ___ a person
   A) over
   B) near the ground
   C) on a rack
   D) in a store
23. A tool is passed to a friend by the ___
   A) rope
   B) blade
   C) handle
   D) teeth
24. Protective gear is worn ___ the work begins
   A) if it rains
   B) when it is over
   C) before
   D) long after
25. Boots keep the ___ safe at work
   A) feet
   B) eyes
   C) head
   D) hands
26. A glove with a ___ lets in what the glove was for
   A) hole
   B) name
   C) colour
   D) size
27. A store for tools must be dry and ___ the ground
   A) off
   B) beside
   C) on top of
   D) above
28. A broken tool is reported and then ___
   A) thrown in the bush
   B) not used again
   C) hidden away
   D) used carefully
29. A small burn is first held under ___ water
   A) hot soapy
   B) muddy
   C) salt
   D) cool clean
30. A person with a nosebleed is made to lean ___
   A) a little forward
   B) right back
   C) down on the chest
   D) to one side

## Section B — Sub-objective (10 marks)
Answer briefly.

31. Name two tools that carry things instead of cutting

33. Mention three rules we keep when tools are about
34. Name two things worn to keep the hands and the feet safe
35. Say two dangers found in a kitchen
36. Mention two jobs in the kitchen that belong to a grown-up
37. Name the place where tools are kept and one thing it keeps out
38. Say three steps for a small cut
39. Mention two things that must be kept shut, high and apart from drink
40. Name two dangers that make a foot fall in the path

## Section C — Theory (10 marks)
Answer in full sentences.

41. Write two sentences on a tool you have held and what you did with it. (2 marks)
42. Mention four things worn to keep the body safe at work. (3 marks)
43. Say why a child must wait for a grown-up before kitchen work is done. (4 marks)
44. Give three rules you keep when tools are being used near you. (3 marks)
45. Write two sentences on what you do first when you see a broken tool. (2 marks)
