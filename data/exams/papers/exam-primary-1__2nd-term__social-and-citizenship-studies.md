# EXAMINATION PRACTICE — SOCIAL AND CITIZENSHIP STUDIES (Primary 1 · 2nd Term)

**Time:** 1 hour · **Total: 50 marks**

## Section A — Objective (30 marks)
Choose the correct option and ring the letter.

1. A person who lives near me is my ___
   A) neighbour
   B) tool
   C) pet
   D) stranger
2. A friend is someone I like and ___ with
   A) lose
   B) hide
   C) fight
   D) play
3. Friends and neighbours ___ one another
   A) hate
   B) hit
   C) help
   D) hide
4. We ___ our neighbours
   A) forget
   B) greet
   C) fear
   D) chase
5. The bigger family with uncles and aunts is the ___ family
   A) extended
   B) class
   C) school
   D) small
6. My uncle's children are my ___
   A) neighbours
   B) brothers
   C) parents
   D) cousins
7. My grandparents are my parents' ___
   A) friends
   B) parents
   C) cousins
   D) children
8. We greet and respect our ___
   A) pets
   B) toys
   C) elders
   D) shoes
9. A person who guides others is a ___
   A) stranger
   B) baby
   C) thief
   D) leader
10. The ___ leads the school
   A) head teacher
   B) class captain
   C) doctor
   D) farmer
11. The head of a town is the traditional ___
   A) trader
   B) driver
   C) ruler
   D) pupil
12. Leaders keep peace and ___
   A) fight
   B) order
   C) dust
   D) noise
13. My country is ___
   A) Ghana
   B) Egypt
   C) Kenya
   D) Nigeria
14. Nigeria is in ___ Africa
   A) West
   B) South
   C) East
   D) North
15. The capital of Nigeria is ___
   A) Ibadan
   B) Lagos
   C) Abuja
   D) Kano
16. The Nigerian flag is green, white and ___
   A) red
   B) green
   C) black
   D) blue
17. Something I ought to have is a ___
   A) game
   B) song
   C) toy
   D) right
18. Something I ought to do is a ___
   A) duty
   B) prize
   C) treat
   D) gift
19. A child has the right to ___ and go to school
   A) steal
   B) hide
   C) learn
   D) fight
20. My duty is to ___ my parents and teachers
   A) chase
   B) forget
   C) ignore
   D) obey
21. Honesty means telling the ___
   A) story
   B) truth
   C) secret
   D) lie

   A) lie
   B) help
   C) greet
   D) share
23. An honest person does not ___
   A) sing

   A) his
   B) theirs
   C) ours
   D) hers

   A) obedience
   B) anger
   C) play
   D) noise

   A) hiding
   B) respect
   C) fighting
   D) losing
27. I obey my parents, teachers and ___
   A) thieves
   B) strangers
   C) nobody
   D) leaders

   A) lost

   A) damage
   B) clean

   D) paint

   A) dark
   B) clean
   C) loud
   D) dirty
