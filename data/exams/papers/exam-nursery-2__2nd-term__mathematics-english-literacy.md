# EXAMINATION PRACTICE — MATHEMATICS & ENGLISH · LITERACY (LETTER WORK) (Nursery 2 · 2nd Term)

**Time:** 1 hour · **Total: 50 marks**

## Section A — Objective (30 marks)
Choose the correct option and ring the letter.

1. A CVC word is made of ___
   A) two vowels and a consonant
   B) consonant, vowel, consonant
   C) three consonants
   D) five letters
2. The word made by the sounds /b/ /æ/ /t/ is ___
   A) bat
   B) but
   C) boat
   D) bit
3. Pen, hen and ten all belong to the word family ___
   A) -un
   B) -an
   C) -en
   D) -in
4. In the word "dog", the middle sound is a ___
   A) full stop
   B) vowel
   C) consonant cluster
   D) digraph
5. The last two letters in "hand" are ___
   A) nd
   B) nt
   C) st
   D) mp
6. Bent, sent and went all end in ___
   A) -ft
   B) -st
   C) -nd
   D) -nt
7. Which word belongs to the -st family?
   A) mend
   B) mint
   C) nest
   D) lamp
8. The words jump, lamp and camp all end in ___
   A) -mp
   B) -nd
   C) -ft
   D) -nk
9. Which word is NOT in the -nk family?
   A) pink
   B) bump
   C) sink
   D) bank
10. A CCVC word begins with ___
   A) three vowels
   B) two vowels
   C) a full stop
   D) two consonants
11. The two sounds at the start of "flag" are ___
   A) f and r
   B) a and g
   C) f and l
   D) l and a
12. Crab, crop and crown all begin with the blend ___
   A) br
   B) cr
   C) gr
   D) cl
13. Which word begins with the blend dr?
   A) play
   B) tray
   C) grip
   D) drum
14. The blend at the start of "blue" is ___
   A) bl
   B) cl
   C) br
   D) fl
15. Words that rhyme are words that ___
   A) are both long
   B) have capitals
   C) sound the same at the end
   D) begin the same way
16. The word that rhymes with "pen" is ___
   A) pit
   B) ten
   C) pin
   D) pan
17. A sight word should be read by ___
   A) counting its lines
   B) copying a picture
   C) sounding out every letter
   D) knowing it at once
18. Which sentence uses the right word? "The pen is ____ the bag."
   A) in
   B) on
   C) at
   D) and
19. "The dog ____ the cat ran" — the word that joins the two is ___
   A) is
   B) and
   C) my
   D) the
20. "I can run." Which two words in it are sight words?
   A) can only
   B) run and can
   C) I and can
   D) none at all
21. A sentence must always begin with a ___
   A) capital letter
   B) small letter
   C) comma
   D) full stop
22. A written sentence ends with a ___
   A) dash
   B) colon
   C) question mark only
   D) full stop
23. "The cat sat on the mat" tells us ___
   A) just three letters in all
   B) one whole thing about the cat
   C) only a single small sound
   D) the name of a place
24. Words like cat, hat and sat are grouped because they share the ___
   A) same ending sound
   B) same length of line
   C) same first letter
   D) same picture
25. The sentence "i am a boy" is wrong because it has no ___
   A) full stop at the end
   B) space between words
   C) sight word
   D) capital letter at the start
26. Blend the sounds /s/ /l/ /æ/ /p/ : the word is ___
   A) sapl
   B) salp
   C) slap
   D) slape
27. The word "gift" belongs to the family ending in ___
   A) -ft
   B) -mp
   C) -st
   D) -nd
28. "Stop, flop and pop" all end in ___
   A) -up
   B) -op
   C) -ap

29. Which of these is a sight word we learnt this term?
   A) pencil case

   A) lathe

## Section B — Sub-objective (10 marks)
Answer briefly.

31. Write one line of words for each family: -at, -og and -un.
32. Say the four sounds in "hand" and write the word.
33. Write two words ending in -nt and two ending in -st.
34. Sort these into -mp, -nk and -ft: lamp, pink, gift, camp, sink, soft.
35. Name five sight words from Weeks 5 and 6.
36. Write the four l-blends and the four r-blends we practised.
37. Write one sentence that begins with "The" and ends with a full stop.
38. Put the words in order: is / The / on / mat / cat / the — write the first three words and how many there are.
39. Write three words that rhyme with "ball" and three that rhyme with "lake".
40. Read and spell: clap, brush, jump, nest.

## Section C — Theory (10 marks)
Answer in full sentences.

41. What is blending? Blend and write four CVC words from the -at family. (2 marks)
42. Explain a CVCC word and give six words ending in -nd, -nt or -st. (2 marks)
43. What is a blend? Write five CCVC words and say the two sounds at their start. (2 marks)
44. Write the ten sight words of this term and use four of them in sentences. (2 marks)
45. What is a sentence? Write five rules you keep when you write one, and give an example. (2 marks)
