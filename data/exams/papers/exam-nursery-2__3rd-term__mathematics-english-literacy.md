# EXAMINATION PRACTICE — MATHEMATICS & ENGLISH · LITERACY (LETTER WORK) (Nursery 2 · 3rd Term)

**Time:** 1 hour · **Total: 54 marks**

## Section A — Objective (30 marks)
Choose the correct option and ring the letter.

1. A word we know at once, without spelling it out, is a ____ word
   A) new
   B) long
   C) sight
   D) hard
2. A line that tells a thing ends with a ____
   A) comma
   B) full stop
   C) exclamation mark
   D) question mark
3. Which of these words do we meet most in English?
   A) is
   B) a
   C) and
   D) the
4. A sentence must begin with a ____ letter
   A) big
   B) round
   C) small
   D) quiet
5. The word "stamp" has ____ letters
   A) three
   B) five
   C) four
   D) six
6. In "sheep", the two letters that make one sound together are ____
   A) ep
   B) he
   C) sh
   D) ee
7. Which of these words begins with two consonants run together?
   A) ant
   B) pen
   C) cup
   D) brush
8. To write a long word, we say it, count the ____ , then write one letter for each
   A) pages
   B) sounds
   C) books
   D) lines
9. Two words joined to make one word are called a ____ word
   A) compound
   B) sight
   C) long
   D) small
10. "Sun + set" joined makes the word ____
   A) cupcake
   B) outside
   C) sunset
   D) rainbow
11. A "bedroom" is the place we ____
   A) play in
   B) cook in
   C) wash in
   D) sleep in
12. Which two words make "notebook"?
   A) book + look
   B) note + book
   C) boat + book
   D) node + book
13. The people or animals a story is about are its ____
   A) middle
   B) setting
   C) characters
   D) page
14. "Where and when a story happens" is called its ____
   A) setting
   B) title
   C) character
   D) ending
15. In our hen story, what happened first?
   A) She eats the corn.
   B) She runs home.
   C) The rain comes.
   D) The hen goes out.
16. A story has a beginning, a middle and a(n) ____
   A) line
   B) map
   C) end
   D) age
17. A describing word tells us what a thing is ____
   A) made
   B) like
   C) named
   D) worth
18. In "the tall tree", the describing word is ____
   A) tall
   B) tree
   C) the
   D) one
19. Which of these is the opposite of "big"?
   A) thin
   B) tall
   C) long
   D) small
20. For a snail we use the word ____
   A) fast
   B) slow
   C) happy
   D) hot
21. An action word tells us what somebody ____
   A) has
   B) owns
   C) does
   D) is called
22. In "The girl can swim", the action word is ____
   A) swim
   B) can
   C) girl
   D) the
23. We say "I run" but we say "she ____" .
   A) run
   B) runned
   C) running
   D) runs
24. Which pair are both action words?
   A) hot and cold
   B) kick and catch
   C) table and chair
   D) red and big
25. The first line of our small story tells us who and ____
   A) when
   B) how much
   C) where
   D) whose
26. If a word will not come, we ____ it in the box above the line
   A) draw
   B) throw
   C) hide
   D) burn
27. A story you write yourself may be as short as ____ lines
   A) seven
   B) three
   C) eleven
   D) fifty
28. "Look out ____" is a strong call, so it needs a(n) ____ mark
   A) empty
   B) equal
   C) question
   D) exclamation
29. The name of a friend begins with a ____ letter
   A) big
   B) small
   C) round
   D) quiet
30. "Ican seeit" is hard to read because it has no ____ between words
   A) stop
   B) dot
   C) room
   D) mark

## Section B — Sub-objective (10 marks)
Answer briefly.

31. Say three sight words you know at once
32. Name two things that make a sentence neat on the page
33. Say the two letters that make one sound in "sheep" and in "three"
34. Join the two words and write the new one: sun + set, foot + ball
35. Name the three parts of a story in order
36. Say the three questions that open nearly any story
37. Mention four describing words
38. Say six action words you can do in class
39. Name the three marks that may end a line
40. Write two names that begin with a big letter

## Section C — Theory (10 marks)
Answer in full sentences.

41. Write one sentence about what you did this morning, with a big letter and a full stop. (2 marks)
42. Say the two words inside each of these: bedroom, rainbow, notebook. (3 marks)
43. Write three lines of a story from these boxes: a boy finds a ball; he kicks it over a fence; a girl gives it back. (4 marks)
44. Put the right mark at the end of each line and copy them out: Where is my bag / Look out / I can jump (3 marks)
45. Name four describing words and use one of them in a sentence. (2 marks)
