# EXAMINATION PRACTICE — YORUBA (Nursery 2 · 2nd Term)

**Time:** 1 hour · **Total: 50 marks**

## Section A — Objective (30 marks)
Choose the correct option and ring the letter.

1. "Papá ìdárayá" in our school means the ___
   A) fence
   B) garden
   C) kitchen
   D) playground
2. The Yoruba word for a book is ___
   A) tébèèlì
   B) ìwé
   C) ọ̀nà
   D) pátákó
3. "Ọ̀giri" is the ___
   A) fence round a place
   B) roof of a house
   C) door of a class
   D) bag of a pupil
4. The Yoruba for goat is ___
   A) àdìẹ
   B) màlúù
   C) ewúrẹ́
   D) agbo
5. The animal that brings eggs to the house every morning is the ___
   A) oúnjẹ
   B) ọ̀wọ́ọ̀n
   C) ewúrẹ́
   D) àdìẹ
6. The Yoruba for twenty is ___
   A) ẹ́wọ́
   B) ogún
   C) àárún
   D) ọ̀kànlá
7. "Ọ̀kànlá" is the number ___
   A) twelve
   B) eighteen
   C) eleven
   D) fifteen
8. The number between 14 and 16 in Yoruba is ___
   A) ogún
   B) ọ̀kànlá
   C) mẹ́tàlá (ẹ̀tálá)
   D) mẹ́rìndínlógún (ẹ̀rìnlá)
9. Which of these letters is NOT used in writing Yoruba?
   A) q
   B) gb
   C) ń
   D) ṣ
10. The letters made by joining two sounds in Yoruba include gb and ___
   A) x
   B) j
   C) v
   D) c
11. The seven vowels of the Yoruba alphabet are a, e, ẹ, i, o, ọ and ___
   A) y only
   B) ü
   C) w
   D) u
12. The Yoruba word for water is ___
   A) omi
   B) ọ̀nà
   C) ilé
   D) inú
13. "Ìyan" is food made from ___
   A) roasted maize
   B) pounded yam
   C) boiled rice
   D) fried beans
14. The soup made from melon seed is called ___
   A) ẹ̀fẹ́
   B) ógọ́rọ́
   C) ègúsí
   D) ẹ̀wẹ̀dú
15. "Ọmọlúàbí" means a child who is ___
   A) well brought up
   B) never taught
   C) very rich
   D) always last
16. "Ìwà lẹ́wàá" teaches that true beauty is in the ___
   A) shoes
   B) character
   C) face only
   D) clothes
17. The Yoruba word for the hand is ___
   A) ẹsẹ̀
   B) etí
   C) ọwọ́
   D) ojú
18. At a gathering, a child greets first the ___
   A) smallest child
   B) visitor at the gate
   C) driver outside
   D) eldest person present
19. When an elder is speaking to the assembly, a child should ___
   A) walk out and go and play ball
   B) stand or sit quite quiet and listen
   C) add his own noise to the talk
   D) answer the elder very loudly
20. The opposite of "ńlá" (big) in Yoruba is ___
   A) gígùn
   B) òkè
   C) kékeré
   D) tútù
21. The opposite of "jìnnà" (far) is ___
   A) súnmọ́
   B) ẹ̀yìn
   C) ọ̀tún
   D) gbígbóná
22. "Ọ̀tún" means the ___
   A) front only
   B) back only
   C) left side
   D) right side
23. The story told for children, with a lesson inside, is called ___
   A) ẹ̀wì
   B) àlọ́
   C) àkọ́
   D) oríkì
24. When the teller says "Àlọ́!", the class answers ___
   A) "Káàlẹ̀!"
   B) "Ẹ̀ ṣé!"
   C) "Àlọ́ ni!"
   D) "Ọ́ dàbọ̀!"
25. A fast rhyme of hard sounds that trains the mouth is called ___
   A) àròsọ
   B) oúnjẹ
   C) ìkíni
   D) àṣẹjọ
26. The animal that keeps the house and barks is called ___
   A) eku
   B) ologbo
   C) ẹyẹ
   D) ajá
27. Things we should never do to a farm animal is to ___
   A) always give it clean water daily
   B) keep its place neat and clean
   C) tie its leg too tight and beat it badly
   D) give it food at the set time
28. The song "Ọmọ tó mọ ìyá rẹ̀ lójú" teaches a child to ___
   A) forget his name
   B) know his own family
   C) run far away
   D) hide his face
29. "Ẹ̀ káàbọ̀" is said when somebody ___
   A) eats
   B) sleeps
   C) leaves
   D) arrives
30. The word for honour or giving respect in Yoruba is ___
   A) ìbọ̀wọ̀
   B) ìdí
   C) ìjọ̀sùn
   D) ìkòkò

## Section B — Sub-objective (10 marks)
Answer briefly.

31. Name six things in and around the school, in Yoruba.
32. Count from ogún down to ọ̀kan in Yoruba.
33. Name five foods of the Yoruba home and one soup.
34. Say four greetings with the time they are used.
35. Mention five animals a Yoruba home keeps.
36. Write the seven vowels of the Yoruba alphabet.
37. Name four duties of each: father, mother, children and grandparents.
38. Give six opposite word pairs in Yoruba.
39. Say the parts of an àlọ́ story in order.
40. Mention three songs of this term and what one of them teaches.

## Section C — Theory (10 marks)
Answer in full sentences.

41. Name the things round the school in Yoruba and write two sentences on your classroom. (2 marks)
42. Count in Yoruba from eleven to twenty, and write the two ways of saying nineteen. (2 marks)
43. Write on the food of your home: name five dishes and say how two of them are paired with soup. (2 marks)
44. Explain who an ọmọlúàbí is and mention five of his marks. (2 marks)
45. Tell a short àlọ́ in Yoruba or English with its call, its answer and the lesson it teaches. (2 marks)
