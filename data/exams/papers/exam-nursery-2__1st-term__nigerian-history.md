# EXAMINATION PRACTICE — NIGERIAN HISTORY ( · )

**Time:** 1 hour · **Total: 50 marks**

## Section A — Objective (30 marks)
Choose the correct option and ring the letter.

1. History is the story of ___
   A) what will happen tomorrow
   B) what we eat today
   C) how to count money
   D) what happened in the past
2. Which of these is a thing of the past?
   A) what you will do at noon
   B) what you are doing now
   C) what you did yesterday
   D) the lunch in the pot
3. The best people to tell you about the past are ___
   A) the toys under my bed at home
   B) grandparents and old people of the town
   C) the new clock on the staff room wall
   D) the puppies playing in our street
4. A photograph of your baby days helps you know about ___
   A) tomorrow's weather
   B) the price of bread
   C) next session
   D) your past
5. At the top of a family tree we write ___
   A) the grandparents
   B) the cousins of a friend
   C) the class teacher
   D) the babies
6. Knowing your family name tells people your ___
   A) bus number
   B) weight
   C) identity
   D) shoe size
7. The story of what happened before can help us to ___
   A) count to one hundred
   B) make good decisions
   C) run faster
   D) sleep late always
8. A community is made up of ___
   A) the people and places around us
   B) only the children of our class
   C) nothing except the market stalls
   D) only the big houses on our road
9. The person who sells us tomatoes and fish is the ___
   A) the pupil in Nursery 1
   B) the nurse at the clinic
   C) the pilot in the aeroplane
   D) trader
10. The man who keeps our street safe is the ___
   A) the baker with hot bread
   B) the tailor with a sewing machine
   C) policeman
   D) the driver of the bus
11. A sick person in the community is taken to the ___
   A) the barber at the shop
   B) nurse or hospital
   C) the driver of the keke
   D) the musician with a drum
12. The three major ethnic groups of Nigeria are ___
   A) Red, Blue and Green
   B) Tuesday, Friday and Sunday
   C) Lagos, Kano and Ibadan
   D) Hausa, Igbo and Yoruba
13. The greeting 'Sannu' belongs to the ___
   A) Hausa people
   B) Igbo people
   C) Yoruba people
   D) Tiv people
14. Amala and ewédú are food of the ___
   A) Kanuri only
   B) Ijaw only
   C) Yoruba
   D) Hausa only
15. 'Ndewo' is the greeting of the ___
   A) Fulani people
   B) Hausa people
   C) Nupe people
   D) Igbo people
16. Besides the big three, another people in Nigeria are the ___
   A) Tiv
   B) Toyota
   C) Ankara
   D) Naira
17. We buy food and things for the house at the ___
   A) mosque
   B) market
   C) classroom
   D) playground
18. A house that has caught fire needs the ___
   A) the tailor near the school gate
   B) the library of the school
   C) fire station
   D) the barber shop at the junction
19. A thief in the street should be reported to the ___
   A) the school gate keeper
   B) the church choir on Sunday
   C) the market woman with her basket
   D) police station
20. People gather to pray in the ___
   A) the market stall of the trader
   B) church or the mosque
   C) the playground in the evening
   D) the bus park in the morning
21. In the playground, when only one ball is available, we should ___
   A) take turns
   B) fight for it
   C) hide it
   D) take it home
22. The home of a traditional ruler is called the ___
   A) clinic
   B) garage
   C) palace
   D) bakery
23. The meeting of the whole town is held in the ___
   A) the kitchen of my house
   B) community hall
   C) the animal shed behind the fence
   D) the water side at the stream
24. In the north of Nigeria the traditional ruler is the ___
   A) Oba
   B) Igwe
   C) Deji
   D) Emir
25. An old monument in our town tells us about ___
   A) the next election
   B) the new cinema
   C) the history of the place
   D) the price of new school shoes
26. Tolerance means we ___
   A) respect people who are different from us
   B) laugh at the names of other people
   C) refuse to sit with anybody at lunch
   D) fight over food in the market square
27. What we want for ourselves, we should also ___
   A) hide from our friends
   B) take by force
   C) laugh at
   D) want for other people
28. A bundle of sticks is hard to break because we are ___
   A) weak alone
   B) too many children
   C) stronger together
   D) not allowed to try
29. How many groups of people are there in Nigeria?
   A) more than two hundred and fifty
   B) only three groups in the whole country
   C) about ten groups
   D) exactly one group
30. The green colour on the Nigerian flag stands for ___
   A) the river Niger in our country
   B) the land of Nigeria
   C) the sky above us at noon
   D) our school uniform on Monday

## Section B — Sub-objective (10 marks)
Answer briefly.

31. Mention three things or people that tell us about the past.
32. Write the order of a family tree from the top.
33. Name four people who help us in the community.
34. Mention the three major ethnic groups and one thing each is known for.
35. Match the place to its use: market, hospital, school, police station.
36. Name three traditional places of a town.
37. State four rules of living together in peace.
38. Mention three festivals in Nigeria and where they are held.
39. Describe the Nigerian flag and what its colours mean.
40. Say two ways Nigerians are different and two things that make us one.

Answer in full sentences.

44. What happens in the palace, and what happens in the community hall? (2 marks)
