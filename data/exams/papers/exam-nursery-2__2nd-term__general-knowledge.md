# EXAMINATION PRACTICE — GENERAL KNOWLEDGE (Nursery 2 · 2nd Term)

**Time:** 1 hour · **Total: 50 marks**

## Section A — Objective (30 marks)
Choose the correct option and ring the letter.

1. The helper who treats people when they are sick is the ___
   A) tailor
   B) farmer
   C) doctor
   D) driver
2. A nurse works mainly at the ___
   A) market
   B) hospital or clinic
   C) filling station
   D) farm
3. The helper who sews our school cloth is the ___
   A) tailor
   B) barber
   C) mason
   D) pilot
4. Where does a police officer work? He works at the ___
   A) school kitchen
   B) motor park
   C) police station
   D) poultry house
5. The farmer is important to the town because he ___
   A) drives the bus
   B) grows the food we eat
   C) keeps the gate
   D) sews our uniforms
6. Our money in Nigeria is called the ___
   A) franc
   B) shilling
   C) cedi
   D) Naira
7. The sign written for the Naira is ___
   A) ₦
   B) $
   C) £
   D) ¢
8. The colours of the Nigerian flag are ___
   A) white and blue only
   B) green and gold
   C) green, white and green
   D) red, white and black
9. The white colour on our flag stands for ___
   A) water
   B) market
   C) farms
   D) peace
10. The green colour on our flag is for our ___
   A) planes and birds
   B) farms and land
   C) books and pens
   D) cars and roads
11. Our capital city is ___
   A) Abuja
   B) Sokoto
   C) Ibadan
   D) Lagos
12. On a simple map, our country Nigeria is found in ___ Africa.
   A) East
   B) South only
   C) North only
   D) West
13. The eagle is important to us because it is our ___
   A) pet in the house
   B) national bird
   C) fish of the river
   D) farm animal
14. The animal called the king of animals is the ___
   A) rabbit
   B) goat
   C) lion
   D) dog
15. The home of a dog is called a ___
   A) kennel
   B) coop
   C) hutch
   D) stable
16. A chicken is kept in a ___
   A) nest on a tall tree
   B) coop or a poultry house
   C) sty or a pig hole
   D) stable for a horse
17. The language used for our lessons in school is ___
   A) Latin
   B) French
   C) Arabic only
   D) English
18. A day with clouds and water falling from the sky is a ___
   A) windy day
   B) sunny day
   C) rainy day
   D) dusty day
19. The harmattan season in Nigeria brings air that is ___
   A) wet and muddy
   B) dry, dusty and cool
   C) dark all day
   D) hot and rainy
20. On a rainy morning the thing we carry to keep dry is an ___
   A) plate
   B) fan
   C) broom
   D) umbrella
21. The part of a plant that drinks water from the soil is the ___
   A) root
   B) flower
   C) fruit
   D) leaf
22. A flower on a plant later becomes the ___
   A) branch
   B) stone
   C) fruit
   D) shadow
23. The things a plant gives us for our home are ___
   A) iron and tin
   B) books and pens
   C) only fire and ash
   D) food, shade and fresh air
24. Which of these is a vegetable and not a fruit?
   A) ugu
   B) mango
   C) banana
   D) orange
25. We hold a grown-up's ___ when we cross a busy road.
   A) bag
   B) hand
   C) shoe
   D) finger only
26. Before crossing the road a child should first ___
   A) shout at all of the cars
   B) close both eyes and then jump
   C) stop and look left, right, then left
   D) run across as fast as possible
27. The sense we use for smelling food is the ___
   A) nose
   B) ear
   C) tongue
   D) skin
28. A dangerous thing at home that must never be touched is ___
   A) a folded towel on the bed
   B) a school shoe by the door
   C) a wooden spoon for the pot
   D) a bottle of medicine on the shelf
29. The tortoise in our folktales is known for being clever and ___
   A) big as an elephant
   B) greedy
   C) fast always
   D) strong like a horse
30. In the story of the wisdom gourd, the tortoise learnt that wisdom must be ___
   A) sold at the market
   B) hidden in a tree
   C) shared with everybody
   D) buried in the farm

## Section B — Sub-objective (10 marks)
Answer briefly.

31. Name five helpers in our community and what each does.
32. Say where four of these helpers work: doctor, teacher, police, farmer.
33. Name the two colours of our flag and what each means.
34. Mention four Nigerian foods and two clothes we wear on special days.
35. Name the two seasons in Nigeria and one thing each brings.
36. Name four parts of a plant and what the root does.
37. Match four animals with their homes: dog, fowl, cow, rabbit.
38. Name the five senses with the organ of each.
39. Mention four dangers of the home we must keep away from.
40. Name three songs, rhymes or tales we learnt this term.

## Section C — Theory (10 marks)
Answer in full sentences.

41. Who are community helpers? Write on four of them and why we thank them. (2 marks)
42. Describe the Nigerian flag, our money and where our country is. (2 marks)
43. Write on the weather: the two seasons of Nigeria and the clothes for each. (2 marks)
44. Name plants around you: four parts of a plant and what each part does. (2 marks)
45. Tell a story or a rhyme you learnt this term and say what it teaches. (2 marks)
