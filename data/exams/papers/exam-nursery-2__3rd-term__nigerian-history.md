# EXAMINATION PRACTICE — NIGERIAN HISTORY (Nursery 2 · 3rd Term)

**Time:** 1 hour · **Total: 53 marks**

## Section A — Objective (30 marks)
Choose the correct option and ring the letter.

1. A traditional ruler is the ___ of the people in his area
   A) head
   B) trader
   C) visitor
   D) child
2. A traditional ruler is chosen into the throne by ___
   A) the monitor of the class
   B) the elders of the ruling house
   C) the children of the school
   D) the women of the market
3. When two families are fighting they are taken to the ruler to be ___
   A) laughed at
   B) forgotten
   C) divided
   D) settled
4. The ruler keeps our ___ safe for the people
   A) the cups in the market
   B) the fees of the school
   C) customs and festivals
   D) the shoes of the house
5. An Emir is the traditional ruler of the ___
   A) North
   B) South
   C) river bank
   D) coast
6. The Oba is known by his long ___
   A) school bag
   B) beads of coral
   C) fishing net
   D) broom
7. In the Southeast the ruler is called an Igwe or an ___
   A) pilot
   B) Oba
   C) Emir
   D) Eze
8. Many thrones in one country teach the people to ___
   A) honour one another
   B) sell the land
   C) forget one another
   D) divide the market
9. A chief is a ___ who speaks for a group of families
   A) stranger
   B) title holder
   C) child
   D) trader
10. Titles are mostly given to people who ___
   A) come from a very far place
   B) shout the loudest in the market
   C) tell the truth and help the community
   D) own many goats in the village
11. A title is given by the people and then ___ by the ruler
   A) denied
   B) forgotten
   C) sold
   D) confirmed
12. A title is a honour that ___
   A) is a toy
   B) cannot be bought
   C) can be bought
   D) lasts one day
13. The father of the village street is called the ___
   A) Stranger
   B) Iyaloja
   C) Baale
   D) Otunba
14. The mother of the market is called the ___
   A) Iyaloja
   B) Baale
   C) Baby
   D) Otunba
15. The chief who carries messages to the quarters is the ___
   A) child
   B) Otunba
   C) farmer
   D) visitor
16. Title holders are respected as ___, not as rich persons
   A) traders
   B) friends
   C) institutions
   D) singers
17. The nearest government to the people is the ___
   A) Local Government
   B) the church on Sunday
   C) the market on Friday
   D) the palace at home
18. The head of a Local Government is called the ___
   A) king
   B) Babalawo
   C) class teacher
   D) Executive Chairman
19. A chairman comes into office by ___
   A) buying the chair
   B) voting
   C) a race
   D) a coin toss
20. To vote for a chairman one must be ___ years or more
   A) two
   B) ten
   C) eighteen
   D) four
21. A chairman sits in his office for ___
   A) one hour
   B) one day
   C) the whole of his life
   D) a fixed time
22. A chairman clears the ___ before the rain comes
   A) drains and gutters
   B) the books in the class
   C) the dogs at the gate
   D) the birds in the tree
23. A leader who does not take what is not his is called ___
   A) sleepy
   B) tall
   C) honest
   D) rich
24. A good leader ___ before he decides
   A) runs away
   B) hits the table
   C) closes his ears
   D) listens to both sides
25. A child chosen to keep order in the class is the ___
   A) baby
   B) class monitor
   C) visitor
   D) trader
26. A prefect is a leader for ___
   A) the whole school
   B) the market
   C) one house only
   D) his friends only
27. Before I ask the class to be quiet I must be ___ myself
   A) loud
   B) singing
   C) running
   D) quiet
28. A ruler and a chairman together give the town ___
   A) new shoes
   B) only rain
   C) wisdom and money
   D) a holiday
29. A person who goes in front and keeps order is a ___
   A) leader
   B) trader
   C) stranger
   D) sleeper
30. If a chairman does not do his work the people ___
   A) will give him a new horse
   B) will not vote for him again
   C) will send a chief to him
   D) will build him a fine house
