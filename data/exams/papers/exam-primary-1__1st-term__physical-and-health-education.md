## Section A — Objective (30 marks)

   B) sitting
   C) walking
   D) sleeping

   C) year

   D) warm

   D) jump

   C) a hundred
   D) five

   A) rest
   B) shuttle
   C) slow
   D) long

   A) sit
   B) sleep
   C) eat
   D) turn

   D) noise
