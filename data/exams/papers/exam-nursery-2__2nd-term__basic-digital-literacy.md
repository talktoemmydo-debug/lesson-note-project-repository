# EXAMINATION PRACTICE — BASIC DIGITAL LITERACY (Nursery 2 · 2nd Term)

**Time:** 1 hour · **Total: 50 marks**

## Section A — Objective (30 marks)
Choose the correct option and ring the letter.

1. The old counting frame with beads on wires is called the ___
   A) monitor
   B) abacus
   C) printer
   D) keyboard
2. Charles Babbage is remembered because he made the ___
   A) steam engine
   B) printing press
   C) first mobile phone
   D) plan of a counting machine
3. The woman who wrote the first steps for a machine was ___
   A) Ada Lovelace
   B) Margaret Ekpo
   C) Queen Amina
   D) Funmilayo Ransome-Kuti
4. As computers changed, they became smaller and ___
   A) slower
   B) dearer
   C) faster
   D) heavier
5. A machine of the fifth generation can learn and answer questions; this is called ___
   A) a paper tape reader
   B) artificial intelligence
   C) a slow old typewriter
   D) a hand-driven generator
6. Hardware means the parts of a computer that we can ___
   A) print on paper
   B) hear but not see
   C) only dream of
   D) touch and see
7. Which of these is software?
   A) a drawing app
   B) the mouse
   C) the keyboard
   D) the power cable
8. The brain of the computer is the ___
   A) monitor
   B) fan
   C) CPU
   D) cover
9. The part that keeps our work in the computer until we call it again is the ___
   A) power switch
   B) screen glass
   C) mouse pad
   D) hard drive
10. Work kept in RAM is lost when ___
   A) we close the file
   B) the power goes off
   C) the fan stops
   D) the desk is swept
11. The big board inside the machine that joins all the parts is the ___
   A) speaker
   B) lamp
   C) motherboard
   D) keyboard
12. The keys used for typing on a computer are the ___
   A) keyboard
   B) scanner
   C) projector
   D) printer
13. The order of letters on a keyboard is called the ___
   A) Friday layout
   B) QWERTY layout
   C) ABC layout
   D) number layout
14. The key that moves us to the next line is ___
   A) Esc
   B) Caps Lock
   C) Shift
   D) Enter
15. To make all the letters big we press ___
   A) Caps Lock
   B) the space bar
   C) Backspace
   D) the wheel
16. Clicking once on the mouse is for ___
   A) shutting the machine down
   B) cooling the CPU
   C) choosing something
   D) printing a page
17. To open a folder we usually ___
   A) shake the mouse
   B) double-click it
   C) pull the cable
   D) press Caps Lock
18. Moving an icon by holding the button, moving the mouse and letting go is ___
   A) scanning
   B) saving
   C) printing
   D) drag and drop
19. A device that takes our voice into the computer is the ___
   A) microphone
   B) fan
   C) monitor
   D) printer
20. While speaking into a microphone we should ___
   A) drop it on the table for fun
   B) shout straight into it very close
   C) hold it by the body, not the cable
   D) put it under running water
21. The right sitting position for computer work keeps our ___
   A) hands wet with water
   B) back straight and eyes level
   C) shoes clean and laced up
   D) bag closed on the floor
22. A device that puts a paper drawing into the computer is the ___
   A) speaker
   B) projector
   C) mouse
   D) scanner
23. A small camera on top of the screen that shows our face on a call is the ___
   A) printer
   B) router
   C) webcam
   D) keyboard
24. A device joined to the computer with no wire at all uses ___
   A) a long rope
   B) Bluetooth
   C) a nail
   D) glue
25. A flash drive carries ___
   A) food for lunch
   B) water for the plant
   C) chalk for the board
   D) our work in the pocket
26. A monitor shows us ___
   A) the work of the computer on a screen
   B) the papers that need cutting
   C) the dust inside the box
   D) the sound of the machine only
27. A blurry picture on a screen means the picture has ___
   A) too many small dots
   B) too few small dots
   C) no colour at all
   D) a bigger box
28. The printer that squirts tiny drops of liquid is the ___
   A) laser printer
   B) typewriter

   D) inkjet printer
29. Too much paper in a printer tray will make the printer ___
   A) wake up
   B) sing
   C) jam
   D) cool down

   A) only the person wearing it
   B) everybody in the room
   C) nobody at all
   D) the whole class

Answer in full sentences.

41. Explain the difference between hardware and software and give two examples of each. (2 marks)
42. Describe the keyboard and the mouse and say how we take care of them. (2 marks)
43. What is an input device? Write on the microphone, the scanner and the webcam. (2 marks)
44. Write on any two output devices: what each one does and how we care for it. (2 marks)
