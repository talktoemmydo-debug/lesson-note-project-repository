# EXAMINATION PRACTICE — CHRISTIAN RELIGIOUS STUDIES (Nursery 2 · 3rd Term)

**Time:** 1 hour · **Total: 54 marks**

## Section A — Objective (30 marks)
Choose the correct option and ring the letter.

1. A saviour is somebody who ___ us
   A) forgets
   B) paints
   C) saves
   D) frightens
2. Jesus came into the world to save us from our ___
   A) sins
   B) sleep
   C) school
   D) soup
3. Jesus died on the ___ for us
   A) rock
   B) cross
   C) horse
   D) table
4. Jesus rose from the dead on the ___ day
   A) second
   B) fifth
   C) third
   D) tenth
5. Today Jesus is alive in ___
   A) heaven
   B) my school
   C) the river
   D) the market
6. God so loved the world that He gave His only ___
   A) brother
   B) donkey
   C) servant
   D) Son
7. When we accept Jesus we become children of ___
   A) the king
   B) God
   C) my teacher
   D) the priest
8. Jesus said, "I call you ___"
   A) friends
   B) visitors
   C) neighbours far off
   D) strangers
9. We show that we are Jesus' friends by ___ Him
   A) frightening
   B) tricking
   C) obeying
   D) cheating
10. In God's family everybody in the class is my ___
   A) paying guest
   B) stranger only
   C) class enemy
   D) brother or sister
11. The greatest commandment is to love ___
   A) the market on Sunday
   B) God with all my heart
   C) sleeping in the morning
   D) my own new ball
12. The second commandment is to love ___
   A) the compound gate
   B) my morning
   C) my neighbour as myself
   D) my teacher's stick
13. Who is my neighbour?
   A) everybody around me
   B) only rich people
   C) only grown-ups
   D) only old people
14. We show love to God by praying, thanking Him and ___ Him
   A) losing
   B) hiding from
   C) teasing
   D) obeying
15. Jesus said, "Love ___ as I have loved you."
   A) strangers only
   B) one another
   C) the sun only
   D) nobody at all
16. God's rules for living are called ___
   A) commandments
   B) birthdays
   C) market days
   D) playgrounds
17. The commandments were brought to the people by ___ on a mountain
   A) Pharaoh
   B) Noah
   C) Goliath
   D) Moses
18. Which of these is one of God's rules?
   A) do not sing
   B) do not pray
   C) do not steal
   D) do not share
19. We obey God because He ___
   A) loves us and knows what is best
   B) is too far away to hear me
   C) lives next door and is busy
   D) does not know what I need
20. Jesus obeyed God ___ all His life
   A) by mistake
   B) perfectly
   C) never at all
   D) once a year
21. Prayer is talking to ___
   A) the farm
   B) the moon
   C) the door
   D) God
22. We can pray ___
   A) only at night
   B) only in the church
   C) in any place
   D) only on Sunday
23. Before a meal we ___
   A) thank God for the food
   B) sleep on the mat
   C) count the plates
   D) wash the cup
24. When we are sad or afraid we should ___
   A) run and shout
   B) tell a long lie
   C) hide under the bed
   D) ask God for help
25. When somebody is sick we ___
   A) take their food
   B) pray for them
   C) leave them alone
   D) laugh at them
26. "Pray without ceasing" means ___
   A) sleep and dream
   B) never pray at all
   C) keep talking to God all day
   D) pray once in a year
27. Which of these is a way to pray?
   A) kneeling with the head bowed
   B) shouting at my friends
   C) closing the door tight
   D) jumping up and down
28. God's rules were written on two tables of ___
   A) mud
   B) stone
   C) cloth
   D) wood
29. At night we ask God for ___
   A) new shoes
   B) a long class
   C) peaceful sleep
   D) another plate
30. A prayer is not better because it is ___
   A) short
   B) true
   C) quiet
   D) long

## Section B — Sub-objective (10 marks)
Answer briefly.

31. Name two wrong things the Bible calls sin
32. Mention three things a friend does for you
33. Name two commandments that keep my mouth and my hands clean
34. Say three times we pray every day
35. Name three places where we can pray
36. Say two parts of the pattern Jesus taught in prayer
37. Mention three people we should pray for
38. Name two prayer positions we learnt
39. Say two ways we show love to God
40. Fill in: God's reply to a prayer may be yes, wait, or ___

## Section C — Theory (10 marks)
Answer in full sentences.

41. Write two sentences on why Jesus came into the world. (2 marks)
42. Mention three ways you can show love to a friend who is different from you. (3 marks)
43. What is prayer? Name three times you pray in one day. (4 marks)
44. Explain why we obey God's commandments. (3 marks)
45. Write two sentences on the prayer you said before eating today. (2 marks)
