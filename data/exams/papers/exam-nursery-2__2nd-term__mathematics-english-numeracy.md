## Section B — Sub-objective (10 marks)
Answer briefly.

31. Write the numbers 11 to 20 in figures.
32. Count in tens from ten to one hundred.
33. Give the tens and the ones in 63 and in 91.
34. Arrange in descending order: 51, 24, 66, 39.
35. Put in the correct sign: 45 ____ 54, 72 ____ 27, 30 + 20 ____ 50.
36. Solve: 8 + 6, 15 − 7, 9 + 9 and 20 − 6.
37. There are 11 oranges and 6 bananas. How many fruits, and what operation did you use?
38. Fill the boxes: 7 + □ = 13, □ − 8 = 6 and 14 − □ = 6.
39. Count the money: ₦20 + ₦10 + ₦5 + ₦5, and ₦50 − ₦20.
40. Name four notes of Nigeria from the smallest and two coins we count with.

## Section C — Theory (10 marks)
Answer in full sentences.

41. Write the numbers from 31 to 50 and say how many tens and ones are in 47. (2 marks)
42. Explain the difference between > and <, and arrange 38, 83, 33 and 88 from the smallest. (2 marks)
43. Solve these and show your work: 12 + 5, 17 − 6, 9 + 8, 20 − 13. (2 marks)
44. What is an open sentence? Find the box in 6 + □ = 15 and in □ − 7 = 8. (2 marks)
45. Write on the naira: the coins and notes you know, and how you pay ₦50 for a thing that costs ₦35. (2 marks)
