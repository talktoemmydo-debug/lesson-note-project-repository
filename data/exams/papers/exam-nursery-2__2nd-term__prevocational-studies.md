# EXAMINATION PRACTICE — PREVOCATIONAL STUDIES (PVS) (Nursery 2 · 2nd Term)

**Time:** 1 hour · **Total: 50 marks**

## Section A — Objective (30 marks)
Choose the correct option and ring the letter.

1. A farmer is a person who ___
   A) drives a big bus to the town
   B) sells cloth inside the market
   C) grows crops or keeps animals for food
   D) mends our shoes at the roadside
2. The farmer who keeps birds for eggs and meat is a ___
   A) poultry farmer
   B) fish farmer
   C) tree farmer
   D) miner
3. Work that begins a new farm, before any seed is planted, is ___
   A) selling the produce
   B) drying the grain
   C) harvesting the crop
   D) clearing the land
4. After planting, the farmer removes the ___
   A) stones from the market
   B) weeds
   C) birds from the sky
   D) roof from the shed
5. The last work in growing a crop, before the money comes, is ___
   A) watering the weeds
   B) plucking the roof
   C) harvesting and selling
   D) burning the fence
6. A fish farmer feeds his fish at set times and keeps the ___
   A) water clean and moving
   B) birds away from the roof
   C) tools in the sun
   D) fire in the shed
7. Why does a farmer begin his work very early in the morning?
   A) because the sun is brighter at noon
   B) because the body is cooler and weeds are soft
   C) because the tools are heavier in rain
   D) because the market is shut down later
8. A farm visit is when the class goes to the farm to ___
   A) play football with the bigger boys
   B) have a long sleep on the grass
   C) buy plenty of food for the house
   D) see with the eyes what the class was told
9. Before we start a farm visit we should ___
   A) carry our lunch in an open bag
   B) run to the gate first
   C) shut our shoes and fill the water flask
   D) leave our books on the ground
10. On the farm we must not ___
   A) pick the fruit or chase the birds away
   B) wash our hands when we come back
   C) look at the plants in the rows
   D) ask the farmer any good question
11. An animal that sits apart from the others in the house is probably ___
   A) sleeping well
   B) happy
   C) the newest
   D) sick
12. A good question to ask the farmer on a visit is ___
   A) what is your father's name?
   B) when did you plant this crop?
   C) how old is your gate?
   D) where do you sleep?
13. A farm tool is a thing we use to ___
   A) make farm work easier
   B) sew the school uniform
   C) cook the family food
   D) wash our plates
14. The tool with a broad blade used for tilling and making ridges is the ___
   A) basket
   B) watering can
   C) hoe
   D) rake
15. A cutlass is used mainly for ___
   A) carrying the harvest to the market
   B) watering the young seedlings daily
   C) sowing the small seeds in rows
   D) clearing the grass and small sticks
16. A tool with teeth for gathering leaves and leveling a seedbed is the ___
   A) rake
   B) spade
   C) cutlass
   D) basket
17. A watering can has a spout with small holes called a ___
   A) leaf
   B) handle
   C) rose
   D) root
18. The tool with a flat stiff blade that is pushed with the foot into the ground is the ___
   A) shovel
   B) spade
   C) basket
   D) sickle
19. Which tool is better for lifting and moving loose sand?
   A) a hoe
   B) a watering can
   C) a rake
   D) a shovel
20. A wheelbarrow carries a big load because it has a ___
   A) wheel, a tray and two handles
   B) sharp edge under the front
   C) net and a heavy wooden lid
   D) very long rope for pulling it
21. In loading a barrow, the heavy things are put ___
   A) under the wheel
   B) near the wheel and over it
   C) at the back by the handles
   D) on top of everything
22. A wheelbarrow must never be used for carrying ___
   A) a bag of manure
   B) sand or soil
   C) people or children
   D) the harvest
23. A woven carrier used for the harvest and put on the head is a ___
   A) basket
   B) watering can
   C) shovel
   D) sprayer
24. Before we swing a cutlass we must first ___
   A) water the grass around it
   B) count the leaves on the bush
   C) hide our tools
   D) clear the people and the stones away
25. A tool is kept after work by ___
   A) burying it deep in the planting bed
   B) cleaning off the mud, drying and hanging it
   C) leaving it there on the wet ground
   D) pouring cold water on the blade
26. The tool used to fan chaff from grain is the ___
   A) cutlass
   B) watering can
   C) winnowing basket
   D) spade
27. A knapsack sprayer differs from a watering can because it sends a ___
   A) big stream of water on the root
   B) whole load of clean soil
   C) heavy heap of farm manure
   D) fine mist of medicine on to the leaf
28. A sickle is a curved one-hand blade used for ___
   A) carrying water
   B) reaping grass and maize
   C) digging a big pit
   D) tying the yam
29. The farmers' saying about the two tools that look alike is: a spade digs, a shovel ___
   A) dries
   B) sings
   C) moves
   D) sleeps
30. Tools must not be left where a child can step on them, so they are ___
   A) racked or hung with the edge covered
   B) stood up out in the hot sun
   C) thrown down inside the gutter
   D) hidden under the young crop

## Section B — Sub-objective (10 marks)
Answer briefly.

31. Name the four kinds of farmer we learnt and what each keeps or grows.
32. Mention five works of a crop farmer in order.
33. Name four things to look for on a crop farm during a visit.
34. Draw and name the parts of a tool: the working metal and the part in the hand.
35. Mention the parts of a watering can.
36. Name four tools used for carrying on the farm.
37. Sort these into digging and clearing: hoe, cutlass, spade, rake.
38. Mention the parts of a wheelbarrow.
39. Name two pairs of tools that look alike and the one difference in each.
40. Give four rules for storing farm tools well.

## Section C — Theory (10 marks)
Answer in full sentences.

41. Who is a farmer? Explain the work of any two kinds of farmer. (2 marks)
42. Write on a farm visit: the rules we keep and four things we look for. (2 marks)
43. Name four farm tools and say what each is used for. (2 marks)
44. Explain the difference between a spade and a shovel, and between a hoe and a rake. (2 marks)
45. Write on the care and storage of farm tools. (2 marks)
