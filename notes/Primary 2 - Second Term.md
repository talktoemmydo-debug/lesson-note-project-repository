# Basic Science

These are the notes for the second term of Primary 2. They follow the NERDC
Basic Education Curriculum (2025 revision) and the school's scheme of work.
The eight weeks below are the weeks that carry a written note; the remaining
weeks of the term are for revision and the end-of-term paper, so no note is
written for them.

*Depth: NERDC Primary 2 · Basic Science · Second Term W1-6, 8-9 (air in motion; quality of water; uses of water; plants I; plants II; animals; forms of technology; forms of technology — light).*


### WEEK 1 — AIR IN MOTION

**You will learn to**

• say what moving air is

• name what moving air does

• use moving air

**Things to know**

• **Air** is all around us.

• When air **moves**, we call it **wind**.

• Wind can be **gentle** or **strong**.

• A gentle wind is a **breeze**.

• A strong wind can **blow** things away.

• Wind makes **leaves** and **flags** move.

• Wind helps **sails** on a boat to move.

• Wind can **cool** us when we are hot.

• A **fan** moves air to cool us.

• We can feel the wind on our skin.

**Let us talk**

1. What is moving air called?

2. Name one thing wind can do.

3. How does wind help a boat?

**Words for my notebook**

• wind — moving air

• breeze — a gentle wind

• blow — to move with air

**Worksheet**

1. Moving air is called ___.
   A) wind B) water C) stone D) fire

2. A gentle wind is a ___.
   A) breeze B) storm C) rain D) cloud

3. Wind makes leaves and ___ move.
   A) flags B) stones C) water D) fire

4. A ___ moves air to cool us.
   A) fan B) stone C) pot D) book

5. Fill in: To move with air is to _____.

6. Mention two things wind can do.

7. Write what wind is.

**My own work**

• Draw a flag moving in the wind.

### WEEK 2 — QUALITY OF WATER

**You will learn to**

• say what clean water is

• tell clean from dirty water

• keep water clean

**Things to know**

• **Clean water** is water that is safe to use.

• Clean water has **no colour**, **no smell** and **no taste**.

• Clean water is **clear**.

• **Dirty water** may have colour, smell or taste.

• Dirty water can make us **sick**.

• Germs live in dirty water.

• We **filter** or **boil** water to make it clean.

• We keep water in a **clean** container.

• We do not throw rubbish into water.

• Clean water keeps us healthy.

**Let us talk**

1. What is clean water?

2. How can we tell dirty water?

3. How do we make water clean?

**Words for my notebook**

• clean water — water that is safe to use

• dirty water — water that is not safe

• filter — to clean water by passing it through

**Worksheet**

1. Clean water has no colour, no smell and no ___.
   A) taste B) stone C) fire D) smoke

2. Clean water is ___.
   A) clear B) dark C) dirty D) thick

3. Dirty water can make us ___.
   A) sick B) strong C) tall D) happy

4. We ___ or boil water to make it clean.
   A) filter B) dirty C) waste D) hide

5. Fill in: Water that is not safe is _____.

6. Mention two ways to keep water clean.

7. Write what clean water is.

**My own work**

• Draw a glass of clean water.

### WEEK 4 — PLANTS I

**You will learn to**

• name parts of a plant

• say what each part does

• care for plants

**Things to know**

• **Plants** are living things.

• The parts of a plant are **root**, **stem**, **leaf**, **flower** and **fruit**.

• The **root** holds the plant and takes in water.

• The **stem** carries water to the leaves.

• The **leaf** makes food for the plant.

• The **flower** turns into fruit.

• The **fruit** has seeds inside.

• Plants need **water**, **air** and **sunlight**.

• We care for plants by watering them.

• Plants give us food and air.

**Let us talk**

1. Name the parts of a plant.

2. What does the root do?

3. What do plants need to grow?

**Words for my notebook**

• plant — a living thing that grows in the ground

• root — the part that holds the plant

• leaf — the part that makes food

**Worksheet**

1. The part that holds a plant in the ground is the ___.
   A) root B) flower C) fruit D) leaf

2. The part that makes food for the plant is the ___.
   A) leaf B) root C) stem D) fruit

3. The ___ turns into fruit.
   A) flower B) root C) stem D) leaf

4. Plants need water, air and ___.
   A) sunlight B) stone C) fire D) smoke

5. Fill in: The part that carries water to the leaves is the _____.

6. Mention two parts of a plant.

7. Write what plants need to grow.

**My own work**

• Draw a plant and label its parts.

• say how plants grow

• use plants

• Some plants are **trees** (big and tall).

• Some plants are **herbs** (small and soft).

• Some plants are **shrubs** (medium size).

• Some plants **climb** on sticks or walls.

• Plants grow from **seeds**.

• Plants are useful to us.

• Some animals live in the **bush** (wild): lion, elephant.

• Some animals live in **water**: fish, crab.

• Some animals **fly**: bird, bat.

• Animals **move**, **feed**, **breathe** and **grow**.

• A **torch** gives light in the dark.

• **Electricity** makes bulbs give light.
