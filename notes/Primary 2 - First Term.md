# Basic Science

These are the notes for the first term of Primary 2. They follow the NERDC
Basic Education Curriculum (2025 revision) and the school's scheme of work.
The eight weeks below are the weeks that carry a written note; the remaining
weeks of the term are for revision and the end-of-term paper, so no note is
written for them.

*Depth: NERDC Primary 2 · Basic Science · First Term W1-6, 8-9 (living and non-living things; plants around us; animals around us; the human body; our senses; health and cleanliness; air and water; weather and seasons).*


### WEEK 1 — LIVING AND NON-LIVING THINGS

**You will learn to**

• tell living from non-living things

• name living things

• name non-living things

**Things to know**

• **Living things** are things that have life.

• **Non-living things** are things that do not have life.

• Living things **breathe**, **grow**, **move**, **feed** and **reproduce**.

• Living things **die** when their life ends.

• Examples of living things: **man**, **animals**, **plants**.

• Non-living things do **not** breathe, grow or feed.

• Examples of non-living things: **stone**, **table**, **water**, **chair**.

• A plant is a living thing; a stone is a non-living thing.

• Living things need **air**, **water** and **food**.

• We can tell living things by what they do.

**Let us talk**

1. What is a living thing?

2. Name one living thing.

3. Name one non-living thing.

**Words for my notebook**

• living thing — a thing that has life

• non-living thing — a thing that does not have life

• breathe — to take in air

**Worksheet**

1. A thing that has life is a ___ thing.
   A) living B) non-living C) dead D) stone

2. Living things ___ and grow.
   A) breathe B) break C) burn D) melt

3. Which of these is a living thing?
   A) a goat B) a stone C) a table D) a chair

4. Which of these is a non-living thing?
   A) water B) a bird C) a tree D) a boy

5. Fill in: A thing that does not have life is a _____.

6. Mention two living things.

7. Write one thing living things do.

**My own work**

• Draw one living thing and one non-living thing.

### WEEK 2 — PLANTS AROUND US

**You will learn to**

• name parts of a plant

• name types of plants

• care for plants

**Things to know**

• **Plants** are living things.

• The parts of a plant are **root**, **stem**, **leaf**, **flower** and **fruit**.

• The **root** holds the plant in the ground and takes in water.

• The **stem** carries water to the leaves.

• The **leaf** makes food for the plant.

• The **flower** turns into fruit.

• Some plants are **trees** (big), some are **herbs** (small).

• Plants give us **food**, **shade** and **air**.

• We care for plants by watering them.

• We do not pluck leaves for no reason.

**Let us talk**

1. Name the parts of a plant.

2. What does the root do?

3. How do we care for plants?

**Words for my notebook**

• plant — a living thing that grows in the ground

• root — the part that holds the plant and takes in water

• leaf — the part that makes food

**Worksheet**

1. The part that holds a plant in the ground is the ___.

### WEEK 9 — WEATHER AND SEASONS

**You will learn to**

• name types of weather

• name the seasons

• dress for the weather

**Things to know**

• **Weather** is how the air is at a time.

• The weather can be **sunny**, **rainy**, **windy** or **cloudy**.

• When it is **sunny**, the sun shines.

• When it is **rainy**, water falls from the sky.

• When it is **windy**, the air moves fast.

• When it is **cloudy**, clouds cover the sky.

• Nigeria has two main seasons: the **dry season** and the **rainy season**.

• In the dry season, the sun is hot and there is little rain.

• In the rainy season, there is much rain.

• We dress for the weather.

**Let us talk**

1. What is weather?

2. Name one type of weather.

**Words for my notebook**

• weather — how the air is at a time

• season — a time of the year with its own weather

• rainy season — the time when much rain falls
