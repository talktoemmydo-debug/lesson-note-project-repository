# Basic Science

These are the notes for the third term of Primary 1. They follow the NERDC
Basic Education Curriculum (2025 revision) and the school's scheme of work.
The eight weeks below are the weeks that carry a written note; the remaining
weeks of the term are for revision and the end-of-term paper, so no note is
written for them.

*Depth: NERDC Primary 1 · Basic Science · Third Term W1-6, 8-9 (energy; push and pull; simple machines; growth and change; electricity and safety; magnets; waste and recycling; caring for our environment).*


### WEEK 1 — ENERGY AROUND US

**You will learn to**

• say what energy is

• name sources of energy

• say what energy does

**Things to know**

• **Energy** is the power to do work.

• The **sun** gives us light and heat energy.

• A **fire** gives heat energy.

• A **battery** gives energy to a torch.

• **Food** gives our body energy.

• **Electricity** gives energy to lamps and fans.

• Energy helps us to work and play.

• We cannot see energy, but we see what it does.

• The sun is the biggest source of energy.

• We need energy every day.

**Let us talk**

1. What is energy?

2. Name one source of energy.

3. What gives our body energy?

**Words for my notebook**

• energy — the power to do work

• sun — the biggest source of energy

• battery — a thing that stores energy

**Worksheet**

1. Energy is the power to do ___.
   A) work B) sleep C) nothing D) harm

2. The biggest source of energy is the ___.
   A) sun B) moon C) star D) stone

3. Food gives our body ___.
   A) energy B) water only C) dust D) sleep

4. A ___ gives energy to a torch.
   A) battery B) stone C) leaf D) cloth

5. Fill in: Electricity gives energy to lamps and _____.

6. Mention two sources of energy.

7. Write what energy is.

**My own work**

• Draw the sun and one thing that uses energy.
