# Basic Science

These are the notes for the second term of Primary 1. They follow the NERDC
Basic Education Curriculum (2025 revision) and the school's scheme of work.
The eight weeks below are the weeks that carry a written note; the remaining
weeks of the term are for revision and the end-of-term paper, so no note is
written for them.

*Depth: NERDC Primary 1 · Basic Science · Second Term W1-6, 8-9 (plants; animals; sense organs; health and cleanliness; water; weather; light and sound; the sun, moon and stars).*


### WEEK 1 — PLANTS

**You will learn to**

• name the parts of a plant

• say what each part does

• care for a plant

**Things to know**

• A **plant** is a living thing that grows in the soil.

• The **root** holds the plant and takes in water.

• The **stem** carries water to the leaves.

• The **leaf** makes food for the plant.

• The **flower** is the bright part; it can become a fruit.

• Plants need **water**, **air**, **sunlight** and **soil** to grow.

• Some plants give us food, like maize and yam.

• Some plants give us shade, like a big tree.

• We water plants and pull out weeds.

• Plants make our world green and beautiful.

**Let us talk**

1. Name one part of a plant.

2. What does the root do?

3. What do plants need to grow?

**Words for my notebook**

• root — the part that holds the plant and takes in water

• leaf — the part that makes food

• flower — the bright part that can become a fruit

**Worksheet**

1. The part that holds a plant and takes in water is the ___.
   A) root B) leaf C) flower D) fruit

2. The ___ makes food for the plant.
   A) leaf B) stem C) root D) seed

3. Plants need water, air, sunlight and ___.
   A) soil B) stone C) plastic D) smoke

4. The bright part that can become a fruit is the ___.
   A) flower B) root C) stem D) bark

5. Fill in: A plant is a _____ thing.

6. Mention two parts of a plant.

7. Write one thing plants need to grow.

**My own work**

• Draw a plant and label its root, stem and leaf.

### WEEK 2 — ANIMALS

**You will learn to**

• name domestic and wild animals

• say where animals live

• be kind to animals

**Things to know**

• An **animal** is a living thing that can move.

• **Domestic animals** live with us at home, like the dog, cat, goat and hen.

• **Wild animals** live in the bush or forest, like the lion, elephant and monkey.

• Some animals live in water, like the fish.

• Some animals fly, like the bird.

• Animals give us food, such as meat, eggs and milk.

• Animals need food, water and a clean home.

• We are kind and gentle to animals.

• Some animals help us, like the dog that guards the house.

• Animals are living things, so they grow and have young ones.

**Let us talk**

1. Name one domestic animal.

2. Name one wild animal.

3. Where does the fish live?

**Words for my notebook**

• domestic animal — an animal that lives with us

• wild animal — an animal that lives in the bush

• habitat — the place where an animal lives

**Worksheet**

1. An animal that lives with us at home is a ___ animal.
   A) domestic B) wild C) water D) flying

2. The lion is a ___ animal.
   A) wild B) domestic C) pet D) farm

3. The fish lives in ___.
   A) water B) air C) sand D) tree

4. Animals give us meat, eggs and ___.
   A) milk B) stone C) wood D) sand

5. Fill in: The place where an animal lives is its _____.

6. Mention two domestic animals.

7. Write one thing animals need.

**My own work**

• Draw one domestic animal and one wild animal.

### WEEK 3 — OUR SENSE ORGANS

**You will learn to**

• name the five sense organs

• say what each sense organ does

• care for our sense organs

**Things to know**

• We have **five senses**: sight, hearing, smell, taste and touch.

• The **eye** is for seeing.

• The **ear** is for hearing.

• The **nose** is for smelling.

• The **tongue** is for tasting.

• The **skin** is for touching and feeling.

• We keep our eyes and ears clean.

• We do not put sharp things in our ears or nose.

• We wash our hands and body to keep the skin clean.

• Our sense organs help us to know the world.

**Let us talk**

1. Which organ do we use to see?

2. What does the nose do?

3. How do we care for our ears?

**Words for my notebook**

• sense organ — a part of the body that helps us know things

• sight — seeing with the eyes

• taste — knowing flavour with the tongue

**Worksheet**

1. We see with our ___.
   A) eyes B) ears C) nose D) tongue

2. We hear with our ___.
   A) ears B) eyes C) skin D) nose

3. We smell with our ___.
   A) nose B) tongue C) eyes D) ears

4. We taste with our ___.
   A) tongue B) skin C) ears D) eyes

5. Fill in: We touch and feel with our _____.

6. Mention two sense organs.

7. Write what the eye does.

**My own work**

• Draw my face and label two sense organs.

### WEEK 4 — HEALTH AND CLEANLINESS

**You will learn to**

• say how to keep the body clean

• name ways germs spread

• keep ourselves healthy

**Things to know**

• **Germs** are tiny things that can make us sick.

• Germs live in dirt, on our hands and in bad food.

• We wash our hands with soap and water.

• We bathe every day and wear clean clothes.

• We brush our teeth in the morning and at night.

• We cover our food and drink clean water.

• We cut our nails and keep them clean.

• Germs spread through dirty hands, flies and bad food.

• A clean body keeps germs away.

• Cleanliness helps us to be healthy and strong.

**Let us talk**

**Words for my notebook**

• healthy — well and strong

### WEEK 5 — WATER

**You will learn to**

• say where we get water

• name the uses of water

• keep water clean

**Things to know**

• **Water** is a liquid we drink and use every day.

• We get water from the **tap**, **well**, **river**, **rain** and **borehole**.

• We drink water to live.

• We use water to bathe, cook, wash and water plants.

• Clean water is clear and has no bad smell.

• We keep water in a clean, covered container.

• Dirty water can make us sick.

• We do not waste water.

• Water has no colour and no taste.

• We need clean water every day.

**Let us talk**

### WEEK 6 — WEATHER AND SEASONS

**You will learn to**

• name the types of weather

• say what we do in each weather

• name the seasons

**Things to know**

• **Weather** is how the air is on a day.

• The weather can be **sunny**, **rainy**, **cloudy** or **windy**.

• On a **sunny** day the sun shines; we wear light clothes.

• On a **rainy** day water falls; we use an umbrella.

• On a **cloudy** day the sky is full of clouds.

• On a **windy** day the air blows hard.

• Nigeria has two seasons: the **dry season** and the **rainy season**.

• The dry season is hot and has little rain.

• The rainy season has much rain.

• We dress and plan for the weather.

**Let us talk**

1. Name one type of weather.

2. What do we use on a rainy day?

3. Name the two seasons in Nigeria.

**Words for my notebook**

• weather — how the air is on a day

• season — a long time of one kind of weather

• rainy season — the time of much rain

**Worksheet**

   A) umbrella B) fan C) oven D) ink

   A) rainy B) hot C) cold D) dark

4. On a ___ day the sun shines.
   A) sunny B) rainy C) cloudy D) windy

**My own work**
