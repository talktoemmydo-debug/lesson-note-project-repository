### WEEK 1 — DIGITAL DEVICES AROUND US

*Depth: NERDC Primary 1 · Basic Digital Literacy · First Term W1 (technology and devices).*

**You will learn to**

• say what technology means in one line
• name the digital devices I know
• point to a computer, a tablet, a phone, a watch and a camera
• tell which things at home are digital

**Things to know**

• **Technology** is the smart things people make and use to do work more easily.
• A **digital device** is a machine that works with electricity and shows things on a screen.
• A **computer**, a **tablet**, a **smartphone**, a **smart watch** and a **camera** are digital devices.
• A **desktop** stays on a table; a **laptop** opens like a book; a **tablet** is flat and light.
• A wooden broom and a plastic bucket are not digital devices.

**Let us talk**

1. Name one digital device you have seen at home.
2. Which device do you touch with your finger to make it work?
3. Is a wooden spoon a digital device? Why?

**Words for my notebook**

• technology — smart things people make
• digital — works with electricity and a screen
• tablet — a flat, light computer
• camera — takes pictures

**Worksheet**

1. A machine that works with electricity and shows things on a screen is a ____ device. A) digital B) wooden C) plastic D) paper
2. Which of these is NOT a digital device? A) broom B) camera C) smartphone D) tablet
3. The computer that stays on a table is a A) camera B) watch C) tablet D) desktop
4. Fill in: A ____ opens like a book.
5. Mention two digital devices you can carry about.
6. Write one thing a camera does.
7. Draw a tablet and a smartphone side by side.

**My own work**

Draw one digital device I have seen and colour its screen grey.

### WEEK 2 — THE FOUR BASIC PARTS

*Depth: NERDC Primary 1 · Basic Digital Literacy · First Term W2 (parts of a computer).*

**You will learn to**

• name the four basic parts of a computer
• say what each part does
• point to each part on a real computer
• put the parts in the right order of work

**Things to know**

• The **monitor** shows pictures, letters and numbers like a television.
• The **keyboard** has keys we press to put information into the computer.
• The **mouse** is held in the hand and moves the arrow on the screen.
• The **CPU** is the box that does the thinking; it is the brain of the computer.
• We give information with the keyboard or mouse, the CPU thinks, and the monitor shows the answer.

**Let us talk**

1. Which part shows the picture?
2. Which part do we press with our fingers?
3. Which part does the thinking?

**Words for my notebook**

• monitor — shows the picture
• keyboard — keys we press
• mouse — moves the arrow
• CPU — the thinking box

**Worksheet**

1. The part that shows pictures and letters is the A) keyboard B) monitor C) CPU D) mouse
2. We move the arrow on the screen with the A) monitor B) mouse C) speaker D) printer
3. The brain of the computer is the A) mouse B) cable C) keyboard D) CPU
4. Fill in: We press keys on the ____.
5. Mention two parts we touch to give the computer information.
6. Write what the monitor does.
7. Draw a computer and label the monitor and the keyboard.

**My own work**

Draw the four basic parts of a computer in my notebook.

### WEEK 3 — CARING FOR DEVICES AND LAB RULES

*Depth: NERDC Primary 1 · Basic Digital Literacy · First Term W3 (care and safety).*

**You will learn to**

• keep a device clean and dry
• hold and carry a device with two hands
• shut a device the right way
• keep the rules of the computer lab

**Things to know**

• Water and food spoil a device, so we keep them far away from it.
• We carry a laptop or tablet with **two hands** and never by its wire.
• We clean a screen with a **soft, dry cloth** and never with a wet mop.
• We shut a computer the right way from the screen, not by pulling the plug.
• In the lab we walk slowly, we do not eat or drink, and we tell the teacher when something is wrong.

**Let us talk**

1. Why must we keep water away from a phone?
2. How many hands do we use to carry a tablet?
3. What do we clean a screen with?

**Words for my notebook**

• care — to look after
• dry cloth — soft cloth with no water
• shut down — to switch off the right way
• rule — what we must keep

**Worksheet**

1. We clean a screen with a A) soft, dry cloth B) wet mop C) rough stone D) sandy rag
2. A tablet should be carried A) with two hands B) by its wire C) open in one finger D) under a foot
3. Water on a device A) charges it B) cleans it C) spoils it D) makes it faster
4. Fill in: In the lab we do not eat or ____.
5. Mention two ways we care for a device.
6. Write one rule of the computer lab.
7. Draw a child carrying a tablet with two hands.

**My own work**

Draw myself cleaning a screen with a soft, dry cloth.

### WEEK 4 — FROM THE ABACUS TO TODAY

*Depth: NERDC Primary 1 · Basic Digital Literacy · First Term W4 (how computers changed).*

**You will learn to**

• name the first tool people used to count
• tell how computers changed over time
• say what became smaller, faster and cheaper
• know the five generations in order

**Things to know**

• The **abacus** is an old frame with beads on wires that people used to count.
• Computers began big and slow and became **smaller, faster and cheaper**.
• The five generations moved from **vacuum tubes** to **transistors**, then **integrated circuits**, then **microprocessors**, and now **artificial intelligence**.
• A modern phone can do more than the first big computers.

**Let us talk**

1. What did people use to count long ago?
2. Are today's computers bigger or smaller than the first ones?
3. Name one thing a modern phone can do.

**Words for my notebook**

• abacus — old counting frame with beads
• generation — one step in the change
• transistor — a small part that replaced tubes
• artificial intelligence — when a machine learns

**Worksheet**

1. The first tool people used to count is the A) fan B) radio C) telephone D) abacus
2. Over time computers became smaller, cheaper and A) faster B) heavier C) slower D) louder
3. The beads that slide on wires are on an A) umbrella B) abacus C) egg D) orange
4. Fill in: A machine that can learn is called artificial ____.
5. Mention two things computers became over time.
6. Write what the abacus was used for.
7. Draw an abacus with beads on wires.

**My own work**

Draw an abacus and a modern phone side by side.

### WEEK 5 — THE TWO GREAT INVENTORS

*Depth: NERDC Primary 1 · Basic Digital Literacy · First Term W5 (Babbage and Lovelace).*

**You will learn to**

• name the father of the computer
• say what Ada Lovelace did
• put the two inventors in their time order
• say why an idea can be as valuable as a machine

**Things to know**

• **Charles Babbage** designed a machine called the **Analytical Engine**, so he is called the father of the computer.
• **Ada Lovelace** wrote the first set of instructions for his machine; she is the first **programmer**.
• Babbage drew the plan and Ada taught the machine what to do with instructions.
• One built the body and the other taught it to think, so an idea can be as valuable as a machine.

**Let us talk**

1. Who is called the father of the computer?
2. What did Ada Lovelace write?
3. Which came first, the plan or the programme?

**Words for my notebook**

• father of the computer — Charles Babbage
• programmer — one who writes instructions
• Analytical Engine — Babbage's machine plan
• idea — a thought that can build things

**Worksheet**

1. The father of the computer is A) Thomas Edison B) Isaac Newton C) Ada Lovelace D) Charles Babbage
2. The first programmer in the world was A) Flora Shaw B) Queen Amina C) Mary Kingsley D) Ada Lovelace
3. Babbage's machine plan was called the A) Printing Press B) Flying Loom C) Analytical Engine D) Steam Boat
4. Fill in: Ada wrote the first ____ for the machine.
5. Mention the two great inventors of the computer.
6. Write one sentence on why an idea is valuable.
7. Draw a machine with gears like Babbage's engine.

**My own work**

Draw Charles Babbage's thinking machine with wheels and gears.

### WEEK 6 — HARDWARE AND SOFTWARE

*Depth: NERDC Primary 1 · Basic Digital Literacy · First Term W6 (hardware vs software).*

**You will learn to**

• say what hardware means
• say what software means
• sort things into hardware and software
• know that we can touch hardware but not software

**Things to know**

• **Hardware** is the parts of a computer that we can touch and see, like the keyboard and the mouse.
• **Software** is the sets of instructions that tell the computer what to do; we cannot touch it.
• A drawing **app** and a game are software; the screen and the mouse are hardware.
• Hardware is the body and software is the thoughts that make it work.

**Let us talk**

1. Can you touch software? Why?
2. Name one hardware part.
3. Is a game hardware or software?

**Words for my notebook**

• hardware — parts we can touch
• software — instructions we cannot touch
• app — a small program we use
• instruction — a step the computer follows

**Worksheet**

1. The parts of a computer we can touch are the A) software B) songs C) ideas D) hardware
2. A drawing app is A) a cable B) a chair C) software D) hardware
3. Which of these is hardware? A) song B) story C) mouse D) game
4. Fill in: We cannot ____ software.
5. Mention two hardware parts.
6. Write one example of software.
7. Draw one hardware part and one software idea as a box with a name.

**My own work**

Draw two things I can touch (hardware) and write the name of one app.

### WEEK 8 — INPUT DEVICES: KEYBOARD AND MOUSE

*Depth: NERDC Primary 1 · Basic Digital Literacy · First Term W8 (input devices).*

**You will learn to**

• say what an input device is
• use the keyboard and the mouse to give information
• click, scroll and drag with the mouse
• care for the keyboard and the mouse

**Things to know**

• An **input device** puts information into the computer, like the keyboard and the mouse.
• The keyboard has letters in the **QWERTY** order and special keys like Enter and Caps Lock.
• The mouse has a left button and a right button, and a wheel to **scroll**.
• Moving an icon by holding the button and letting go is called **drag and drop**.
• We keep the keyboard clean and we do not eat over it.

**Let us talk**

1. Which two devices put information into the computer?
2. What key moves us to the next line?
3. What do we call moving an icon and letting go?

**Words for my notebook**

• input — putting information in
• QWERTY — the order of the letter keys
• scroll — to move the page with the wheel
• drag and drop — hold, move, let go

**Worksheet**

1. A device that puts information into the computer is an ____ device. A) input B) output C) print D) sound
2. The order of letters on a keyboard is called A) QWERTY B) ABCDE C) FRIDAY D) MONDAY
3. Moving an icon and letting go is A) save B) scan C) drag and drop D) print
4. Fill in: The key that moves us to the next line is ____.
5. Mention two input devices.
6. Write one way we care for the keyboard.
7. Draw a mouse and a keyboard.

**My own work**

Draw a keyboard and circle the Enter key.

### WEEK 9 — OUTPUT DEVICES AND THE MICROPHONE

*Depth: NERDC Primary 1 · Basic Digital Literacy · First Term W9 (output devices and microphone).*

**You will learn to**

• say what an output device is
• name the devices that bring information out
• say what a microphone does
• sort devices into input and output

**Things to know**

• An **output device** brings information out of the computer, like the monitor, the printer and the speakers.
• The **printer** puts our picture or letter onto paper.
• The **speakers** and **headphones** let us hear sound from the computer.
• A **microphone** takes our voice into the computer, so it is an input device.
• The monitor shows, the printer prints and the speakers play.

**Let us talk**

1. Which device puts our work on paper?
2. Which devices let us hear sound?
3. Is a microphone input or output?

**Words for my notebook**

• output — bringing information out
• printer — puts work on paper
• speaker — plays sound
• microphone — takes voice in

**Worksheet**

1. The device that puts our work on paper is the A) microphone B) printer C) keyboard D) mouse
2. We hear sound from the computer through A) mouse B) cable C) speakers D) printer
3. A microphone takes our ____ into the computer. A) voice B) hair C) shoes D) food
4. Fill in: The monitor is an ____ device.
5. Mention two output devices.
6. Write what a microphone does.
7. Draw a printer with a sheet of paper coming out.

**My own work**

Draw a speaker and a microphone and colour the microphone.
