# General Knowledge — Primary 2 · Second Term

These are the notes for the second term of Primary 2. They follow the NERDC
Basic Education Curriculum (2025 revision) and the school's scheme of work.
The eight weeks below are the weeks that carry a written note; the remaining
weeks of the term are for revision and the end-of-term paper, so no note is
written for them.

*Depth: NERDC Primary 2 · General Knowledge · Second Term W1-6, 8-9 (our world: the seven continents; the five oceans; countries and capitals: Africa; countries and capitals: the world; Nigerian culture: languages, dress and festivals; the human body and healthy habits; plants, animals and their habitats; technology around us).*

---

### WEEK 1 — OUR WORLD: THE SEVEN CONTINENTS

**Main content**

• The **world** is made up of big lands called **continents**.

• There are **seven** continents.

• They are **Africa**, **Asia**, **Europe**, **North America**, **South America**, **Australia** and **Antarctica**.

• **Africa** is our continent.

• Nigeria is in **Africa**.

• **Asia** is the biggest continent.

• **Antarctica** is very cold and covered with ice.

• Each continent has many **countries**.

• We live on the continent of Africa.

• The continents make up the land of the world.

**Let us talk**

1. How many continents are there?

2. What is our continent?

3. Name one continent.

**Words for my notebook**

• continent — a very big land

• world — the earth and all the people on it

• country — a land with its own people and government

**Worksheet**

1. There are ___ continents.
   A) seven B) five C) ten D) two

2. Our continent is ___.
   A) Africa B) Asia C) Europe D) Australia

3. The biggest continent is ___.
   A) Asia B) Africa C) Europe D) Australia

4. ___ is very cold and covered with ice.
   A) Antarctica B) Africa C) Asia D) Europe

5. Fill in: A very big land is a _____.

6. Mention three continents.

7. Write what our continent is.

**My own work**

• Draw the world and colour Africa.

### WEEK 2 — OUR WORLD: THE FIVE OCEANS

**Main content**

• The world has big bodies of **salt water** called **oceans**.

• There are **five** oceans.

• They are the **Atlantic**, **Pacific**, **Indian**, **Southern** and **Arctic** Oceans.

• The **Pacific** is the biggest ocean.

• The **Atlantic Ocean** is near Nigeria.

• Oceans are **salty** — we do not drink their water.

• Many **fish** and animals live in the oceans.

• Ships travel on the oceans.

• Oceans cover much of the world.

• The oceans are part of our world.

**Let us talk**

1. How many oceans are there?

2. Which ocean is near Nigeria?

3. Name one ocean.

**Words for my notebook**

• ocean — a very big body of salt water

• salt water — water that is salty

• Pacific — the biggest ocean

**Worksheet**

### WEEK 5 — NIGERIAN CULTURE: LANGUAGES, DRESS AND FESTIVALS

**Main content**

• Nigeria has many **languages**.

• The three main languages are **Hausa**, **Yoruba** and **Igbo**.

• We also speak **Ijaw**, **Tiv**, **Edo** and many more.

• English is our **official** language.

• Our **dress** includes aso-oke, isiagu and babbar riga.

• Our **festivals** include the New Yam festival, durbar and Egungun.

• Culture is the **way of life** of a people.

• Our culture includes our **food**, **music** and **dance**.

• We respect and keep our culture.

• Nigerian culture is rich and beautiful.

**Let us talk**

1. Name the three main languages in Nigeria.

2. Name one Nigerian festival.

3. What is culture?

**Words for my notebook**

• language — the words a people speak

• festival — a special day of joy

• culture — the way of life of a people

**Worksheet**

1. The three main languages in Nigeria are Hausa, Yoruba and ___.
   A) Igbo B) French C) Latin D) Arabic

2. The official language of Nigeria is ___.
   A) English B) French C) Arabic D) Latin

3. The Igbo have the ___ festival.
   A) New Yam B) durbar C) Egungun D) Osun

4. Culture is the ___ of life of a people.
   A) way B) stone C) tree D) cloud

5. Fill in: A special day of joy is a _____.

6. Mention two Nigerian languages.

7. Write what culture is.

**My own work**

• Draw someone in Nigerian traditional dress.

### WEEK 6 — THE HUMAN BODY AND HEALTHY HABITS

**Main content**

• The **human body** has many parts.

• The **head**, **hands**, **legs** and **body**.

• The **eyes** see; the **ears** hear.

• The **nose** smells; the **tongue** tastes.

• **Healthy habits** keep the body well.

• We **bathe** every day.

• We **brush** our teeth every morning and night.

• We **wash** our hands before eating.

• We eat **good food** and **rest** well.

• Healthy habits keep us strong.

**Let us talk**

1. Name one part of the body.

2. Name one healthy habit.

**Words for my notebook**

• body — the whole physical part of a person

• healthy habit — something we do to stay well

• rest — taking it easy

**Worksheet**

### WEEK 8 — PLANTS, ANIMALS AND THEIR HABITATS

**Main content**

• A **habitat** is where a plant or animal lives.

• **Plants** live in the **soil**.

• Some plants live in **water** (like water lily).

• **Animals** live in different habitats.

• **Fish** live in **water**.

• **Birds** live in **trees** and **nests**.

• **Camels** live in the **desert**.

• **Polar bears** live in **cold** places.

• The habitat gives a plant or animal what it needs.

• We care for plants and animals.

**Let us talk**

1. What is a habitat?

2. Where do fish live?

3. Where do birds live?

**Words for my notebook**

• habitat — where a plant or animal lives

• soil — the earth plants grow in

• desert — a dry sandy place

**Worksheet**

1. Where a plant or animal lives is its ___.
   A) habitat B) stone C) song D) dream

2. Fish live in ___.
   A) water B) trees C) desert D) sky

3. Birds live in trees and ___.
   A) nests B) water C) desert D) fire

4. Camels live in the ___.
   A) desert B) water C) tree D) sky

5. Fill in: The earth plants grow in is _____.

6. Mention two animals and their habitats.

7. Write what a habitat is.

**My own work**

• Draw an animal in its habitat.
