# Basic Digital Literacy — Primary 2 · Second Term

These are the notes for the second term of Primary 2. They follow the NERDC
Basic Education Curriculum (2025 revision) and the school's scheme of work.
The eight weeks below are the weeks that carry a written note; the remaining
weeks of the term are for revision and the end-of-term paper, so no note is
written for them.

*Depth: NERDC Primary 2 · Basic Digital Literacy · Second Term W1-6, 8-9 (interface customization; file management I; file management II; digital content creation I; digital content creation II; safety and protection; safe internet use I; safe internet use II).*

---

### WEEK 1 — INTERFACE CUSTOMIZATION

**Main content**

• The **interface** is what we see on the screen.

• The screen shows **icons**, **menus** and **buttons**.

• An **icon** is a small picture we click.

• We can change how the screen **looks**.

• We can make the pictures **bigger** or **smaller**.

• We can change the **background** picture.

• We can move the icons to where we like.

• Changing the screen is called **customizing**.

• We customize to make the screen easy to use.

• We use the screen with care.

**Let us talk**

1. What is an interface?

2. What is an icon?

3. What does customizing mean?

**Words for my notebook**

• interface — what we see on the screen

• icon — a small picture we click

• customize — to change how something looks

**Worksheet**

1. What we see on the screen is the ___.
   A) interface B) keyboard C) mouse D) wire

2. A small picture we click is an ___.
   A) icon B) wire C) key D) plug

3. We can make the pictures bigger or ___.
   A) smaller B) heavier C) louder D) hotter

4. Changing the screen is called ___.
   A) customizing B) breaking C) washing D) burning

5. Fill in: To change how something looks is to _____.

6. Mention two things we see on the screen.

7. Write what an icon is.

**My own work**

• Draw a screen with icons.

### WEEK 2 — FILE MANAGEMENT I

**Main content**

• A **file** is where we keep our work on a computer.

• We keep **pictures**, **writing** and **songs** in files.

• Each file has a **name**.

• We give a file a name we can **remember**.

• We **open** a file to see what is inside.

• We **close** a file when we finish.

• We **save** our work in a file.

• Files help us keep our work safe.

• We do not lose our files.

• File management helps us find our work.

**Let us talk**

1. What is a file?

2. Why do we name a file?

3. What do we do when we finish a file?

**Words for my notebook**

• file — where we keep our work on a computer

• name — what we call a file

• save — to keep our work

**Worksheet**

1. Where we keep our work on a computer is a ___.

   A) name B) stone C) tree D) cloud

3. We ___ a file to see what is inside.
   A) open B) burn C) break D) hide

4. We ___ our work in a file.
   A) save B) lose C) burn D) hide

5. Fill in: To keep our work is to _____.

6. Mention two things we keep in files.

7. Write what a file is.

### WEEK 3 — FILE MANAGEMENT II

**Main content**

• A **folder** is like a box that holds files.

• We keep files in **folders** to be tidy.

• We can name a folder after what is inside.

• A folder can hold many **files**.

• We can put folders inside other folders.

• Keeping files in folders helps us **find** them.

• We **delete** a file we do not need.

• We do not delete files by mistake.

• Tidy files are easy to find.

• Good file management saves time.

**Let us talk**

3. When do we delete a file?

**Words for my notebook**

### WEEK 6 — SAFETY AND PROTECTION

**Main content**

• We keep the computer **safe** from harm.

• We keep the computer away from **water**.

• We do not eat or drink **near** the computer.

• We handle the parts with **care**.

• We use a **password** to keep our work safe.

• A password is a **secret** word we know.

• We do not tell our password to others.

• We do not talk to **strangers** online.

• We tell a grown-up if something seems wrong.

• Safety keeps us and the computer well.

**Let us talk**

1. How do we keep the computer safe?

2. What is a password?

3. Why do we not tell our password?

**Words for my notebook**

• safety — being free from harm

• password — a secret word that keeps our work safe

• stranger — a person we do not know

**Worksheet**

### WEEK 8 — SAFE INTERNET USE I

**Main content**

• The **internet** joins computers all over the world.

• We use the internet to **learn** and **find** things.

• A **grown-up** stays near when we use the internet.

• We do not share our **name** or **address** online.

• We do not chat with people we do not know.

• We do not open links from people we do not know.

• We use the internet for **good** things.

• We do not watch things that are not for children.

• We tell a grown-up if we see something bad.

• Safe internet use keeps us well.

**Let us talk**

### WEEK 9 — SAFE INTERNET USE II

**Main content**

• We are **kind** to others online.

• We do not say **unkind** things online.

• We do not **bully** anyone online.

• We keep our **information** private.

• We do not share **photos** of ourselves with strangers.

• We use **strong** passwords.

• We do not click on **strange** links.

• A **grown-up** helps us before we download.

• We tell a grown-up if someone is unkind online.

• Being safe and kind online is important.

**Let us talk**

1. How do we behave online?

2. What do we keep private?

3. When do we get help?

**Words for my notebook**

• kind — nice and caring

• private — kept for ourselves only

• bully — to hurt someone with words

**Worksheet**

1. We are ___ to others online.
   A) kind B) rude C) angry D) cruel

2. We keep our information ___.
   A) private B) public C) loud D) lost

3. We do not ___ anyone online.
   A) bully B) help C) teach D) sing to

4. We get help before we ___.
   A) download B) sleep C) eat D) run

5. Fill in: Kept for ourselves only is _____.

6. Mention two ways to be safe online.

7. Write how we behave online.

**My own work**

• Draw a child being kind online.
