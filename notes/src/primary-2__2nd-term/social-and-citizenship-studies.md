# Social and Citizenship Studies — Primary 2 · Second Term

These are the notes for the second term of Primary 2. They follow the NERDC
Basic Education Curriculum (2025 revision) and the school's scheme of work.
The nine weeks below are the weeks that carry a written note; the remaining
weeks of the term are for revision and the end-of-term paper, so no note is
written for them.

*Depth: NERDC Primary 2 · Social and Citizenship Studies · Second Term W1-6, 8, 9, 12 (secure environment; local wildlife and conservation; national values; followership; healthy family relationships; people's beliefs and tradition; civic activities; civic activity — volunteerism; secure environment).*

---

### WEEK 1 — SECURE ENVIRONMENT

**You will learn to**

• say what a secure environment is

• keep our environment safe

• avoid danger

**Things to know**

• A **secure environment** is a place that is safe.

• We keep our **home** and **school** safe.

• We do not leave **sharp** things about.

• We do not play with **fire** or **hot** things.

• We keep the floor **dry** so we do not slip.

• We do not talk to **strangers**.

• We tell a **grown-up** if we see danger.

• We lock the **door** at night.

• A secure environment keeps us from **harm**.

• We all help to keep our environment safe.

**Let us talk**

1. What is a secure environment?

2. Name one way to keep our environment safe.

3. What do we do if we see danger?

**Words for my notebook**

• secure environment — a place that is safe

• danger — something that can harm us

• harm — to hurt or damage

**Worksheet**

1. A place that is safe is a ___ environment.
   A) secure B) dirty C) dark D) loud

2. We do not play with ___ or hot things.
   A) fire B) water C) air D) light

3. We keep the floor ___ so we do not slip.
   A) dry B) wet C) dirty D) dark

4. We tell a ___ if we see danger.
   A) grown-up B) stranger C) thief D) baby

5. Fill in: Something that can harm us is a _____.

6. Mention two ways to keep our environment safe.

7. Write what a secure environment is.

**My own work**

• Draw a safe home.

### WEEK 2 — LOCAL WILDLIFE AND CONSERVATION

**You will learn to**

• name local wildlife

• say what conservation is

• protect animals

**Things to know**

• **Wildlife** are the animals and plants in nature.

• We have **birds**, **snakes**, **monkeys** and **butterflies** around us.

• Some wildlife live in the **bush** and **forest**.

• **Conservation** means protecting wildlife.

• We do not **kill** animals for no reason.

• We do not **destroy** their homes.

• We do not cut down **trees** where animals live.

• Wildlife are part of our **environment**.

• Protecting wildlife keeps nature **balanced**.

• We care for the wildlife around us.

**Let us talk**

3. How do we protect wildlife?

**Words for my notebook**

• conservation — protecting wildlife

• environment — the place we live in

### WEEK 12 — SECURE ENVIRONMENT (REVIEW)

**You will learn to**

• review how to keep safe

• name safety rules

• live safely

**Things to know**

• We review how to keep our environment **secure**.

• We remember to keep our home and school safe.

• We recall not to play with fire or sharp things.

• We remember not to talk to strangers.

• We recall to tell a grown-up if we see danger.

• We know the **emergency number** (112).

• We recall to lock the door at night.

• A secure environment protects us from harm.

• We all have a part in keeping our environment safe.
