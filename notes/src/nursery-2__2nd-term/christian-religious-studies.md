### WEEK 1 — GOD IS OUR FATHER (PART 1)

*Depth: NERDC Primary 1 · CRS · Second Term W1 (pp 640–641).*

**You will learn to**

• say that God is my Father in heaven
• tell how God loves and cares for me
• name the good things God gives me every day
• say how a father on earth is different from God

**Things to know**

• A **father** in the family is the man who owns the house, works and takes care of the children.
• **God is my Father in heaven** — He is not in the house, He is in heaven, but He still keeps me and gives me all good things.
• God **loves** me: the Bible says God so loved the world that He gave His only Son (John 3:16).
• God **cares** for me: He gives rain, sunshine, food, sleep and the people who keep me.
• God **provides**: He gave birds food, and He gives me my daily bread.
• A father on earth is a man; he can be far away, he can be tired, he can die. God is a spirit — He is always near, never tired, and He never dies.
• A father can give a shoe; God gives the **feet** that put on the shoe.
• God's love is **perfect** — it has no mistake in it; God's love is **everlasting** — it does not finish.
• Nobody is too small for God: the little child in the class is as much God's child as the big man.
• I am not God's child because I am good; I am God's child because God chose to love me.
• We close with a small prayer: Thank you God, our Father, for today.

**Words for my notebook**

God is my Father in heaven. He loves me, cares for me and provides for me.

**Let us talk**

1. Who is your father at home?
2. Who is your Father in heaven?
3. Name three things God gave you today.
4. How is God different from the father at home?

### WEEK 2 — GOD IS OUR FATHER (PART 2)

*Depth: NERDC Primary 1 · CRS · Second Term W2 (pp 640–641).*

**You will learn to**

• say that prayer is talking to God
• tell God everything I need
• say sorry when I do wrong
• begin the Lord's Prayer

**Things to know**

• **Prayer is talking to God.** It does not need big words; it can be whispered, and God hears it.
• God **hears** every prayer, even the one said in the heart with no sound at all.
• God **answers** prayers: sometimes He says yes, sometimes wait, sometimes no — all three are answers.
• When I do wrong, I **say sorry to God**; that is confession, and God forgives and cleans me.
• God **protects** me like a father shields his child from the road, the dog and the rain.
• The **Lord's Prayer** is the prayer Jesus taught: "Our Father who art in heaven, hallowed be thy name. Thy kingdom come, thy will be done, on earth as it is in heaven."
• Give God the name He loves: Father.
• Four easy ways to pray: **thank** Him, **ask** Him, **sorry**, and **praise** Him.
• We can pray anywhere: in bed, at the table, in the bush, in class; and any time: morning, noon and night.
• A prayer may be one line: "God, keep me tonight." God is not tired of short prayers.
• Kneeling, folding hands or lifting them is the body's way of praying; the mouth's way is the word.

**Words for my notebook**

Prayer is talking to God. God hears, answers, forgives and protects.

**Let us talk**

1. What did you thank God for this morning?
2. Is prayer a big talk or a talk to a Father?
3. What do we say when we have done something wrong?
4. Say the first line of the Lord's Prayer with me.

### WEEK 3 — GOD IS OUR FATHER (PART 3)

*Depth: NERDC Primary 1 · CRS · Second Term W3 (pp 640–641).*

**You will learn to**

• say that we are all children of God
• know that God knows me well
• say that I am never alone
• trust God when I am afraid

**Things to know**

• All of us in this class are **children of God** — that makes us brothers and sisters.
• God **calls me to call Him "Father"**; a servant says master, a child says Father.
• God **knows everything** about me: my name, my face, my thoughts, what I ate, where I am.
• The Bible says God even knows the number of the hairs on my head.
• God is **always with me**: at school, at home, in the bus, in the dark, when nobody else is there.
• Because He is with me, I am **never alone**; being alone is not the same as God leaving me.
• **Trust** means depending on somebody: trusting God is leaning on Him the way I lean on a strong wall.
• What trust looks like: not crying in the dark, sharing when I am afraid of having nothing, saying sorry first, telling the truth when lying would pay me.
• Children of God do not fight over who is God's favourite; God loves each one fully.
• Fear and trust cannot sit together on the same chair — when I trust God, fear goes out of the chair.
• We say the verse together: "The Lord is my helper; I will not be afraid."

**Words for my notebook**

I am a child of God. God knows me, God is with me, I can trust God.

**Let us talk**

1. Whose children are we all?
2. Does God know your name? Does He know your thoughts?
3. When are you most afraid? Who is with you there?
4. What is one thing you can stop worrying about today?

### WEEK 4 — THINGS WE MUST DO AS CHILDREN OF GOD (PARTS 1 AND 2)

*Depth: NERDC Primary 1 · CRS · Second Term W4 (Part 1) and W7 (Part 2), pp 640–641 — the school puts two parts in one week.*

**You will learn to**

• pray in the morning, at the table and at night
• thank God and ask Him for help
• obey and respect those who keep me
• be kind to the children in my house

**Things to know**

• Part 1 is about **prayer**; Part 2 is about **obedience**.
• Praying every day: **morning prayer** before I leave the bed, **meal prayer** before I eat, **night prayer** before I sleep.
• **Thanking** God for everything He gives — food, clothes, school, health, family.
• **Asking** God for help when I need it: for strength in an exam, for protection on the road, for a sick person.
• **Saying sorry** when I do wrong, the same day, not after one week.
• A simple prayer I know: "God, thank You for today. Forgive my wrong. Help me tomorrow. In Jesus' name, Amen."
• **Obey** means do what you were told, the first time, without crying about it.
• Children, obey your parents in the Lord, for this is right (Ephesians 6:1) — the first commandment with a promise.
• **Respect and honour** my father and mother: answer them politely, do not shout them down, stand when they come, carry what they hand me.
• Be **kind to brothers and sisters**: share the chair, do not tear their book, do not tell on them for small things.
• **Help with the small work of the house**: fetch water, sweep, arrange shoes, wash the plate after eating.
• Obedience is proved by what I do when nobody is watching.

**Words for my notebook**

Morning, table and night — pray every day. Obey and respect those who keep me (Ephesians 6:1).

**Let us talk**

1. When do you pray in a day?
2. What do you say before you eat?
3. Name two things you were told to do at home today.
4. How do you show respect to your father and mother?

### WEEK 5 — THINGS WE MUST DO AS CHILDREN OF GOD (PARTS 3 AND 4)

*Depth: NERDC Primary 1 · CRS · Second Term W8 (Part 3) and W9 (Part 4), pp 640–641 — the school puts two parts in one week.*

**You will learn to**

• speak the truth when a lie would pay me
• share my things and play nicely
• forgive those who hurt me
• worship God in the church on the Lord's day

**Things to know**

• Part 3 is about **living with people**; Part 4 is about **worship**.
• Be **truthful and honest**: do not tell lies, do not take what is not yours, do not hide a broken thing and say the cat did it.
• A lie is a small gain and a great loss; the truth keeps my name clean for a long time.
• **Share** what I have: the chalk, the bench, the orange, the ball; a child of God is not tight-fisted.
• Be **friendly and play nicely**: take turns, do not push, do not laugh at the one who loses.
• **Forgive** those who offend me: if my friend broke my pen and said sorry, I let it go.
• Say sorry myself when I hurt somebody — first, before the other comes.
• Going to **church to worship God**: sit well, listen, sing, do not run in the aisle.
• In church I **listen to Bible stories** and learn about God; the Bible is God's word.
• **Singing songs of praise** is also prayer: a happy voice teaches the heart.
• **Giving offerings** is done with a cheerful heart — God loves the one who gives gladly, not the one who grumbles.
• We keep **Sunday** (the Lord's day) special for God: church, rest, and doing good.

**Words for my notebook**

Truth, sharing, forgiveness. On the Lord's day we go to church to hear God's word, sing and give.

**Let us talk**

1. Which is better: a lie that saves me or the truth that pains me for a while?
2. What do you do when somebody takes your thing?
3. What happens when you forgive?
4. Name three things you do in church.

### WEEK 6 — THINGS WE MUST DO AS CHILDREN OF GOD (PART 5)

*Depth: NERDC Primary 1 · CRS · Second Term W10 (Part 5, pp 640–641) — care for what God created.*

**You will learn to**

• take care of the things God made
• be kind to animals
• keep plants and trees from destruction
• keep my environment clean

**Things to know**

• God made the world and He said it was **very good**; so what I break, I break from His hands.
• **Kindness to animals**: give water to the dog, do not tie the goat's leg too tight, do not stone the cat, do not take the bird's eggs for fun.
• The Bible says a good man cares for the life of his beast.
• **Do not destroy plants and trees**: do not snap the young stem, do not pluck the bark, do not burn the grass round the tree; water what is planted.
• Trees give shade, fruit, clean air and wood; a cut tree takes years to be replaced.
• **Keeping the environment clean**: sweep the compound, throw refuse in the bin, do not pour water on the road, keep the drain open.
• Dirty water and blocked drains breed mosquitoes; cleanliness protects the health God gave us.
• Do not **waste** what God made: turn the tap off, use one cup of water, do not throw away half a bread.
• Do not make noise on purpose; the ears of the neighbours are part of my care too.
• Caring for creation is worship: when I look after the garden, I am serving God with my hands.
• We end with a walk round the school: pick what is on the ground, and say — God made this and gave it to us to keep.

**Words for my notebook**

God made the world and gave it to us to keep. Be kind to animals, spare the plants, keep the place clean.

**Let us talk**

1. What did God say about the world He had made?
2. How do we show kindness to the animals in our street?
3. Name two things that spoil the environment.
4. How is cleaning the compound a way of serving God?

### WEEK 8 — DISCOVERING GOD THROUGH JESUS CHRIST (PART 1)

*Depth: NERDC Primary 1 · CRS · Third Term W1 (pp 642–643).*

**You will learn to**

• say who Jesus is
• know that Jesus shows us what God is like
• name two miracles of Jesus
• say that Jesus teaches us about God's love

**Things to know**

• **Jesus is the Son of God** who came to earth as a baby in Bethlehem, born of Mary.
• Jesus came to **show us God**: nobody has seen God, but when we look at Jesus we see what God is like.
• The life of Jesus is God's **love written in action** — healing the sick, feeding the hungry, receiving the outcast.
• A **miracle** is a work that only God can do, done in front of people to show His power.
• Miracles of Jesus: water changed into wine, the five loaves and two fish feeding five thousand, the blind receiving sight, Lazarus raised from death, the storm calmed.
• Jesus had **no sin** in Him: He never lied, never hated, never took what was not His.
• Jesus **died on the cross** for our sins and **rose again** on the third day — that is the gospel we preach.
• He walked about doing good and is still alive; He sits at the right hand of God and **prays for us**.
• Learning about Jesus makes me know God better: God does not sit far off, He came near.
• The Bible that tells us this is called the New Testament; the four first books are Matthew, Mark, Luke and John.
• We say the verse together: "For God so loved the world that He gave His only begotten Son" (John 3:16).

**Words for my notebook**

Jesus is the Son of God. He shows us God, does miracles, died for our sins and rose again.

**Let us talk**

1. Where was Jesus born, and of whom?
2. What is a miracle?
3. Name two miracles of Jesus.
4. How does Jesus show us what God is like?

### WEEK 9 — DISCOVERING GOD THROUGH JESUS CHRIST (PART 2)

*Depth: NERDC Primary 1 · CRS · Third Term W2 (pp 642–643).*

**You will learn to**

• say that Jesus was a teacher
• explain what a parable is
• tell how Jesus was kind to everybody
• know that Jesus loves children

**Things to know**

• Jesus was a **teacher**; people called Him **Rabbi**, which means master teacher.
• Jesus taught mostly by **stories** called **parables** — a simple story with a deep lesson in it.
• The **sower**: the seed that fell on good ground grew and gave much fruit; the good ground is the heart that hears God's word.
• The **prodigal son** (the lost son): the boy left home and wasted all he had, but when he returned his father ran to embrace him — that is how God receives a returning sinner.
• The **good Samaritan**: a stranger helped the beaten traveller when the religious men passed by; my neighbour is anybody I can help.
• Jesus was **kind to everybody**: children, grown-ups, the sick, the poor, the woman at the well, the tax collector Zacchaeus.
• Jesus loved **children**: they were brought to Him, He took them in His arms and blessed them.
• "Suffer the little children to come unto me, and forbid them not: for of such is the kingdom of God" (Mark 10:14) — let the children come, do not stop them.
• Jesus is not only kind to the good people; He ate with sinners and forgave them.
• The way Jesus taught is the way I learn to talk: tell the truth in a story people can hold.
• What Jesus asks of me: love God, and love my neighbour as myself.

**Words for my notebook**

Jesus was a teacher who used parables. Jesus loved children and said, "Let the little children come to me."

**Let us talk**

1. What is a parable?
2. Who was the good neighbour in the story of the beaten traveller?
3. What did Jesus do when children were brought to Him?
4. Say the lesson of the sower in your own words.
