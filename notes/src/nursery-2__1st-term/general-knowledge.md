# General Knowledge — Nursery 2 · First Term

Weeks 1 to 6, 8 and 9. No note for Week 7 (Mid-Term), 10 (Revision), 11 (Examination).
*Term theme: Myself, My Family & My School. This subject is school-generated — there is no NERDC
scheme for it — so each week's extent is the school's own `Breakdown (Subtopics)` list, reproduced
line for line as bullets below.*

### WEEK 1 — MYSELF: MY NAME, MY AGE AND MY BODY

*Depth: school breakdown for N2 F1 W1.*

**Main content**

• My **full name** has two parts: my first name and my family name — "Adaeze Okafor".
• My **nickname** is the short name people at home call me — "Ada".
• I say it clearly, standing up, and I do not shout it.
• My **age**: "I am four years old." I know whether I am a **boy** or a **girl**.
• **"I am special, I am me"** — there is no other child with my face and my name.
• Parts of my body I can name: **head, eyes, ears, nose, mouth, hands, legs**.
• Senses: I **see** with my eyes, **hear** with my ears, **smell** with my nose, **taste** with my tongue, **feel** with my skin.
• Song we sing with the actions: *"Head, shoulders, knees and toes"* — twice, then fast.
• Rhyme of the week: **"I am special, I am me; I have two hands, one, two, three…"** (clap on each number).

**Words for my notebook**

My name is ______. I am ____ years old. I am a boy / a girl. I am special, I am me.

**Let us talk**

1. Stand up and say your full name.
2. What do people at home call you?
3. How old are you, and are you a boy or a girl?
4. Touch something you can smell, and tell us what you use.

**Worksheet**

1. The two parts of my full name are my first name and my A) age B) nickname C) class D) family name
2. We see with our A) eyes B) nose C) hands D) ears
3. "I am special, I am ___." A) we B) me C) they D) you
4. The name people at home call you is your A) full name B) nickname C) tribe D) school name
5. Fill in: I am ____ years old.
6. Name seven parts of your body.
7. Say which sense you use for: a bell, a flower, a mango.

**My own work**

Write your first name five times and draw your face under it.

### WEEK 2 — MY FAMILY: MEMBERS AND THEIR ROLES

*Depth: school breakdown for N2 F1 W2.*

**Main content**

• The members of my family at home: **father, mother, brothers, sisters** — and me.
• The extended family is bigger: **grandpa, grandma, uncles, aunts, cousins**.
• What each one does: father works and brings things home; mother cooks and buys food; brothers and sisters play, learn and help.
• Love and respect at home: we greet in the morning, we do not shout at a small child, we help grandpa to sit.
• At home we say **"please"** when we ask, **"thank you"** when we receive, and **"sorry"** when we do wrong.
• We share what we have: the biscuit, the crayons, the chair by the fan.
• Family song: *"This is my family — this is my family, we love one another."*
• Drawing of the week: my family, with five names written under the five people.

**Words for my notebook**

My family: father, mother, brothers, sisters. Big family: grandpa, grandma, uncles, aunts, cousins.

**Let us talk**

1. Name the people who live with you.
2. Who is your father's mother to you?
3. Say one thing your mother or father does every day.
4. What do you say when your sister gives you a cup?

**Worksheet**

1. Your mother's mother is your A) aunt B) cousin C) sister D) grandma
2. When somebody gives you something you say A) please B) sorry C) goodbye D) thank you
3. A girl who is your father's daughter is your A) aunt B) cousin C) grandma D) sister
4. Who cooks food at home? A) anybody who can help B) only the mother C) only the children D) nobody
5. Fill in: My uncle's children are my ____.
6. Name four people in your family and one thing each does.
7. Say the three polite words we use at home.

**My own work**

Draw your family and write one word under each person: father, mother, brother, sister, me.

### WEEK 3 — MY SCHOOL: PLACES, PEOPLE AND THINGS

*Depth: school breakdown for N2 F1 W3.*

**Main content**

• The name of my school: **______**. My class: **Nursery 2**. I say both out loud.
• Places in the school: **classroom** (we learn), **playground** (we play), **library** (we read), **toilet** (we freshen up), **office** (the head teacher works).
• People in the school: **the head teacher** (head of the school), **teachers** (they teach), **the caretaker** (he keeps the compound clean), **the school nurse** (she treats anyone who is hurt or sick).
• What is in my school bag: **exercise books, reader, pencils, crayons, sharpener, handkerchief, water bottle**.
• Taking care of school things: no torn pages, no writing on the desk, the bag shut, the books in the bag — not in a heap.
• School tour: we walked to the five places and said each name at the door.
• Matching game: the picture to the word — library, office, toilet.

**Words for my notebook**

My school is ______. My class is Nursery 2. Places: classroom, playground, library, toilet, office.

**Let us talk**

1. What is the name of this school?
2. Where do we read story books?
3. Who treats a pupil that has fallen?
4. Name four things inside your bag today.

**Worksheet**

1. We play during break in the A) library B) office C) toilet D) playground
2. The head of the school is the A) nurse B) head teacher C) pupil D) caretaker
3. Where do we freshen up? A) the toilet B) the store C) the library D) the office

5. Fill in: My class is Nursery ____.

### WEEK 4 — MY CLASSROOM: RULES AND MATERIALS

*Depth: school breakdown for N2 F1 W4.*

**Main content**

• Objects in the classroom: **table, chair, board, books, pencils, crayons**, plus the duster, the clock and the shelf.
• Classroom rule one: **raise your hand** before you speak or before you leave your seat.
• Rule two: **listen** when the teacher or another pupil is talking.
• Rule three: **share** the crayons, the books and the toys.
• Rule four: **keep quiet** while somebody else is answering.
• Keeping the room clean: paper goes in the **dustbin**, desks are **tidied**, the board is wiped after use.
• Colours of the things we see: **red, blue, yellow, green, black, white**.
• Game of the week: **"Find something that is red… that is round… that is soft"** — three runs, one for each clue.

**Words for my notebook**

Our rules: raise the hand, listen, share, keep quiet. The dustbin is for paper. Colours: red, blue, yellow, green, black, white.

**Let us talk**

1. Name four things in this classroom.
2. What must you do before you speak?
3. Where does a piece of wrapper go?
4. Find something yellow and bring it near the board.

**Worksheet**

1. We write on the A) bag B) chair C) board D) dustbin
2. Before speaking in class we A) raise our hand B) stand on the chair C) clap D) shout
3. Paper and wrappers go into the A) cupboard B) shoe C) dustbin D) bag
4. Which of these is a colour of a crayon? A) fast B) soft C) loud D) yellow
5. Fill in: When a friend is answering, we keep ____.
6. Mention four rules of the classroom.
7. Name three objects in the room and the colour of each.

**My own work**

Colour the classroom picture: the book red, the bag blue, the board green, the chair brown.

### WEEK 5 — MY HOME AND MY NEIGHBOURHOOD

*Depth: school breakdown for N2 F1 W5.*

**Main content**

• Rooms in my house: **sitting room** (visitors sit), **kitchen** (food is cooked), **bedroom** (we sleep), **bathroom** (we bathe).
• Some houses have a **dining room**, a **veranda**, a **store** and a **compound**.
• The people who live with me are my family; I sleep in the bedroom and I eat in the sitting room or the dining area.
• My street / village / town is called **______**; my house number or landmark is **______**.
• On my way to school I see: the shop, the bus stop, the church, the mosque, the gutter, the mango tree, my neighbour's gate.
• **Near** means not far — the gate is near. **Far** means a long walk — the market is far.
• **Up** is the hill or the shelf; **down** is the gutter or the floor.
• **"My home is my first school"** — at home I learned to walk, to speak, to greet and to hold a spoon.

**Words for my notebook**

Rooms: sitting room, kitchen, bedroom, bathroom. My street is ______. Home is my first school.

**Let us talk**

1. Where is food cooked in your house?
2. Which room do you sleep in?
3. Name two things you see on your way to school.
4. Point to something near you and something far from you.

**Worksheet**

1. We bathe in the A) kitchen B) bedroom C) store D) bathroom
2. The room where visitors sit is the A) fence B) shed C) toilet D) sitting room
3. A place a long walk away is said to be A) far B) small C) up D) near
4. We call our home our first A) church B) market C) school D) shop
5. Fill in: Food is cooked in the ____.
6. Name the four rooms of your house and what each is for.
7. Write two sentences on what you see on your way to school.

**My own work**

Draw your house with four rooms and write the name of each room inside it.

### WEEK 6 — COLOURS, SHAPES AND SIZES AROUND ME

*Depth: school breakdown for N2 F1 W6.*

**Main content**

• **Primary colours** (the colours that other colours are mixed from): **red, blue, yellow**.
• Other colours I know: **black, white, green, brown, orange, purple**.
• Shapes: **circle** (plate, ball, clock, wheel), **square** (tile, handkerchief, box face), **triangle** (roof, sandwich cut in two, ruler end), **star** (on the flag, in the sky at night).
• Sizes: **big and small** (a bag and a pencil), **long and short** (a chalk and a stick), **tall and short** (two pupils, back to back).
• Words for comparison: "This is **bigger than** that", "Mine is **shorter than** yours".
• The **Nigerian flag is green and white**: two green strips and one white strip in the middle.
• Games: **shape hunt** (find five round things), **colour sorting** (beads into six cups), **size stacking** (biggest box at the bottom).
• Mixing two colours makes a new one: red and yellow make orange; blue and white make light blue.

**Words for my notebook**

Red, blue, yellow are primary colours. Circle, square, triangle, star. Our flag is green and white.

**Let us talk**

1. Say the three primary colours.
2. Bring something round to the front.
3. Which of you is taller, you or your partner?
4. What are the colours of the Nigerian flag?

**Worksheet**

1. Which of these is a primary colour? A) pink B) purple C) brown D) yellow
2. The shape of a ball is a A) circle B) square C) star D) triangle
3. A roof is the shape of a A) triangle B) square C) line D) circle
4. The Nigerian flag is A) red and blue B) green and white C) black and yellow D) purple and pink
5. Fill in: Red and yellow mixed together make ____.
6. Name four shapes and one thing of each shape in this room.
7. Write two sentences comparing two things by size.

**My own work**

Colour three circles red, two squares blue and one triangle yellow.

### WEEK 8 — ANIMALS AND BIRDS AROUND ME

*Depth: school breakdown for N2 F1 W8.*

**Main content**

• **Domestic animals** live with people and are looked after: **dog, cat, goat, chicken, cow**, and in some homes, sheep and rabbit.
• Their sounds: dog — **woof**; cat — **miaow**; goat — **meh**; hen — **koko**; cow — **moo**.
• **Wild animals** live in the bush and are not kept in the house: **lion, elephant, monkey, snake**, leopard, buffalo.
• **Birds** have feathers, two legs and a beak, and most of them fly: **parrot, pigeon, hen, duck, eagle**.
• The **eagle** appears on our national coat of arms — it is the first look at "our national bird".
• Where animals live: **home** (dog), **farm** (cow, goat, hen), **bush** (lion, monkey), **water** (fish, duck, frog), **sky** (bird, kite).
• What animals give us: the cow gives milk, the hen gives eggs, the dog guards the house.
• Song with actions: *"Old MacDonald had a farm, E-I-E-I-O"* — each child makes the sound of one animal.
• We do not go near a strange dog, and we never touch a snake.

**Words for my notebook**

Domestic: dog, cat, goat, chicken, cow. Wild: lion, elephant, monkey, snake. Birds have feathers and beaks.

**Let us talk**

1. Which animal says "meh"?
2. Name two wild animals.
3. What does a hen give us?
4. Say two things that show an animal is a bird.
5. Where does a fish live?

**Worksheet**

1. Which animal gives us milk? A) dog B) hen C) goat D) cow
2. Which one is a wild animal? A) goat B) chicken C) cat D) lion
3. A bird has A) fur B) feathers C) four legs D) scales only
4. The sound of the dog is A) miaow B) woof C) koko D) moo
5. Fill in: The ____ is on our national coat of arms.
6. Name four animals that live on a farm.
7. Write two sentences on one animal you like and what it gives us.

**My own work**

Draw four animals, two that live with people and two that live in the bush.

### WEEK 9 — GOOD HABITS: GREETINGS, POLITENESS AND CLEANLINESS

*Depth: school breakdown for N2 F1 W9.*

**Main content**

• Morning (before noon): **"Good morning."** Afternoon: **"Good afternoon."** Evening: **"Good evening."** Bedtime: **"Good night."**
• **We greet elders first** — the grown person is greeted before a child of my age; it is a duty, not a favour.
• In Yoruba: **Ẹ̀ káàárọ̀** (morning), **Ẹ̀ káàṣánṣá** (afternoon), **Ẹ̀ káàlẹ̀** (evening). In Hausa: **Ina wuni?**, **Ina rana?**, **Ina fada?** — the reply to all is **"Lafiya."**
• The four magic words: **"please"** (asking), **"thank you"** (receiving), **"sorry"** (when I do wrong), **"excuse me"** (passing, or after sneezing).
• Hands are washed **before eating and after using the toilet** — with soap, rubbing the fingers and the nails.
• Teeth are brushed **morning and night**; the body is bathed daily; the nails are cut short.
• The uniform is kept neat: shirt in, buttons done, shoes brushed, hair combed.
• Role-play of the week: a visitor comes to the class — we stand, greet, offer a seat, and speak when spoken to.
• The Good-Habit chart on our wall: each day a tick for the habit we kept; Friday is the day we count the ticks.

**Words for my notebook**

Good morning, good afternoon, good evening, good night. Please, thank you, sorry, excuse me. We greet elders first.

**Let us talk**

1. What do you say at seven in the morning?
2. Whom do you greet first — your headmaster or your friend?
3. When do we say "excuse me"?
4. Show me how you wash your hands.
5. How many times a day do we brush our teeth?

**Worksheet**

1. At half past six in the evening we say A) hello sir B) good night C) good evening D) good morning
2. The word we use when we ask for something is A) please B) welcome C) sorry D) goodbye
3. We wash our hands A) only on Friday B) once a week C) before eating and after the toilet D) never
4. Who is greeted first? A) a dog B) a baby C) an elder D) a friend
5. Fill in: We brush our teeth ____ and night.
6. Mention the four magic words and when each is used.
7. Write two sentences on the good habit you will keep next term.

**My own work**

Write the four magic words and use each one in a sentence of your own.
