### WEEK 1 — GOD THE CREATOR (PART 1)

*Depth: NERDC Primary 1 · CRS · First Term W1 (pp 638–639).*

**Main content**

• God is the **Supreme Being**, the **Almighty** — the greatest, and there is nobody greater than Him.
• God made the world and everything in it.
• **Day 1:** God said, "Let there be light." There was light. God divided the **light from the darkness**, and called them **day and night**.
• **Day 2:** God made the **sky** and the **clouds** above the earth.
• The sun, the moon, the trees, the rivers, my eyes and my dog are all part of God's creation.
• Things that people make (a chair, a cup, a book) are not creation the way the sky is — people make them from things God already made.
• When I look at something beautiful, I can say: "Thank you, God, for Your creation."

**Words for my notebook**

God is the Almighty. God made the world. On Day 1 He made light; on Day 2 He made the sky.

**Let us talk**

1. Who made the world?
2. What did God make on the first day?
3. Name three things around you that God made.
4. What do we say when we see something beautiful?

**Worksheet**

1. God is the A) small B) guest C) neighbour D) Supreme Being
2. On Day 1 God made A) fish B) light C) stars D) birds
3. The division of light from darkness gave us A) church and mosque B) yam and rice C) day and night D) rain and wind
4. Which of these is part of God's creation? A) a plastic cup B) a school bag C) the sky D) a chair
5. Fill in: On Day 2 God made the sky and the ____.
6. Mention two things God made.
7. Write two sentences thanking God for something you saw today.

**My own work**

Write the names of five things you can see from your window that God made.

Draw three things God made on the first day and colour the light.

### WEEK 2 — GOD THE CREATOR (PART 2)

*Depth: NERDC Primary 1 · CRS · First Term W2 (pp 638–639).*

**Main content**

• **Day 3:** God made the **dry land and the seas**, and He made **grass, plants, trees and flowers**.
• **Day 4:** God made the **sun** to rule the day, the **moon** to rule the night, and the **stars**.
• **Day 5:** God made the **fish in the sea** and the **birds in the sky**, the great whales and every small creeping thing of the water.
• God told the fish and the birds to be many all over the earth.
• Each living thing has its own home: fish in water, birds in the air and on trees, animals on the land.
• Naming the creatures is part of knowing them: goat, hen, eagle, tilapia, catfish, sparrow.
• Creation songs we can sing: "All good gifts around us are from God above"; "He made the stars also."
• Because God made these things, I do not waste food, I do not break branches for fun, and I do not throw dirt into the water where fish live.

**Words for my notebook**

Day 3 — land, sea and plants. Day 4 — sun, moon and stars. Day 5 — fish and birds.

**Let us talk**

1. What did God make on the third day?
2. Which two lights did God make on the fourth day?
3. Name three living things from Day 5.
4. Mention one way we care for God's creation.

**Worksheet**

1. On Day 3 God made A) the birds B) man C) land, seas and plants D) the sun
2. The light that rules the night is the A) fire B) lamp C) moon D) torch
3. Fish and birds were made on Day A) 7 B) 5 C) 1 D) 3
4. Which of these did God NOT make on Day 4? A) goat B) sun C) stars D) moon
5. Fill in: God told the fish and the birds to be ____ on the earth.
6. Mention two things we do to care for God's creation.
7. Write two sentences on the day of creation you like best and why.

**My own work**

Draw one thing God made on Day 3, one on Day 4 and one on Day 5, and write the day under each.

### WEEK 3 — GOD THE CREATOR (PART 3)

*Depth: NERDC Primary 1 · CRS · First Term W3 (pp 638–639).*

**Main content**

• **Day 6:** God made the **animals of the land** — cattle, creeping things and wild beasts — and then He made **man and woman**.
• God made people **in His own image**: like God in the way we think, love, speak and choose what is right.
• God told the first people to be kind, to have children, and to take care of the earth.
• **Day 7:** God **rested** from all His work, and He made that day holy.
• Rest is not laziness: God Himself rested, so rest is good for the body that works.
• On the seventh day people go to church or mosque to **worship** God and thank Him.
• I am special because God made me — not by chance, but on purpose, as part of the last day of creation.
• A prayer of thanks: "Thank You, God, for making me and for making everything."

**Words for my notebook**

On Day 6 God made animals and people. On Day 7 He rested. We are made in God's image.

**Let us talk**

1. Whom did God make on Day 6?
2. What did God do on Day 7?
3. Say one thing that shows you are made in God's image.
4. Why do we go to church or mosque on the seventh day?

**Worksheet**

1. On Day 6 God made A) light B) animals and people C) the sea D) the stars
2. People are made in the image of A) the king B) their friends C) the sun D) God
3. God rested on Day A) 6 B) 5 C) 1 D) 7
4. The first people God made were A) a king and a queen B) twins C) two brothers D) a man and a woman
5. Fill in: We are special because ____ made us.
6. Mention two things God made on Day 6.
7. Write two sentences on why rest and worship are good for us.

**My own work**

### WEEK 4 — GOD, THE GIVER OF GOOD THINGS (PART 1)

*Depth: NERDC Primary 1 · CRS · First Term W4 (pp 638–639).*

**Main content**

• God is the One who gives us good things, and we thank Him for them.
• God gives us **life and breath** — the fact that I am awake this morning is a gift.
• God gives us **families**: father, mother, brothers, sisters and relatives.
• God gives us **homes** to live in — a room to sleep in, a place to eat, a door to shut at night.
• Nobody can add one hour to their life by worrying; the days we live are God's gift.
• When a good thing comes to me, I do two things: I say "Thank you, God", and I share it.
• A home is kept clean and peaceful by everybody in it — that too is part of valuing the gift.

**Words for my notebook**

God gives me life, my family and my home. I thank Him and I share.

**Let us talk**

1. Who gives us life and breath?
2. Name the people in your family that you thank God for.
3. What is one good thing about your home?
4. What two things do we do when something good comes to us?

**Worksheet**

2. The people God puts around us first are our A) dogs B) strangers C) enemies D) family
3. Our home is a gift from A) the market B) God C) the shop D) the neighbour
4. When somebody gives me something good, I A) thank God and share B) fight C) hide it D) boast
5. Fill in: God gives us ____ to live in.
6. Mention three good things God has given you.
7. Write two sentences of thanks to God for your family.

**My own work**

Write the names of four people in your family and one good thing each of them does.

Draw four good things God gives us and colour the one you like best.

### WEEK 5 — GOD, THE GIVER OF GOOD THINGS (PART 2)

*Depth: NERDC Primary 1 · CRS · First Term W7 (pp 638–639).*

**Main content**

• God gives us **food to eat**: fruits (mango, banana, watermelon, orange), vegetables (ewédú, ugu, spinach), rice, yam, beans, cassava and fish.
• The rain that waters the farm comes from God; the farmer who plants is also God's help.
• God gives us **water to drink** — clean water keeps the body well.
• God gives us **clothes to wear** — cotton grown from the earth, cloth woven and dyed.
• God gives us **schools and teachers** so that we can learn to read, to count and to know right from wrong.
• Before a meal we say: "Thank You, God, for this food." We do not throw food on the floor and we finish what we take.
• Daily provision means: today's food for today, and thanks today — not keeping greed for tomorrow.

**Words for my notebook**

God gives food, water, clothes, schools and teachers. We thank God before we eat.

**Let us talk**

4. What do we say before a meal?

### WEEK 6 — GOD, THE GIVER OF GOOD THINGS (PART 3)

*Depth: NERDC Primary 1 · CRS · First Term W8 (pp 638–639).*

**Main content**

• God gives us **friends to play with** — a friend is a gift, not something we buy.
• God gives us **health and strength**: a body that runs, eyes that see, a mouth that speaks.
• God gives us the **sun** for light and warmth, the **rain** for the farm, and good **weather** for our journeys.
• God **protects us from danger** — from the road, from water, from a bad fall, from people who mean harm.
• Thanking God is called **praise**; asking God for help is called **petition**; both belong in our prayers.
• A short prayer of thanks: "Thank You, God, for my friends, for my body, for the sun and the rain. Keep me from danger."
• Before crossing a busy road or going near water, we still ask God's help — and we also use what we were taught to do.

**Words for my notebook**

Friends, health, sun, rain and safety are God's gifts. We thank God and we ask for protection.

**Let us talk**

1. Name one gift from God that you cannot buy with money.
2. What does God give that makes crops grow?
3. How does God protect us?
4. What is the difference between praise and petition?

**Worksheet**

### WEEK 8 — GOD'S GIFT OF HIS SON (PART 1)

*Depth: NERDC Primary 1 · CRS · First Term W9 (pp 638–639).*

**Main content**

• **John 3:16** — God loved the world so much that He gave His only Son, so that whoever believes in Him should not perish but have everlasting life.
• God sent **His Son Jesus** into the world. This is God's greatest gift.
• **Mary** was a young woman who lived in Nazareth; an angel told her that she would have a son. **Joseph** was a carpenter, and he took care of Mary.
• Jesus was born in **Bethlehem**, and He was laid in a **manger** — a feeding trough for animals — because there was no room in the inn.
• **Angels** appeared to **shepherds** who were watching their flocks at night, and said: "Fear not: unto you is born this day a Saviour, which is Christ the Lord."
• The shepherds went and saw the baby, and they told everybody what the angels had said.
• Wise men later came from the east following a star — that part of the story belongs to next week.

**Words for my notebook**

God loved the world and sent Jesus, His Son. Jesus was born in Bethlehem in a manger.

4. Who heard the angels first?

### WEEK 9 — GOD'S GIFT OF HIS SON (PART 2)

*Depth: NERDC Primary 1 · CRS · First Term W10 (pp 638–639).*

**Main content**

• **Jesus is God's greatest gift** to us, and a gift is received with thanks.
• Wise men from the east came following the star, and they brought gifts: **gold, frankincense and myrrh**.
• **Gold** is for a king; **frankincense** is a sweet perfume burned in worship; **myrrh** is an ointment used to prepare a body for burial — it told us ahead of time that Jesus came to die for us.
• Why God sent Jesus: **to save us from sin** and **to show us God's love**.
• Jesus came to **teach us how to live**: to love God, to love our neighbour, to forgive, to care for the poor and the small.
• We remember Jesus' birth at **Christmas**, on the 25th of December: churches have services, families share food, and children receive gifts.
• The best Christmas gift we can give is a kind act — the same love we received.

**Words for my notebook**

The wise men brought gold, frankincense and myrrh. God sent Jesus to save us and to show us love.

**Let us talk**

1. What three gifts did the wise men bring?
2. Why did God send Jesus?
3. Name two things Jesus taught us.
4. What do people do at Christmas?

6. Mention two reasons why God sent Jesus.
