# Basic Science — Primary 1 · Third Term

These are the notes for the third term of Primary 1. They follow the NERDC
Basic Education Curriculum (2025 revision) and the school's scheme of work.
The eight weeks below are the weeks that carry a written note; the remaining
weeks of the term are for revision and the end-of-term paper, so no note is
written for them.

*Depth: NERDC Primary 1 · Basic Science · Third Term W1-6, 8-9 (energy; push and pull; simple machines; growth and change; electricity and safety; magnets; waste and recycling; caring for our environment).*

---

### WEEK 1 — ENERGY AROUND US

**You will learn to**

• say what energy is

• name sources of energy

• say what energy does

**Things to know**

• **Energy** is the power to do work.

• The **sun** gives us light and heat energy.

• A **fire** gives heat energy.

• A **battery** gives energy to a torch.

• **Food** gives our body energy.

• **Electricity** gives energy to lamps and fans.

• Energy helps us to work and play.

• We cannot see energy, but we see what it does.

• The sun is the biggest source of energy.

• We need energy every day.

**Let us talk**

1. What is energy?

2. Name one source of energy.

3. What gives our body energy?

**Words for my notebook**

• energy — the power to do work

• sun — the biggest source of energy

• battery — a thing that stores energy

**Worksheet**

1. Energy is the power to do ___.
   A) work B) sleep C) nothing D) harm

2. The biggest source of energy is the ___.
   A) sun B) moon C) star D) stone

3. Food gives our body ___.
   A) energy B) water only C) dust D) sleep

4. A ___ gives energy to a torch.
   A) battery B) stone C) leaf D) cloth

5. Fill in: Electricity gives energy to lamps and _____.

6. Mention two sources of energy.

7. Write what energy is.

**My own work**

• Draw the sun and one thing that uses energy.

### WEEK 2 — PUSH AND PULL

**You will learn to**

• say what a push is

• say what a pull is

• push and pull objects

**Things to know**

• A **push** moves a thing away from us.

• A **pull** moves a thing towards us.

• Push and pull are **forces**.

• A **force** is a push or a pull.

• We push a door to close it.

• We pull a door to open it.

• We push a trolley forward.

• We pull a rope in a tug of war.

• A force can move or stop a thing.

• We use force every day.

**Let us talk**

1. What is a push?

2. What is a pull?

3. Name one thing you push.

**Words for my notebook**

• push — to move a thing away

• pull — to move a thing towards us

• force — a push or a pull

**Worksheet**

1. A push moves a thing ___ from us.
   A) away B) towards C) under D) over

2. A pull moves a thing ___ us.
   A) towards B) away C) beside D) behind

3. A push or a pull is a ___.
   A) force B) song C) colour D) sound

4. We ___ a door to close it.
   A) push B) pull C) sing D) hide

5. Fill in: We pull a rope in a tug of _____.

6. Mention two things you push or pull.

7. Write what a force is.

**My own work**

• Draw myself pushing a trolley.

### WEEK 4 — GROWTH AND CHANGE

**You will learn to**

• say how living things grow

• name the stages of growth

• care for my growth

**Things to know**

• Living things **grow** and change.

• A baby grows into a child, then a grown-up.

• A seed grows into a plant.

• An egg hatches into a chick.

• Growth needs good **food** and **water**.

• Growth needs **rest** and **care**.

• We measure growth by height and weight.

• Plants and animals grow.

• Growth takes time.

• Good food helps us to grow strong.

**Let us talk**

1. How does a baby grow?

2. What does a seed grow into?

3. What helps us to grow?

**Words for my notebook**

• grow — to get bigger

• stage — a step in growth

• hatch — when a baby comes out of an egg

**Worksheet**

1. Living things ___ and change.
   A) grow B) shrink C) stop D) hide

2. A seed grows into a ___.
   A) plant B) stone C) bird D) fish

3. An egg hatches into a ___.
   A) chick B) dog C) seed D) stone

4. Growth needs good food and ___.
   A) water B) smoke C) dust D) noise

5. Fill in: To get bigger is to _____.

6. Mention two things that help us grow.

7. Write what a seed grows into.

### WEEK 5 — ELECTRICITY AND SAFETY

**You will learn to**

• say what electricity does

• name things that use electricity

• be safe with electricity

**Things to know**

• **Electricity** gives energy to many things.

• Electricity lights the **bulb**.

• Electricity runs the **fan** and **television**.

• Electricity comes through **wires**.

• We must be careful with electricity.

• We do not touch a bare wire.

• We do not put our fingers in a socket.

• We keep water away from electric things.

• We tell a grown-up if a wire is broken.

• Electricity is useful but can be dangerous.

**Let us talk**

1. What does electricity do?

2. Name one thing that uses electricity.

3. How do we stay safe with electricity?

**Words for my notebook**

• electricity — energy that comes through wires

• bulb — a thing that gives light

• socket — a place where we plug in

   A) wires B) ropes C) strings D) threads

3. We do not put our fingers in a ___.
   A) socket B) cup C) bag D) box

4. We keep ___ away from electric things.
   A) water B) air C) light D) sound

5. Fill in: Electricity is useful but can be _____.

6. Mention two things that use electricity.

7. Write one way to be safe with electricity.

### WEEK 6 — MAGNETS

**You will learn to**

• say what a magnet is

• say what a magnet attracts

• use a magnet

**Things to know**

• A **magnet** is a thing that pulls some metals.

• A magnet attracts **iron** and **steel**.

• A magnet does not attract wood, plastic or paper.

• The pulling power of a magnet is **magnetism**.

• A magnet has two ends called **poles**.

• A magnet can pull a nail or a pin.

• A magnet works through thin things.

• We use magnets to pick up pins.

• A compass uses a magnet to show direction.

• Magnets are useful tools.

**Let us talk**

1. What is a magnet?

2. What does a magnet attract?

3. Name one thing a magnet does not attract.

**Words for my notebook**

• magnet — a thing that pulls some metals

• attract — to pull towards

• pole — an end of a magnet

**Worksheet**

1. A magnet attracts iron and ___.
   A) steel B) wood C) paper D) plastic

2. A magnet does not attract ___.
   A) wood B) iron C) steel D) a pin

3. The pulling power of a magnet is ___.
   A) magnetism B) gravity C) force only D) light

4. A magnet has two ends called ___.
   A) poles B) sides C) corners D) edges

5. Fill in: To pull towards is to _____.

6. Mention two things a magnet attracts.

7. Write what a magnet is.

**My own work**

• Draw a magnet picking up pins.

### WEEK 8 — WASTE AND RECYCLING

**You will learn to**

• say what waste is

• name ways to handle waste

• reuse and recycle

**Things to know**

• **Waste** is a thing we no longer need.

• We put waste in the **dustbin**.

• We do not throw waste on the ground.

• Some waste we can **reuse**.

• Some waste we can **recycle** into new things.

• We can recycle paper, plastic and metal.

• Burning waste makes the air dirty.

• Keeping waste well keeps our environment clean.

• We separate waste to recycle it.

• Less waste means a cleaner world.

**Let us talk**

1. What is waste?

2. Where do we put waste?

3. What is recycling?

**Words for my notebook**

• waste — a thing we no longer need

• reuse — to use a thing again

• recycle — to make waste into new things

**Worksheet**

1. A thing we no longer need is ___.
   A) waste B) food C) money D) a friend

2. We put waste in the ___.
   A) dustbin B) river C) road D) desk

3. To make waste into new things is to ___.
   A) recycle B) burn C) hide D) lose

4. Burning waste makes the air ___.
   A) dirty B) clean C) fresh D) cold

5. Fill in: To use a thing again is to _____.

6. Mention two things we can recycle.

7. Write where we put waste.

**My own work**

• Draw a dustbin and one thing I can recycle.

### WEEK 9 — CARING FOR OUR ENVIRONMENT

**You will learn to**

• say what the environment is

• name ways to care for it

• keep our environment clean

**Things to know**

• The **environment** is all that is around us.

• Our environment has air, water, land, plants and animals.

• We keep the environment clean by not littering.

• We use the dustbin for our rubbish.

• We plant trees and care for plants.

• We do not waste water.

• We do not burn rubbish.

• A clean environment keeps us healthy.

• A dirty environment breeds germs and flies.

• We all share the work of caring for the environment.

**Let us talk**

2. Name one way to care for the environment.

**Words for my notebook**
