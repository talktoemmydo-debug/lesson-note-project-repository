# Prevocational Studies — Primary 1 · Third Term

These are the notes for the third term of Primary 1. They follow the NERDC
Basic Education Curriculum (2025 revision) and the school's scheme of work.
The eight weeks below are the weeks that carry a written note; the remaining
weeks of the term are for revision and the end-of-term paper, so no note is
written for them.

*Depth: NERDC Primary 1 · Prevocational Studies · Third Term W1-6, 8-9 (work at home and in the community; simple food preparation; care of clothing; crafts; caring for our environment; animals and their care; saving and spending; work we may do when we grow up).*

---

### WEEK 1 — WORK AT HOME AND IN THE COMMUNITY

**You will learn to**

• name work we do at home

• name work in the community

• help in our work

**Things to know**

• **Work** is what we do to help and to live well.

• At home we **sweep**, **fetch water** and **wash**.

• At home we help to **cook** and **care for the house**.

• In the community people **farm**, **trade** and **build**.

• Teachers, doctors and drivers work in the community.

• Every honest work is good.

• We help our family with our own work.

• Work keeps our home and community clean and well.

• We work with care and not lazily.

• Doing our work well makes others happy.

**Let us talk**

1. What is work?

2. Name one work we do at home.

3. Name one work in the community.

**Words for my notebook**

• work — what we do to help and to live well

• community — the people and place we live with

• help — to make work easier for others

**Worksheet**

1. What we do to help and to live well is ___.
   A) work B) play C) sleep D) cry

2. At home we ___ the floor.
   A) sweep B) burn C) break D) hide

3. In the community people ___ and trade.
   A) farm B) sleep C) cry D) fight

4. Every ___ work is good.
   A) honest B) lazy C) bad D) rude

5. Fill in: The people and place we live with are our _____.

6. Mention two works we do at home.

7. Write why work is good.

**My own work**

• Draw someone working at home.

### WEEK 2 — SIMPLE FOOD PREPARATION

**You will learn to**

• name simple foods we prepare

• help to prepare food

• keep food clean

**Things to know**

• We help to prepare simple **foods**.

• We can **wash** fruits and vegetables.

• We can **peel** and **cut** soft food with care.

• We help to **stir** and **serve** food.

• We wash our **hands** before we touch food.

• We keep food **covered** so flies do not touch it.

• We use clean **utensils** to cook.

• We do not play with sharp knives.

• Clean food keeps us from sickness.

• Helping to prepare food is a good work.

**Let us talk**

1. Name one simple food we can prepare.

2. What do we do before we touch food?

3. Why do we cover food?

**Words for my notebook**

• prepare — to make food ready to eat

• utensils — the pots, plates and spoons we use

• covered — kept with something over it

**Worksheet**

1. To make food ready to eat is to ___ it.
   A) prepare B) waste C) burn D) hide

2. We wash our ___ before we touch food.
   A) hands B) shoes C) books D) chairs

3. We keep food ___ so flies do not touch it.
   A) covered B) open C) dirty D) wet

4. We use clean ___ to cook.
   A) utensils B) stones C) leaves D) paper

5. Fill in: The pots, plates and spoons we use are _____.

6. Mention two things we do to prepare food.

7. Write why we keep food clean.

### WEEK 3 — CARE OF CLOTHING

**You will learn to**

• care for our clothes

• wash and keep clothes

• mend simple clothes

**Things to know**

• We keep our **clothes** clean.

• We **wash** our clothes with soap and water.

• We **dry** clothes in the sun.

• We **fold** and keep clothes in a place.

• We **iron** clothes to make them neat.

• If a button falls, a grown-up helps to **mend** it.

• We do not play in the mud with clean clothes.

• We wear clean clothes every day.

• Clean clothes keep us neat and healthy.

• Care of clothing is a good work at home.

**Let us talk**

1. How do we wash clothes?

2. Where do we dry clothes?

3. Why do we keep clothes clean?

**Words for my notebook**

• clothing — the clothes we wear

• mend — to repair something that is torn

• fold — to bend clothes neatly

**Worksheet**

1. We wash our clothes with soap and ___.
   A) water B) sand C) oil D) ink

3. We ___ clothes to make them neat.
   A) iron B) burn C) cut D) hide

4. If a button falls, a grown-up helps to ___ it.
   A) mend B) break C) eat D) lose

7. Write why we keep clothes clean.

### WEEK 4 — CRAFTS: MAKING THINGS

**You will learn to**

• name things we make

• make simple crafts

• use materials well

**Things to know**

• A **craft** is something we make with our hands.

• We can make a **basket** from leaves or straw.

• We can make a **mat** from straw.

• We can make **beads** and **pots** from clay.

• We use **paper**, **clay**, **leaves** and **thread**.

• We cut and paste paper to make shapes.

• We weave to make baskets and mats.

• Crafts can be sold to make money.

• Making crafts is fun and useful.

• We keep our craft materials well.

**Let us talk**

1. What is a craft?

2. Name one craft we can make.

3. What materials do we use?

**Words for my notebook**

• craft — something we make with our hands

• weave — to cross threads or straw to make things

• material — what we use to make something

**Worksheet**

1. Something we make with our hands is a ___.
   A) craft B) song C) dance D) dream

2. We can make a basket from leaves or ___.
   A) straw B) stone C) water D) sand

3. We ___ to make baskets and mats.
   A) weave B) sleep C) run D) cry

4. We use paper, clay, leaves and ___.
   A) thread B) fire C) glass D) iron

5. Fill in: What we use to make something is a _____.

6. Mention two crafts we can make.

7. Write what a craft is.

• We care for our environment every day.

• A clean environment is good for all.

• name animals we keep

• care for animals

• say what animals give us

• We give animals a clean **shelter**.

• We do not beat or hurt animals.

• Dogs help to **guard** our home.

• Kindness to animals is good.

• Caring for animals is a useful work.

### WEEK 8 — SAVING AND SPENDING

**You will learn to**

• say what saving is

• spend money well

• keep money safe

**Things to know**

• **Saving** is keeping money for later.

• We save money in a **money box** or bank.

• We do not spend all our money at once.

• We buy what we **need** first.

• We do not waste money on things we do not need.

• Saving helps us when we need money later.

• We keep our money in a safe place.

• We do not take money that is not ours.

• Good spending and saving help our family.

• Learning to save is wise.

**Let us talk**

1. What is saving?

2. Where do we save money?

3. Why do we save?

**Words for my notebook**

• saving — keeping money for later

• spend — to use money to buy things

• need — something we must have

**Worksheet**

1. Keeping money for later is ___.
   A) saving B) wasting C) losing D) throwing

2. We save money in a money box or ___.
   A) bank B) fire C) river D) dustbin

3. We buy what we ___ first.
   A) need B) lose C) break D) hide

4. We do not ___ money on things we do not need.
   A) waste B) save C) keep D) hide

5. Fill in: To use money to buy things is to _____.

6. Mention two ways to use money well.

7. Write why we save money.

**My own work**

• Draw a money box.
