These are the notes for the third term of Primary 1. They follow the NERDC
Basic Education Curriculum (2025 revision) and the school's scheme of work.

### WEEK 1 — ORÍ Ọ̀RỌ̀ (TONES)

**You will learn to**

• say the Yoruba tones

• use tones in words

• hear the tones well

**Things to know**

• Yoruba has three **tones** (orí ọ̀rọ̀).

• The tones are **dò** (low), **rè** (middle) and **mí** (high).

• We mark dò with a grave ( \` ), rè with none, and mí with an acute ( ´ ).

• The tone changes the **meaning** of a word.

• **Bà** (to meet) and **bá** (to meet with) are not the same.

• We must use the right tone when we speak.

• We listen well to hear the tones.

• Tones make Yoruba a **tonal** language.

• We practise saying words with their tones.

• Good tones make our Yoruba clear.

**Let us talk**

1. How many tones does Yoruba have?

2. Name the three tones.

3. Why are tones important?

**Words for my notebook**

• orí ọ̀rọ̀ — the tone of a word

• dò — the low tone

• mí — the high tone

**Worksheet**

1. Yoruba has ___ tones.
   A) three B) one C) five D) ten

2. The low tone is ___.
   A) dò B) mí C) rè D) kò

3. The high tone is ___.
   A) mí B) dò C) rè D) bẹ̀

4. The tone changes the ___ of a word.
   A) meaning B) colour C) size D) smell

5. Fill in: The middle tone is _____.

6. Mention the three Yoruba tones.

7. Write why tones are important.

**My own work**

• Write three words and mark their tones.

### WEEK 2 — KÍKA TÍTÍ DÉ ỌGỌ́RÙN-ÚN (COUNTING TO 100)

**You will learn to**

• count in Yoruba to 100

• say big numbers

• count things

**Things to know**

• We can count in **Yoruba** from 1 to 100.

• 1 = **ọ̀kan**, 2 = **èjì**, 3 = **ẹ̀ta**, 4 = **ẹ̀rin**, 5 = **àrún**.

• 6 = **ẹ̀fà**, 7 = **èje**, 8 = **ẹ̀jọ**, 9 = **ẹ̀sán**, 10 = **ẹ̀wá**.

• 20 = **ogún**, 30 = **ọgbọ̀n**, 40 = **ọgọ́rin**.

• 50 = **àádọ́ta**, 60 = **ọgọ́ta**, 70 = **àádọ́rin**.

• 80 = **ọgọ́rin**, 90 = **àádọ́rùn-ún**, 100 = **ọgọ́rùn-ún**.

• We count things at home and market.

• Counting helps us in trade and school.

**Let us talk**

1. Say 1 to 10 in Yoruba.

2. What is 20 in Yoruba?

3. What is 100 in Yoruba?

**Words for my notebook**

• kíka — counting

• ọgọ́rùn-ún — one hundred

• nọ́mbà — number

**Worksheet**

1. 5 in Yoruba is ___.
   A) àrún B) ọ̀kan C) èjì D) ẹ̀wá

2. 10 in Yoruba is ___.
   A) ẹ̀wá B) ogún C) ẹ̀ta D) ẹ̀fà

3. 20 in Yoruba is ___.
   A) ogún B) ọ̀kan C) àrún D) ẹ̀jọ

4. 100 in Yoruba is ___.
   A) ọgọ́rùn-ún B) ogún C) ọgbọ̀n D) ẹ̀wá

5. Fill in: 3 in Yoruba is _____.

6. Mention five numbers in Yoruba.

7. Write what 50 is in Yoruba.

**My own work**

• Write and trace the numbers 1 to 10 in Yoruba.

### WEEK 3 — ỌJỌ́ ÀTI OṢÙ (DAYS AND MONTHS)

**You will learn to**

• name the days in Yoruba

• name the months in Yoruba

• say the day today

**Things to know**

• There are **seven** days in a week.

• The days are **Àìkú**, **Ajé**, **Ìsẹ́gun**, **Ọjọ́rú**, **Ọjọ́bọ̀**, **Ẹtì**, **Àbámẹ́ta**.

• Àìkú is **Sunday**, Ajé is **Monday**.

• Ìsẹ́gun is **Tuesday**, Ọjọ́rú is **Wednesday**.

• Ọjọ́bọ̀ is **Thursday**, Ẹtì is **Friday**, Àbámẹ́ta is **Saturday**.

• There are **twelve** months in a year.

• The months are **oṣù** — like Oṣù Ṣẹ́rẹ́ (January).

• We say the day and month today.

• Knowing days and months helps us plan.

**Let us talk**

1. How many days are in a week?

2. What is Monday in Yoruba?

3. What is today's day?

**Words for my notebook**

• ọjọ́ — day

• oṣù — month

• ọ̀sẹ̀ — week

### WEEK 4 — ỌJÀ (THE MARKET)

**You will learn to**

• name things in the market

• buy and sell

• behave well in the market

**Things to know**

• The **market** (ọjà) is where we buy and sell.

• We buy **food**, **cloth** and other things at the market.

• The person who sells is a **seller** (olóògbà).

• The person who buys is a **buyer** (olùrà).

• We use **money** (owó) to buy things.

• We can **bargain** (ná) for a good price.

• We behave well and do not steal.

• We greet the sellers.

• The market is busy and full of people.

• Going to market is a useful work.

### WEEK 8 — ÒWE (PROVERBS)

**You will learn to**

• say what a proverb is

• say simple proverbs

• use proverbs well

**Things to know**

• A **proverb** (òwe) is a short saying with wisdom.

• Proverbs teach us good things.

• "Ọmọ tí a kò kọ́, ni yóò gbé ilé tí a kọ́ tà" — a child not taught will sell the house built.

• "Bí a bá ń lọ, a ń bọ̀" — as we go, we return.

• "Ẹni tó mọ̀ ọ́n, ò mọ̀ ọ́n" — one who knows you does not know you.

• Proverbs make our speech sweet.

• Elders use proverbs to teach.

• We learn proverbs from our elders.

• Proverbs are part of our culture.

**Let us talk**

1. What is a proverb?

2. Say one proverb in Yoruba.

3. What do proverbs teach us?

**Words for my notebook**

• òwe — proverb

• ọ̀rọ̀ — word or saying

• ọgbọ́n — wisdom

**Worksheet**

1. A short saying with wisdom is a ___.
