# General Knowledge — Primary 1 · Third Term

These are the notes for the third term of Primary 1. They follow the NERDC
Basic Education Curriculum (2025 revision) and the school's scheme of work.
The eight weeks below are the weeks that carry a written note; the remaining
weeks of the term are for revision and the end-of-term paper, so no note is
written for them.

*Depth: school scheme of work, Primary 1 · General Knowledge · Third Term W1-6, 8-9 (transportation; communication; money and trade; natural resources; technology around us; rules and laws; being a responsible child; our neighbours and the world).*

---

### WEEK 1 — TRANSPORTATION

**You will learn to**

• say what transportation is

• name means of transport

• travel safely

**Things to know**

• **Transportation** is moving people and goods from place to place.

• We travel by **road**, **rail**, **water** and **air**.

• A **car**, **bus** and **bicycle** move on the road.

• A **train** moves on the rail.

• A **boat** and **ship** move on water.

• An **aeroplane** moves in the air.

• Transportation helps us to go far.

• We wear a seat belt in a car.

• We do not stand in a moving bus.

• Safe travel keeps us from harm.

**Let us talk**

1. What is transportation?

2. Name one means of transport.

3. How do we travel safely?

**Words for my notebook**

• transportation — moving people and goods

• road — the way cars and buses move

• aeroplane — an aircraft that flies

**Worksheet**

1. Moving people and goods from place to place is ___.
   A) transportation B) sleeping C) eating D) singing

2. A train moves on the ___.
   A) rail B) road C) water D) air

3. A boat and a ship move on ___.
   A) water B) road C) rail D) sky

4. An ___ moves in the air.
   A) aeroplane B) bus C) train D) car

5. Fill in: In a car we wear a seat _____.

6. Mention two means of transport.

7. Write what transportation is.

**My own work**

• Draw one means of transport.

### WEEK 3 — MONEY AND TRADE

**You will learn to**

• say what money is

• say what trade is

• use money well

**Things to know**

• **Money** is what we use to buy and sell.

• Nigerian money is the **naira** and **kobo**.

• We have **coins** and **notes**.

• **Trade** is buying and selling.

• We trade at the **market** and in **shops**.

• A person who buys is a **customer**.

• A person who sells is a **seller**.

• Money helps us to get what we need.

• We save money for later.

• We use money wisely.

**Let us talk**

1. What is money?

2. What is trade?

3. Where do we trade?

**Words for my notebook**

• money — what we use to buy and sell

• trade — buying and selling

• market — a place where we buy and sell

**Worksheet**

1. What we use to buy and sell is ___.
   A) money B) water C) dust D) stone

2. Nigerian money is the naira and ___.
   A) kobo B) cents C) pounds D) dollars

3. Buying and selling is ___.
   A) trade B) sleep C) play D) fight

4. A person who sells is a ___.
   A) seller B) buyer C) singer D) dancer

5. Fill in: We trade at the market and in _____.

6. Mention two things we buy with money.

7. Write what trade is.

**My own work**

• We come to school on time.

• We do our homework.

### WEEK 9 — OUR NEIGHBOURS AND THE WORLD

**You will learn to**

• name countries near Nigeria

• say who our neighbours are

• live well with others

**Things to know**

• Nigeria is in **West Africa**.

• Nigeria has countries around it.

• **Niger** is to the north.

• **Chad** is to the north-east.

• **Cameroon** is to the east.

• **Benin** is to the west.

• To the south is the **Atlantic Ocean**.

• These countries are our **neighbours**.

• The world has many countries and people.

• We are friends with our neighbours and the world.

**Let us talk**

1. Where is Nigeria?

2. Name one country near Nigeria.

3. What is to the south of Nigeria?

**Words for my notebook**

• neighbour — a country or person near us

• West Africa — the part of Africa where Nigeria is

• world — the earth and all its people

**Worksheet**

1. Nigeria is in ___ Africa.
   A) West B) East C) North D) South

2. ___ is to the north of Nigeria.
   A) Niger B) Ghana C) Egypt D) Kenya

3. ___ is to the east of Nigeria.
   A) Cameroon B) Ghana C) Egypt D) Togo

4. To the south of Nigeria is the ___ Ocean.
   A) Atlantic B) Indian C) Pacific D) Red

5. Fill in: A country near us is our _____.

6. Mention two countries near Nigeria.

7. Write what is to the south of Nigeria.
