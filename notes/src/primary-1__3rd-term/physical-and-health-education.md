# Physical and Health Education — Primary 1 · Third Term

These are the notes for the third term of Primary 1. They follow the NERDC
Basic Education Curriculum (2025 revision) and the school's scheme of work.
The eight weeks below are the weeks that carry a written note; the remaining
weeks of the term are for revision and the end-of-term paper, so no note is
written for them.

*Depth: NERDC Primary 1 · Physical and Health Education · Third Term W1-6, 8-9 (balance and coordination; ball skills; relay races; water safety; simple first aid; care of my teeth and skin; saying no to harmful things; fitness and games).*

---

### WEEK 1 — BALANCE AND COORDINATION

**You will learn to**

• balance the body

• move with coordination

• do balance activities

**Things to know**

• **Balance** is staying steady without falling.

• We can balance on one foot.

• We can balance on a line or a beam.

• **Coordination** is using parts of the body together.

• We coordinate our eyes and hands when we catch.

• We coordinate our arms and legs when we run.

• Balance and coordination help us in games.

• We practise to get better.

• We do balance activities on a mat to be safe.

• Good balance keeps us from falling.

**Let us talk**

1. What is balance?

2. What is coordination?

3. Name one balance activity.

**Words for my notebook**

• balance — staying steady without falling

• coordination — using body parts together

• steady — not shaking or falling

**Worksheet**

1. Staying steady without falling is ___.
   A) balance B) sleep C) noise D) dust

2. Using body parts together is ___.
   A) coordination B) anger C) hunger D) fear

3. We can balance on ___ foot.
   A) one B) no C) a hundred D) zero

4. We coordinate our eyes and ___ when we catch.
   A) hands B) ears C) nose D) hair

5. Fill in: Not shaking or falling is being _____.

6. Mention two balance activities.

7. Write what coordination is.

**My own work**

• Draw myself balancing on one foot.

### WEEK 2 — BALL SKILLS: STRIKING AND KICKING

**You will learn to**

• strike a ball

• kick a ball

• control a ball

**Things to know**

• We can **strike** a ball with the hand.

• We can **kick** a ball with the foot.

• We can **stop** or control a ball.

• We watch the ball as it comes.

• We kick with the inside of the foot.

• We strike gently or hard.

• Ball skills are used in football and other games.

• We practise striking and kicking to get better.

• We play with care and not roughly.

• Ball skills keep us fit.

**Let us talk**

### WEEK 3 — RELAY RACES

**You will learn to**

• say what a relay race is

• run in a relay

• pass the baton

**Things to know**

• A **relay race** is a race in teams.

• Each runner runs a part of the race.

• The runner passes a **baton** to the next.

• The **baton** is a small stick.

• We pass the baton without dropping it.

• The last runner finishes the race.

• The first team to finish wins.

• We run fast and pass well.

• Relay races need teamwork.

• Relay races are fun.

**Let us talk**

1. What is a relay race?

2. What do we pass in a relay?

3. Why do relay races need teamwork?

**Words for my notebook**

• relay race — a race in teams

• baton — the stick passed in a relay

• team — a group that plays together

**Worksheet**

   A) dropping B) eating C) burning D) hiding

### WEEK 4 — WATER SAFETY

**You will learn to**

• say how to be safe near water

• name dangers near water

• swim with a grown-up

**Things to know**

• Water can be dangerous if we are not careful.

• We do not go near deep water alone.

• We swim only with a **grown-up** near.

• We do not push anyone into water.

• We wear a **life jacket** in a boat.

• We do not run near a pool.

• We learn to swim to be safe.

• We tell a grown-up if someone is in trouble.

• We do not play rough in water.

• Water safety keeps us from harm.

**Let us talk**

3. What do we wear in a boat?

**Words for my notebook**

• water safety — being safe in and near water

• life jacket — a jacket that helps us float

• deep water — water that is over our head

### WEEK 5 — SIMPLE FIRST AID

**You will learn to**

• say what first aid is

• give simple first aid

• call for help

**Things to know**

• **First aid** is the first help we give to someone hurt.

• If someone has a small cut, we wash it and cover it.

• We use a clean **bandage** or plaster.

• If someone falls, we help them up gently.

• We do not move someone who is badly hurt.

• We call a **grown-up** or a doctor for help.

• We keep a first aid box at home and school.

• We stay calm and help.

• First aid can save a life.

• We learn first aid to help others.

**Let us talk**

1. What is first aid?

2. What do we do for a small cut?

3. When do we call for help?

**Words for my notebook**

• first aid — the first help we give to someone hurt

• bandage — a cloth we tie over a wound

• wound — a cut or injury

**Worksheet**

1. The first help we give to someone hurt is ___ aid.
   A) first B) last C) no D) slow

2. For a small cut we wash it and ___ it.
   A) cover B) hide C) burn D) cut

3. We use a clean ___ or plaster.
   A) bandage B) stone C) leaf D) stick

4. We call a ___ or a doctor for help.
   A) grown-up B) stranger C) thief D) baby

5. Fill in: A cut or injury is a _____.

6. Mention two things we do in first aid.

7. Write what first aid is.

**My own work**

• Draw a first aid box.

• say no to harmful things

• name things that harm us

• choose what is good

• We do not touch sharp or dangerous things.

• We do not go with strangers.

• We do not take anything from people we do not know.
