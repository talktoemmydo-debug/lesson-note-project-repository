# Basic Digital Literacy — Primary 1 · Third Term

These are the notes for the third term of Primary 1. They follow the NERDC
Basic Education Curriculum (2025 revision) and the school's scheme of work.
The eight weeks below are the weeks that carry a written note; the remaining
weeks of the term are for revision and the end-of-term paper, so no note is
written for them.

*Depth: NERDC Primary 1 · Basic Digital Literacy · Third Term W1-6, 8-9 (storing our work; the internet; sending a message; learning software; computers in daily life; people who work with computers; being safe online; caring for our devices).*

---

### WEEK 1 — STORING OUR WORK

**You will learn to**

• say why we save our work

• name where we save work

• open saved work

**Things to know**

• We **save** our work so we do not lose it.

• Saved work is kept in a **file**.

• Files are kept in a **folder**.

• A folder is like a bag that holds files.

• We give a file a **name** so we can find it.

• We save our work on the computer or a small disk.

• We open a saved file to use it again.

• Saving keeps our work safe.

• We save before we close.

• Good workers save their work.

**Let us talk**

1. Why do we save our work?

2. What is a folder?

3. How do we find a saved file?

**Words for my notebook**

• save — to keep our work safe

• file — our saved work

• folder — a bag that holds files

**Worksheet**

1. We save our work so we do not ___ it.
   A) lose B) find C) read D) draw

2. Saved work is kept in a ___.
   A) file B) song C) stone D) shoe

3. Files are kept in a ___.
   A) folder B) river C) road D) roof

4. We give a file a ___ so we can find it.
   A) name B) colour only C) smell D) sound

5. Fill in: A folder is like a _____ that holds files.

6. Mention two reasons we save our work.

7. Write what a file is.

**My own work**

• Draw a folder with files inside.

### WEEK 2 — THE INTERNET

**You will learn to**

• say what the internet is

• name what we use it for

• use it when a grown-up is near

**Things to know**

• The **internet** joins computers all over the world.

• We use the internet to find **information**.

• We can learn and read on the internet.

• We can see pictures and watch lessons.

• A **grown-up** stays near when we use the internet.

• We do not talk to strangers online.

• We do not give our name or address online.

• The internet has good and bad things.

• We call a grown-up for help online.

• The internet is a useful tool.

**Let us talk**

1. What is the internet?

2. Name one thing we use the internet for.

3. Why do we need a grown-up near when we use it?

**Words for my notebook**

• internet — a network that joins computers worldwide

• information — facts we find out

• online — on the internet

**Worksheet**

   A) computers B) stones C) trees D) cars

2. We use the internet to find ___.
   A) information B) dust C) noise D) sleep

3. We use the internet with a ___ near.
   A) grown-up B) stranger C) pet D) baby

4. We do not talk to ___ online.
   A) strangers B) teachers C) parents D) friends

5. Fill in: On the internet is _____.

6. Mention two things we use the internet for.

7. Write what the internet is.

### WEEK 3 — SENDING A MESSAGE

**You will learn to**

• say what a message is

• name ways we send messages

• send a simple message

**Things to know**

• A **message** is information we send to someone.

• We can send a message by **phone**.

• We can send a message by **email**.

• An email is a message sent on the internet.

• We type a message and send it.

• The person gets the message fast.

• We write a message clearly.

• We send messages to people we know.

• We do not send messages to strangers.

• Sending messages helps us to share news.

**Let us talk**

1. What is a message?

2. Name one way to send a message.

3. What is an email?

**Words for my notebook**

• message — information we send to someone

• email — a message sent on the internet

• send — to make a message go to someone

**Worksheet**

1. A message is information we ___ to someone.
   A) send B) hide C) lose D) break

3. An email is a message sent on the ___.
   A) internet B) road C) river D) roof

4. We write a message ___.
   A) clearly B) in secret C) badly D) slowly only

5. Fill in: To make a message go to someone is to _____.

6. Mention two ways we send messages.

7. Write what an email is.

### WEEK 8 — BEING SAFE ONLINE

**You will learn to**

• say how to be safe online

• name dangers online

• tell a grown-up

**Things to know**

• We go online only when a grown-up is near.

• We do not chat with people we do not know.

• We do not give our **name**, **age** or **address**.

• We do not share our family's information.

• We do not open messages from people we do not know.

• We tell a grown-up if something looks wrong.

• We are kind and do not send bad words.

• We do not believe everything online.

• We use the internet for good things.

• Being safe online keeps us from harm.

**Let us talk**

1. Why do we need a grown-up near online?

2. What must we not share online?

3. What do we do if something looks wrong online?

**Words for my notebook**

• safe online — using the internet without harm

• stranger — a person we do not know

• share — to give information

**Worksheet**

1. We use the internet with a ___ near.
   A) grown-up B) stranger C) pet D) baby

2. We do not give our name, age or ___.
   A) address B) favourite colour C) song D) game

3. We do not talk to ___ online.
   A) strangers B) teachers C) parents D) friends

4. We tell a ___ if something looks wrong online.
   A) grown-up B) stranger C) thief D) nobody

5. Fill in: A person we do not know is a _____.

6. Mention two ways to be safe online.

7. Write what we do if something looks wrong online.

### WEEK 9 — CARING FOR OUR DEVICES

**You will learn to**

• say how to care for devices

• name good habits

• keep devices clean

**Things to know**

• We keep our devices **clean and dry**.

• We do not eat or drink near a device.

• We press keys and buttons **gently**.

• We do not drop or knock a device.

• We keep devices away from water and heat.

• We put a device away when we finish.

• We do not pull the wires.

• We charge a device with care.

• A well-kept device lasts long.

• Caring for devices saves money.

**Let us talk**

1. How do we keep a device clean?

2. Why do we not eat near a device?

3. What do we do when we finish using a device?

**Words for my notebook**

• device — a machine like a computer or phone

• gently — softly, not hard

• care for — to look after well

**Worksheet**

1. We keep our devices clean and ___.
   A) dry B) wet C) dirty D) oily

2. We do not ___ or drink near a device.
   A) eat B) sing C) read D) sleep

3. We press keys and buttons ___.
   A) gently B) hard C) fast only D) with a stick

4. We put a device ___ when we finish.
   A) away B) in water C) on fire D) outside

5. Fill in: A machine like a computer or phone is a _____.

6. Mention two ways to care for a device.

7. Write why we care for our devices.

**My own work**

• Draw myself keeping a device clean.
