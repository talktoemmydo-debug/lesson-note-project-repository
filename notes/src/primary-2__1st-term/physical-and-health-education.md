# Physical and Health Education — Primary 2 · First Term

These are the notes for the first term of Primary 2. They follow the NERDC
Basic Education Curriculum (2025 revision) and the school's scheme of work.
The eight weeks below are the weeks that carry a written note; the remaining
weeks of the term are for revision and the end-of-term paper, so no note is
written for them.

*Depth: NERDC Primary 2 · Physical and Health Education · First Term W1-6, 8-9 (the body and movement; locomotor movements; non-locomotor movements; games and sports; personal hygiene; food and nutrition; safety and simple first aid; rest and sleep).*

---

### WEEK 1 — THE BODY AND MOVEMENT

**You will learn to**

• name parts of the body used in movement

• move the body well

• care for the body

**Things to know**

• Our **body** moves in many ways.

• We move with our **legs**, **arms** and **body**.

• The **legs** help us stand, walk and run.

• The **arms** help us lift, throw and reach.

• The **head** and **trunk** help us balance.

• **Bones** and **muscles** help the body move.

• **Joints** are where two bones meet; they let us bend.

• Movement keeps the body **strong** and healthy.

• We warm up before we move hard.

• We care for the body by eating and resting well.

**Let us talk**

1. Name one part of the body used in movement.

2. What do joints do?

3. Why is movement good?

**Words for my notebook**

• movement — the act of moving the body

• joint — where two bones meet

• muscle — the part that pulls the bones to move

**Worksheet**

1. The part that helps us stand, walk and run is the ___.
   A) legs B) ears C) hair D) teeth

2. Where two bones meet is a ___.
   A) joint B) skin C) tooth D) nail

3. The part that pulls the bones to move is the ___.
   A) muscle B) hair C) nail D) ear

4. Movement keeps the body strong and ___.
   A) healthy B) weak C) sick D) sad

5. Fill in: The act of moving the body is _____.

6. Mention two parts of the body used in movement.

7. Write what a joint is.

**My own work**

• Draw yourself running.

### WEEK 2 — LOCOMOTOR MOVEMENTS

**You will learn to**

• name locomotor movements

• do locomotor movements

• move from place to place

**Things to know**

• **Locomotor movements** move us from one place to another.

• **Walking** is moving on the feet at a gentle pace.

• **Running** is moving on the feet fast.

• **Jumping** is pushing off the ground with the feet.

• **Hopping** is jumping on one foot.

• **Leaping** is a long jump.

• **Skipping** is a step with a hop.

• **Climbing** is going up.

• We use locomotor movements in games.

• Locomotor movements make us strong.

**Let us talk**

1. What is a locomotor movement?

2. Name one locomotor movement.

3. What is hopping?

**Words for my notebook**

• locomotor movement — a movement that takes us from place to place

• hop — to jump on one foot

• leap — a long jump

### WEEK 3 — NON-LOCOMOTOR MOVEMENTS

**You will learn to**

• name non-locomotor movements

• do non-locomotor movements

• move in one place

**Things to know**

• **Non-locomotor movements** are done in **one place**.

• We do not move from place to place.

• **Bending** is moving the body down or sideways.

• **Stretching** is reaching out the arms or legs.

• **Twisting** is turning the body.

• **Turning** is changing the way the body faces.

• **Swinging** is moving the arms to and fro.

• **Balancing** is holding the body steady.

• Non-locomotor movements make us flexible.

• We use them in exercises and games.

**Let us talk**

1. What is a non-locomotor movement?

2. Name one non-locomotor movement.

3. What is balancing?

**Words for my notebook**

• non-locomotor movement — a movement done in one place

• stretch — to reach out the arms or legs

• balance — to hold the body steady

**Worksheet**

1. A movement done in one place is a ___ movement.

### WEEK 5 — PERSONAL HYGIENE

**You will learn to**

• keep the body clean

• name ways to be clean

• prevent sickness

**Things to know**

• **Hygiene** means keeping clean to stay well.

• We **bathe** every day with soap and water.

• We **brush** our teeth every morning and night.

• We **wash** our hands before eating and after the toilet.

• We keep our **hair** neat and combed.

• We keep our **nails** short and clean.

• We wear **clean** clothes.

• Germs cause sickness; cleanliness keeps germs away.

• We cover our mouth when we cough.

• A clean body is a healthy body.

**Let us talk**

1. What is hygiene?

2. Name one way to keep clean.

3. Why do we wash our hands?

**Words for my notebook**

• hygiene — keeping clean to stay well

• germ — a tiny thing that can cause sickness

• clean — free from dirt

### WEEK 6 — FOOD AND NUTRITION

**You will learn to**

• name food groups

• eat a balanced diet

• know why food matters

**Things to know**

• **Food** gives us energy and helps us grow.

• A **balanced diet** has all the food groups.

• The food groups are **carbohydrates**, **proteins**, **vitamins** and **minerals**.

• **Carbohydrates** give us energy — rice, yam, beans.

• **Proteins** build the body — eggs, fish, meat.

• **Vitamins** and **minerals** protect us — fruits and vegetables.

• We drink **water** every day.

• We eat **three meals** a day.

• Good food keeps us strong and healthy.

• We do not eat too many sweets.

**Let us talk**

1. What is a balanced diet?

2. Name one food that gives energy.

3. Why is food important?

**Words for my notebook**

• food — what we eat to live and grow

• balanced diet — a meal with all the food groups

• protein — the food that builds the body

**Worksheet**

4. Vitamins and minerals ___ us.
   A) protect B) hurt C) break D) burn

### WEEK 8 — SAFETY AND SIMPLE FIRST AID

**You will learn to**

• keep safe from harm

• give simple first aid

• call for help

**Things to know**

• **Safety** means being free from harm.

• We do not play with **sharp** or **hot** things.

• We do not run on a **wet** floor.

• We cross the road with care.

• **First aid** is the first help we give to someone hurt.

• For a small cut, we wash it and **cover** it.

• We use a clean **bandage** or plaster.

• We do not move someone who is badly hurt.

• We call a **grown-up** or a doctor for help.

• Safety and first aid keep us well.

**Let us talk**

1. What is safety?

2. What is first aid?

3. When do we call for help?

**Words for my notebook**

• safety — being free from harm

• first aid — the first help we give to someone hurt

• bandage — a cloth we tie over a wound

**Worksheet**

1. Being free from harm is ___.

### WEEK 9 — REST AND SLEEP

**You will learn to**

• say why we rest

• know how much to sleep

• rest well

**Things to know**

• **Rest** is taking it easy after work or play.

• **Sleep** is a deep rest at night.

• Rest helps the body to **recover**.

• Children need about **nine to ten** hours of sleep.

• We sleep at **night** and rest in the day.

• Good sleep keeps us **strong** and fresh.

• We sleep on a clean **bed**.

• We do not watch screens just before bed.

• Rest and sleep keep us healthy.

• A well-rested body learns well.

**Let us talk**

1. What is rest?

2. How much sleep do children need?

3. Why is sleep important?

**Words for my notebook**

• rest — taking it easy after work or play

• sleep — a deep rest at night

• recover — to get back strength

**Worksheet**

1. Taking it easy after work or play is ___.
   A) rest B) running C) shouting D) fighting

2. A deep rest at night is ___.
   A) sleep B) noise C) food D) play

3. Children need about ___ hours of sleep.
   A) nine to ten B) one to two C) twenty D) zero

4. Good sleep keeps us strong and ___.
   A) fresh B) weak C) sick D) sad

5. Fill in: To get back strength is to _____.

6. Mention two ways to rest well.

7. Write why sleep is important.
