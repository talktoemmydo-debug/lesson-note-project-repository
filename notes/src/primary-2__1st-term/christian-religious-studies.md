# Christian Religious Studies — Primary 2 · First Term

These are the notes for the first term of Primary 2. They follow the NERDC
Basic Education Curriculum (2025 revision) and the school's scheme of work.
The eight weeks below are the weeks that carry a written note; the remaining
weeks of the term are for revision and the end-of-term paper, so no note is
written for them.

*Depth: NERDC Primary 2 · Christian Religious Studies · First Term W1-6, 8-9 (God the Creator; God's love for us; the Bible, God's word; prayer; Adam and Eve; Noah's ark; Abraham, the father of faith; Joseph and God's care).*

---

### WEEK 1 — GOD THE CREATOR

**Main content**

• **God** created the heaven and the earth.

• God created the **sun**, **moon** and **stars**.

• God created the **land**, **sea** and **sky**.

• God created **plants** and **trees**.

• God created **animals** — birds, fish and beasts.

• God created **man** in His own image.

• God saw that all He made was **good**.

• God created the world in **six days** and rested on the seventh.

• We thank God for all He created.

• God is the great **Creator**.

**Let us talk**

1. Who created the world?

2. Name two things God created.

3. Why do we thank God?

**Words for my notebook**

• Creator — the one who makes things

• create — to make something new

• image — what something looks like

**Worksheet**

1. God created the heaven and the ___.
   A) earth B) nothing C) dark D) empty

2. God created man in His own ___.
   A) image B) house C) garden D) water

3. God saw that all He made was ___.
   A) good B) bad C) sad D) old

4. God created the world in ___ days.
   A) six B) two C) ten D) one

5. Fill in: The one who makes things is the _____.

6. Mention two things God created.

7. Write why we thank God.

**My own work**

• Draw two things God created.

### WEEK 2 — GOD'S LOVE FOR US

**Main content**

• **God loves** every one of us.

• God shows His love by **caring** for us.

• God gives us **food**, **family** and **life**.

• God sent **Jesus** because He loves us.

• We show God's love by **loving others**.

• We love our **family**, **friends** and **neighbours**.

• We help those in **need**.

• We are kind and **forgive** one another.

• God's love is **great** and never ends.

• We thank God for His love.

**Let us talk**

1. Who loves us?

2. How does God show His love?

3. How do we show God's love?

**Words for my notebook**

• love — to care deeply for someone

• forgive — to stop being angry with someone

• need — something we must have

**Worksheet**

1. God ___ every one of us.
   A) loves B) hates C) forgets D) scolds

2. God sent ___ because He loves us.
   A) Jesus B) a stone C) a tree D) a river

3. We show God's love by ___ others.
   A) loving B) hitting C) ignoring D) teasing

4. We help those in ___.
   A) need B) anger C) sleep D) noise

5. Fill in: To stop being angry with someone is to _____.

6. Mention two ways God shows His love.

7. Write how we show God's love.

**My own work**

• Draw a heart and write "God loves me".

### WEEK 3 — THE BIBLE, GOD'S WORD

**Main content**

• The **Bible** is the holy book of Christians.

• The Bible is **God's word**.

• The Bible has two parts: the **Old Testament** and the **New Testament**.

• The Bible tells us about **God** and **Jesus**.

• The Bible teaches us how to **live well**.

• We read the Bible at home and in church.

• We handle the Bible with **respect**.

• We do not tear or spoil the Bible.

• We listen when God's word is read.

• The Bible is a guide for our life.

**Let us talk**

1. What is the Bible?

2. Name the two parts of the Bible.

3. How do we handle the Bible?

**Words for my notebook**

• Bible — the holy book of Christians

• testament — a part of the Bible

• holy — belonging to God

3. The Bible has the Old Testament and the ___ Testament.

4. We handle the Bible with ___.
   A) respect B) anger C) force D) noise

5. Fill in: Something belonging to God is _____.

6. Mention two things the Bible tells us.

7. Write how we handle the Bible.

### WEEK 4 — PRAYER

**Main content**

• **Prayer** is talking to God.

• We pray to **thank** God, **ask** God and **praise** God.

• We can pray **anywhere** and **anytime**.

• We pray at **home**, **school** and **church**.

• We pray in the **morning** and at **night**.

• We pray **before meals**.

• We fold our hands and close our eyes to pray.

• God **hears** our prayers.

• We pray with a **sincere** heart.

• Prayer brings us close to God.

**Let us talk**

1. What is prayer?

2. Why do we pray?

3. When do we pray?

**Words for my notebook**

• prayer — talking to God

• praise — to say how great God is

• sincere — honest and true

**Worksheet**

1. Talking to God is ___.
   A) prayer B) singing C) sleeping D) eating

2. We pray to thank God, ask God and ___ God.
   A) praise B) scold C) forget D) tease

3. God ___ our prayers.
   A) hears B) ignores C) breaks D) hides

4. We pray with a ___ heart.
   A) sincere B) angry C) lazy D) noisy

5. Fill in: To say how great God is to _____.

6. Mention two times we pray.

7. Write what prayer is.

**My own work**

• Draw yourself praying.

### WEEK 5 — ADAM AND EVE

**Main content**

• **Adam** was the first man God created.

• **Eve** was the first woman God created.

• God put them in a beautiful garden called **Eden**.

• God told them they could eat from every tree **except one**.

• The **serpent** tempted Eve to eat the forbidden fruit.

• Eve ate and gave some to **Adam**, and he ate too.

• They **disobeyed** God.

• Because of their sin, they were sent **out** of the garden.

• We learn to **obey** God.

• Disobedience brings trouble.

**Let us talk**

1. Who were Adam and Eve?

2. What did they do wrong?

3. What do we learn from them?

**Words for my notebook**

• obey — to do what we are told

• disobey — to not do what we are told

• tempt — to make someone want to do wrong

**Worksheet**

1. The first man God created was ___.
   A) Adam B) Noah C) Moses D) David

2. The first woman God created was ___.
   A) Eve B) Mary C) Ruth D) Sarah

3. God put them in the garden of ___.
   A) Eden B) Egypt C) Sinai D) Bethel

4. They ___ God by eating the forbidden fruit.
   A) disobeyed B) obeyed C) praised D) thanked

5. Fill in: To not do what we are told is to _____.

6. Mention two things God created for Adam and Eve.

7. Write what we learn from Adam and Eve.

**My own work**

• Draw the garden of Eden.

### WEEK 6 — NOAH'S ARK

**Main content**

• **Noah** was a good man who obeyed God.

• The people on earth had become **wicked**.

• God was **sad** and decided to send a flood.

• God told Noah to build a big **ark** (a large boat).

• Noah obeyed and built the ark.

• God told Noah to take his **family** and **two of every animal** into the ark.

• **Rain** fell for forty days and forty nights.

• The flood covered the earth, but Noah and his family were **safe** in the ark.

• God saved Noah because he **obeyed**.

• We learn to obey God like Noah.

**Let us talk**

1. Who was Noah?

2. What did God tell Noah to build?

3. Why did God save Noah?

**Words for my notebook**

• ark — the large boat Noah built

• flood — a great amount of water covering the land

• wicked — doing bad things

**Worksheet**

1. The good man who built the ark was ___.
   A) Noah B) Adam C) Moses D) David

2. The ark was a large ___.
   A) boat B) house C) tree D) stone

3. Rain fell for ___ days and nights.
   A) forty B) ten C) two D) one

4. God saved Noah because he ___.
   A) obeyed B) disobeyed C) fought D) slept

5. Fill in: A great amount of water covering the land is a _____.

6. Mention two things Noah took into the ark.

7. Write why God saved Noah.

**My own work**

• Draw Noah's ark.

### WEEK 8 — ABRAHAM, THE FATHER OF FAITH

**Main content**

• **Abraham** is called the father of **faith**.

• God told Abraham to leave his home and go to a new land.

• Abraham **obeyed** God and went.

• God promised Abraham that he would have many **descendants**.

• Abraham **trusted** God, even when it was hard.

• **Faith** means trusting God and believing His promises.

• Abraham is the father of many nations.

• God blessed Abraham because of his faith.

• We learn to trust God like Abraham.

• Faith pleases God.

**Let us talk**

1. Who is called the father of faith?

2. What is faith?

3. What do we learn from Abraham?

**Words for my notebook**

• faith — trusting God and believing His promises

• promise — something God says He will do

• descendants — the children and family that come after

**Worksheet**

1. The father of faith is ___.
   A) Abraham B) Noah C) Moses D) David

2. God told Abraham to go to a new ___.
   A) land B) sea C) sky D) fire

3. Faith means ___ God.
   A) trusting B) forgetting C) scolding D) teasing

4. God blessed Abraham because of his ___.
   A) faith B) anger C) fear D) sleep

5. Fill in: Something God says He will do is a _____.

6. Mention two things Abraham did.

7. Write what faith is.

**My own work**

• Draw Abraham walking with trust.

### WEEK 9 — JOSEPH AND GOD'S CARE

**Main content**

• **Joseph** was a young man who loved God.

• His brothers were **jealous** and sold him.

• Joseph was taken to **Egypt** as a slave.

• Even in trouble, God was **with** Joseph.

• Joseph did **good** and God helped him.

• God made Joseph a **ruler** in Egypt.

• Joseph **forgave** his brothers when they needed help.

• God used Joseph to save many people from hunger.

• God cares for us even in hard times.

• We trust God's care like Joseph.

**Let us talk**

1. Who was Joseph?

2. How did God care for Joseph?

3. What do we learn from Joseph?

**Words for my notebook**

• care — to look after someone well

• slave — a person made to work for others

• jealous — wanting what another has

**Worksheet**

1. The young man who loved God and was sold was ___.
   A) Joseph B) Adam C) Noah D) Moses

2. Joseph was taken to ___ as a slave.
   A) Egypt B) Eden C) Sinai D) Bethel

3. Even in trouble, God was ___ Joseph.
   A) with B) against C) far from D) angry with

4. Joseph ___ his brothers when they needed help.
   A) forgave B) fought C) forgot D) scolded

5. Fill in: To look after someone well is to _____.

6. Mention two things that happened to Joseph.

7. Write how God cared for Joseph.

**My own work**

• Draw Joseph in Egypt.
