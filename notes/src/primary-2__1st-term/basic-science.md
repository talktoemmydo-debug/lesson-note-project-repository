# Basic Science — Primary 2 · First Term

These are the notes for the first term of Primary 2. They follow the NERDC
Basic Education Curriculum (2025 revision) and the school's scheme of work.
The eight weeks below are the weeks that carry a written note; the remaining
weeks of the term are for revision and the end-of-term paper, so no note is
written for them.

*Depth: NERDC Primary 2 · Basic Science · First Term W1-6, 8-9 (living and non-living things; plants around us; animals around us; the human body; our senses; health and cleanliness; air and water; weather and seasons).*

---

### WEEK 1 — LIVING AND NON-LIVING THINGS

**Main content**

• **Living things** are things that have life.

• **Non-living things** are things that do not have life.

• Living things **breathe**, **grow**, **move**, **feed** and **reproduce**.

• Living things **die** when their life ends.

• Examples of living things: **man**, **animals**, **plants**.

• Non-living things do **not** breathe, grow or feed.

• Examples of non-living things: **stone**, **table**, **water**, **chair**.

• A plant is a living thing; a stone is a non-living thing.

• Living things need **air**, **water** and **food**.

• We can tell living things by what they do.

**Let us talk**

1. What is a living thing?

2. Name one living thing.

3. Name one non-living thing.

**Words for my notebook**

• living thing — a thing that has life

• non-living thing — a thing that does not have life

• breathe — to take in air

**Worksheet**

1. A thing that has life is a ___ thing.
   A) living B) non-living C) dead D) stone

2. Living things ___ and grow.
   A) breathe B) break C) burn D) melt

3. Which of these is a living thing?
   A) a goat B) a stone C) a table D) a chair

4. Which of these is a non-living thing?
   A) water B) a bird C) a tree D) a boy

5. Fill in: A thing that does not have life is a _____.

6. Mention two living things.

7. Write one thing living things do.

**My own work**

• Draw one living thing and one non-living thing.

### WEEK 2 — PLANTS AROUND US

**Main content**

• **Plants** are living things.

• The parts of a plant are **root**, **stem**, **leaf**, **flower** and **fruit**.

• The **root** holds the plant in the ground and takes in water.

• The **stem** carries water to the leaves.

• The **leaf** makes food for the plant.

• The **flower** turns into fruit.

• Some plants are **trees** (big), some are **herbs** (small).

• Plants give us **food**, **shade** and **air**.

• We care for plants by watering them.

• We do not pluck leaves for no reason.

**Let us talk**

1. Name the parts of a plant.

2. What does the root do?

3. How do we care for plants?

**Words for my notebook**

• plant — a living thing that grows in the ground

• root — the part that holds the plant and takes in water

• leaf — the part that makes food

**Worksheet**

1. The part that holds a plant in the ground is the ___.
   A) root B) flower C) fruit D) leaf

2. The part that makes food for the plant is the ___.
   A) leaf B) root C) stem D) fruit

3. The ___ turns into fruit.
   A) flower B) root C) stem D) leaf

4. Plants give us food, shade and ___.
   A) air B) stone C) fire D) smoke

5. Fill in: A big plant is called a _____.

6. Mention two parts of a plant.

7. Write how we care for plants.

**My own work**

• Draw a plant and label its parts.

### WEEK 3 — ANIMALS AROUND US

**Main content**

• **Animals** are living things.

• Some animals live at **home** (domestic): dog, cat, goat, cow.

• Some animals live in the **bush** (wild): lion, elephant, tiger.

• Some animals live in **water**: fish, crab.

• Some animals **fly**: bird, bat.

• Animals **move**, **feed**, **breathe** and **grow**.

• Animals give us **food**, **milk** and **help**.

• We care for animals and do not hurt them.

• Animals have different **sizes** and **shapes**.

• We can name many animals.

**Let us talk**

1. Name one animal that lives at home.

**Words for my notebook**

• animal — a living thing that moves and feeds

• domestic — living at home

• wild — living in the bush

5. Fill in: An animal that lives in the bush is a _____.

### WEEK 4 — THE HUMAN BODY

**Main content**

• The **human body** has many parts.

• The **head** has the eyes, nose, mouth and ears.

• The **hands** hold and work.

• The **legs** stand and walk.

• The **eyes** see; the **ears** hear.

• The **nose** smells; the **tongue** tastes.

• The **skin** feels.

• The **heart** pumps blood; the **lungs** breathe.

• We care for the body by bathing and eating well.

• A healthy body helps us work and play.

**Let us talk**

1. Name one part of the body.

2. What do the eyes do?

**Words for my notebook**

### WEEK 5 — OUR SENSES

**Main content**

• We have **five** senses.

• **Sight** — we see with the **eyes**.

• **Hearing** — we hear with the **ears**.

• **Smell** — we smell with the **nose**.

• **Taste** — we taste with the **tongue**.

• **Touch** — we feel with the **skin**.

• Each sense has a **sense organ**.

• The senses help us know the world.

• We care for our sense organs.

• We do not put sharp things in the ears or nose.

• Good senses help us learn.

**Let us talk**

1. How many senses do we have?

2. What do we see with?

3. How do we care for our senses?

**Words for my notebook**

• sense — a way the body knows the world

• sight — the sense of seeing

• touch — the sense of feeling

**Worksheet**

4. We feel with the ___.

5. Fill in: The sense of hearing is done with the _____.

6. Mention the five senses.

### WEEK 6 — HEALTH AND CLEANLINESS

**Main content**

• **Health** means the body is well and not sick.

• We keep the body **clean** by bathing every day.

• We **brush** our teeth every morning and night.

• We **wash** our hands before eating and after the toilet.

• We wear **clean** clothes.

• We eat **good food** to be strong.

• We **rest** and sleep well.

• Germs cause sickness; cleanliness keeps germs away.

• We cover our mouth when we cough.

• A clean body is a healthy body.

**Let us talk**

1. What is health?

2. Name one way to keep clean.

3. Why do we wash our hands?

**Words for my notebook**

• health — the body being well and not sick

### WEEK 8 — AIR AND WATER

**Main content**

• **Air** is all around us.

• We **breathe** air to live.

• Living things need air.

• Air can **move** things (wind).

• **Water** is a liquid we drink.

• We use water to **bathe**, **cook** and **wash**.

• Plants and animals need water.

• We keep water **clean**.

• We do not waste water.

• Air and water are important for life.

**Let us talk**

3. Why do we keep water clean?

### WEEK 9 — WEATHER AND SEASONS

**Main content**

• **Weather** is how the air is at a time.

• The weather can be **sunny**, **rainy**, **windy** or **cloudy**.

• When it is **sunny**, the sun shines.

• When it is **rainy**, water falls from the sky.

• When it is **windy**, the air moves fast.

• When it is **cloudy**, clouds cover the sky.

• Nigeria has two main seasons: the **dry season** and the **rainy season**.

• In the dry season, the sun is hot and there is little rain.

• In the rainy season, there is much rain.

• We dress for the weather.

**Let us talk**

1. What is weather?

2. Name one type of weather.

3. Name the two seasons in Nigeria.

**Words for my notebook**

• weather — how the air is at a time

• season — a time of the year with its own weather

• rainy season — the time when much rain falls

**Worksheet**

1. How the air is at a time is the ___.
   A) weather B) season C) water D) food

2. When water falls from the sky, it is ___.
   A) rainy B) sunny C) windy D) cloudy

3. When the sun shines, it is ___.
   A) sunny B) rainy C) windy D) cloudy

4. Nigeria has the dry season and the ___ season.
   A) rainy B) hot C) cold D) dark

5. Fill in: Moving air that is fast is _____.

6. Mention two types of weather.

7. Write the two seasons in Nigeria.

**My own work**

• Draw a sunny day and a rainy day.
