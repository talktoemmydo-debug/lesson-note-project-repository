# Basic Digital Literacy — Primary 2 · Third Term

These are the notes for the third term of Primary 2. They follow the NERDC
Basic Education Curriculum (2025 revision) and the school's scheme of work.
The eight weeks below are the weeks that carry a written note; the remaining
weeks of the term are for revision and the end-of-term paper, so no note is
written for them.

*Depth: NERDC Primary 2 · Basic Digital Literacy · Third Term W1-6, 8, 9 (phishing awareness; cyberbullying; inclusivity in digital content; basics of programming; introduction to Scratch; coding with Scratch; sequence in programming).*

---

### WEEK 1 — PHISHING AWARENESS

**You will learn to**

• say what phishing is

• spot a fake message

• tell a grown-up

**Things to know**

• **Phishing** is a trick to steal our information.

• A **phishing message** looks real but is fake.

• It may ask for a **password** or **money**.

• It may say "You have **won** a prize."

• It may ask us to **click** a strange link.

• We do **not** click strange links.

• We do **not** give our password to anyone.

• We do **not** send money to strangers.

• We tell a **grown-up** about strange messages.

• We keep our information **safe**.

**Let us talk**

1. What is phishing?

2. Name one thing a phishing message may ask for.

3. What do we do with a strange message?

**Words for my notebook**

• phishing — a trick to steal our information

• password — the secret words we use to sign in

• link — something we click to open a page

**Worksheet**

1. A trick to steal our information is ___.
   A) phishing B) fishing C) washing D) pushing

2. A phishing message may ask for a ___ or money.
   A) password B) pencil C) plate D) pillow

3. It may say "You have ___ a prize."
   A) won B) lost C) hidden D) broken

4. We do not click ___ links.
   A) strange B) safe C) good D) kind

5. Fill in: We tell a _____ about strange messages.

6. Mention two things a phishing message may ask for.

7. Write what phishing is.

**My own work**

• Draw and colour a message we should not click.

### WEEK 2 — CYBERBULLYING

**You will learn to**

• say what cyberbullying is

• know it is wrong

• get help

**Things to know**

• **Cyberbullying** is using a phone or computer to be unkind.

• It can be **mean words** in a message.

• It can be **nasty pictures** sent to hurt.

• It can be **leaving someone out** on purpose.

• Cyberbullying makes people feel **sad**.

• Cyberbullying is **wrong**.

• We do **not** send mean messages.

• We tell a **grown-up** if someone is unkind to us.

• We are **kind** to everyone online.

• Kind words make people feel good.

**Let us talk**

1. What is cyberbullying?

2. Why is cyberbullying wrong?

3. What do we do if someone is unkind online?

**Words for my notebook**

• cyberbullying — using a phone or computer to be unkind

• unkind — not kind

• message — words we send to someone

**Worksheet**

1. Using a phone or computer to be unkind is ___.
   A) cyberbullying B) kindness C) sharing D) helping

2. Cyberbullying makes people feel ___.
   A) sad B) happy C) glad D) proud

3. Cyberbullying is ___.
   A) wrong B) right C) good D) kind

4. We tell a ___ if someone is unkind to us.
   A) grown-up B) stranger C) thief D) baby

5. Fill in: Not kind is _____.

6. Mention two things that are cyberbullying.

7. Write what cyberbullying is.

**My own work**

• Draw and colour children being kind online.

### WEEK 4 — BASICS OF PROGRAMMING

**You will learn to**

• say what programming is

• give instructions

• follow steps

**Things to know**

• **Programming** is telling a computer what to do.

• We give the computer **instructions**.

• An **instruction** tells the computer one thing to do.

• Instructions must be in the **right order**.

• A list of instructions is called a **program**.

• We follow **steps** one by one.

• Step 1, then step 2, then step 3.

• If the order is wrong, the computer gets **confused**.

• We can give instructions to people too.

• Programming is fun.

**Let us talk**

1. What is programming?

2. What is an instruction?

3. Why must instructions be in the right order?

**Words for my notebook**

• programming — telling a computer what to do

• instruction — what tells something what to do

• program — a list of instructions

**Worksheet**

1. Telling a computer what to do is ___.
   A) programming B) playing C) sleeping D) eating

2. We give the computer ___.
   A) instructions B) stones C) trees D) clouds

3. A list of instructions is called a ___.
   A) program B) song C) story D) game

4. Instructions must be in the ___ order.
   A) right B) wrong C) mixed D) no

5. Fill in: What tells something what to do is an _____.

6. Mention two steps in order.

7. Write what programming is.

**My own work**

• Draw and colour the steps to brush your teeth in order.

### WEEK 5 — INTRODUCTION TO SCRATCH

**You will learn to**

• say what Scratch is

• name the parts of Scratch

• move a character

**Things to know**

• **Scratch** is a place where we make programs.

• In Scratch we use **blocks**.

• A **block** is a piece of an instruction.

• We **drag** blocks and **drop** them together.

• The **sprite** is the character we move.

• The **stage** is where the sprite moves.

• We use the **green flag** to start.

• We use the **red stop** to stop.

• We can make the sprite **move** and **talk**.

• Scratch makes programming easy.

**Let us talk**

1. What is Scratch?

2. What do we do with blocks?

3. What starts the program?

**Words for my notebook**

• Scratch — a place where we make programs

• block — a piece of an instruction

• sprite — the character we move

**Worksheet**

   A) green B) red C) black D) blue

5. Fill in: Where the sprite moves is the _____.

### WEEK 6 — CODING WITH SCRATCH (PART 1)

**You will learn to**

• put blocks together

• make the sprite move

• make the sprite talk

**Things to know**

• We **drag** blocks to the work area.

• We **snap** blocks together.

• The "**move 10 steps**" block makes the sprite move.

• The "**say hello**" block makes the sprite talk.

• We can change the **number** in a block.

• "Move **20** steps" moves the sprite further.

• We **click** the green flag to see it work.

• If it is wrong, we **change** the blocks.

• We **try again** until it works.

• Making programs is fun.

**Let us talk**

1. How do we join blocks?

2. What does "move 10 steps" do?

3. What do we do if it is wrong?

**Words for my notebook**

• drag — to pull something across

• snap — to join blocks together

• steps — how far the sprite moves

### WEEK 9 — SEQUENCE IN PROGRAMMING

**You will learn to**

• say what sequence is

• put steps in order

• fix the wrong order

**Things to know**

• **Sequence** means the order of steps.

• Steps must be in the **right order**.

• We put on our **shoes** after our **socks**.

• We **wash** our hands before we **eat**.

• In a program, the computer follows steps **one by one**.

• If the order is **wrong**, the program does not work.

• We can **move** a block to fix the order.

• Sequence matters in **everything** we do.

• We number our steps: 1, 2, 3.

• Good sequence makes programs work.

**Let us talk**

1. What is sequence?

2. What comes first: socks or shoes?

3. What do we do if the order is wrong?

**Words for my notebook**

• sequence — the order of steps

• order — how things are arranged

• fix — to make right

**Worksheet**

1. The order of steps is called ___.
   A) sequence B) silence C) science D) story

2. We put on our shoes after our ___.
   A) socks B) stones C) trees D) clouds

3. We wash our hands before we ___.
   A) eat B) sleep C) run D) sing

4. If the order is wrong, the program does not ___.
   A) work B) sleep C) sing D) dance

5. Fill in: To make right is to _____.

6. Mention two steps in the right order.

7. Write what sequence is.

**My own work**

• Draw and colour three steps in the right order.
