# General Knowledge — Primary 1 · Second Term

These are the notes for the second term of Primary 1. They follow the NERDC
Basic Education Curriculum (2025 revision) and the school's scheme of work.
The eight weeks below are the weeks that carry a written note; the remaining
weeks of the term are for revision and the end-of-term paper, so no note is
written for them.

*Depth: school scheme of work, Primary 1 · General Knowledge · Second Term W1-6, 8-9 (my friends; days and months; time and the clock; weather and seasons; plants and animals; food and nutrition; safety; our festivals).*

---

### WEEK 1 — MY FRIENDS

**You will learn to**

• say who a friend is

• name how to be a good friend

• play well with friends

**Things to know**

• A **friend** is someone I like and play with.

• I have friends at home, school and the neighbourhood.

• A good friend is **kind** and **honest**.

• A good friend **shares** and **helps**.

• We play together and take turns.

• We do not fight or quarrel with friends.

• We say sorry when we are wrong.

• We forgive our friends.

• Friends make us happy.

• We choose friends who do good.

**Let us talk**

1. Who is a friend?

2. Name one way to be a good friend.

3. How do we play with friends?

**Words for my notebook**

• friend — someone I like and play with

• kind — nice and caring

• share — to give part of what I have

**Worksheet**

1. A friend is someone I like and ___ with.
   A) play B) fight C) hide D) lose

2. A good friend is kind and ___.
   A) honest B) rude C) lazy D) angry

3. A good friend shares and ___.
   A) helps B) hits C) hides D) steals

4. We play together and take ___.
   A) turns B) fights C) toys D) blame

5. Fill in: We say _____ when we are wrong.

6. Mention two ways to be a good friend.

7. Write one thing I do with my friends.

**My own work**

• Draw me playing with a friend.

### WEEK 2 — DAYS AND MONTHS

**You will learn to**

• name the days of the week

• name the months of the year

• say today's day and month

**Things to know**

• There are **seven** days in a week.

• The days are Sunday, Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday.

• There are **twelve** months in a year.

• The months begin with **January** and end with **December**.

• Other months are February, March, April, May, June, July, August, September, October, November.

• We go to school on some days.

• We rest and play on others.

• A new month begins after the last day.

• We use a calendar to know the day and month.

• Knowing days and months helps us to plan.

**Let us talk**

1. How many days are in a week?

2. Name the first month of the year.

3. Which day is today?

**Words for my notebook**

• week — seven days

• month — one of the twelve parts of a year

• calendar — a chart of days and months

**Worksheet**

1. There are ___ days in a week.
   A) seven B) five C) ten D) three

2. There are ___ months in a year.
   A) twelve B) seven C) ten D) five

3. The first month of the year is ___.
   A) January B) December C) June D) March

4. The last month of the year is ___.
   A) December B) January C) April D) July

5. Fill in: A chart of days and months is a _____.

6. Mention two days of the week.

7. Write the month we are in now.

**My own work**

• Draw a calendar page and write today's day.

### WEEK 3 — TIME AND THE CLOCK

**You will learn to**

• say what a clock does

• tell the time on the hour

• name parts of the day

**Things to know**

• A **clock** tells us the time.

• The clock has a **short hand** and a **long hand**.

• The short hand tells the **hour**.

• The long hand tells the **minutes**.

• When the short hand is on 7, it is **7 o'clock**.

• The day has **morning**, **afternoon** and **evening**.

• We do things at different times.

• We go to school in the morning.

• We rest at night.

• Knowing time helps us not to be late.

**Let us talk**

1. What does a clock tell us?

2. What does the short hand tell?

3. Name the parts of the day.

**Words for my notebook**

• clock — a thing that tells time

• hour — the time the short hand shows

• o'clock — the time on the hour

**Worksheet**

1. A clock tells us the ___.
   A) time B) date C) weather D) name

2. The short hand of a clock tells the ___.
   A) hour B) minute C) day D) month

3. When the short hand is on 7, it is ___ o'clock.
   A) 7 B) 12 C) 6 D) 3

4. The day has morning, afternoon and ___.
   A) evening B) night only C) dawn D) dusk

5. Fill in: The long hand tells the _____.

6. Mention two parts of the day.

7. Write what a clock does.

**My own work**

• Draw a clock showing 4 o'clock.

### WEEK 4 — WEATHER AND SEASONS

**You will learn to**

• name the types of weather

• name the seasons

• dress for the weather

**Things to know**

• **Weather** is how the air is on a day.

• The weather can be **sunny**, **rainy**, **cloudy** or **windy**.

• On a sunny day the sun shines.

• On a rainy day water falls from the sky.

• Nigeria has two seasons: the **dry season** and the **rainy season**.

• The dry season is hot with little rain.

• The rainy season has much rain.

• We wear light clothes in the hot sun.

• We use an umbrella or raincoat in the rain.

• We plan our work for the weather.

**Let us talk**

1. Name one type of weather.

2. Name the two seasons in Nigeria.

3. What do we use on a rainy day?

**Words for my notebook**

• weather — how the air is on a day

• season — a long time of one kind of weather

• dry season — the hot time with little rain

**Worksheet**

1. How the air is on a day is the ___.
   A) weather B) season C) water D) soil

2. On a ___ day the sun shines.
   A) sunny B) rainy C) cloudy D) windy

3. Nigeria has the dry season and the ___ season.
   A) rainy B) cold C) snow D) dark

4. On a rainy day we use an ___.
   A) umbrella B) fan C) oven D) ink

5. Fill in: A long time of one kind of weather is a _____.

6. Mention two types of weather.

7. Write the two seasons in Nigeria.

**My own work**

• Draw a sunny day and a rainy day.

### WEEK 5 — PLANTS AND ANIMALS AROUND US

**You will learn to**

• name plants around us

• name animals around us

• care for plants and animals

**Things to know**

• **Plants** grow in the soil.

• We see trees, flowers, grass and crops.

• Plants give us food, shade and beauty.

• **Animals** are living things that move.

• We see dogs, cats, goats, hens and birds.

• Some animals live in water, like fish.

• Animals give us meat, eggs and milk.

• We water plants and pull out weeds.

• We feed animals and keep them clean.

• Plants and animals are living things.

**Let us talk**

1. Name one plant you see.

2. Name one animal you see.

3. How do we care for plants?

**Words for my notebook**

• plant — a living thing that grows in soil

• animal — a living thing that moves

• crop — a plant we grow for food

**Worksheet**

1. Plants grow in the ___.
   A) soil B) air C) water only D) fire

2. Plants give us food, shade and ___.
   A) beauty B) smoke C) noise D) stone

3. Animals are living things that ___.
   A) move B) fly only C) sleep D) burn

4. The ___ lives in water.
   A) fish B) goat C) hen D) dog

5. Fill in: A plant we grow for food is a _____.

6. Mention two plants you see.

7. Write one thing animals give us.

**My own work**

• Draw one plant and one animal.

### WEEK 6 — FOOD AND NUTRITION

**You will learn to**

• name the food we eat

• say why we eat

• eat a balanced meal

**Things to know**

• **Food** is what we eat to live and grow.

• We eat **rice**, **yam**, **beans**, **garri** and **pap**.

• We eat **vegetables** and **fruits**.

• We eat **fish**, **meat** and **eggs**.

• Food gives us **energy** to play and work.

• Food helps us to grow strong.

• A **balanced meal** has all the food our body needs.

• We wash our hands before eating.

• We eat fresh, well-cooked food.

• We drink clean water every day.

**Let us talk**

1. Name one food you eat.

2. Why do we eat?

3. What is a balanced meal?

**Words for my notebook**

• food — what we eat to live

• energy — the strength food gives us

• balanced meal — a meal with all the food we need

**Worksheet**

1. We eat food to live and ___.
   A) grow B) sleep C) hide D) cry

2. Food gives us ___ to play and work.
   A) energy B) dust C) noise D) dark

3. Which is a fruit?
   A) mango B) stone C) sand D) wood

4. We wash our hands before ___.
   A) eating B) sleeping C) running D) crying

5. Fill in: A meal with all the food we need is a _____ meal.

6. Mention two foods you eat.

7. Write why we eat.

**My own work**

• Draw a plate of food with one fruit.

### WEEK 8 — SAFETY AT HOME AND ON THE ROAD

**You will learn to**

• say how to be safe at home

• say how to be safe on the road

• avoid danger

**Things to know**

• **Safety** means keeping away from harm.

• At home we do not play with **fire** or **sharp objects**.

• We do not touch **electric sockets**.

• We keep the floor dry so we do not slip.

• On the road we look **left and right** before crossing.

• We cross at the **zebra crossing** or with a grown-up.

• We obey the **traffic light**: red means stop, green means go.

• We do not play on the road.

• We tell a grown-up if we are hurt.

• Being safe keeps us from harm.

**Let us talk**

1. Name one thing we must not play with at home.

2. How do we cross the road safely?

3. What does the red traffic light mean?

**Words for my notebook**

• safety — keeping away from harm

• zebra crossing — the striped place to cross the road

• traffic light — the light that directs road users

**Worksheet**

1. Safety means keeping away from ___.
   A) harm B) food C) friends D) play

2. At home we do not play with fire or ___ objects.
   A) sharp B) soft C) round D) clean

3. Before crossing we look left and ___.
   A) right B) up only C) down D) back

4. The red traffic light means ___.
   A) stop B) go C) run D) sing

5. Fill in: We cross at the zebra _____.

6. Mention two ways to be safe on the road.

7. Write what the green traffic light means.

**My own work**

• Draw a traffic light and colour it.

### WEEK 9 — OUR FESTIVALS

**You will learn to**

• say what a festival is

• name festivals in Nigeria

• respect our culture

**Things to know**

• A **festival** is a special time of celebration.

• People come together to celebrate a festival.

• Nigeria has many festivals.

• The **Eid** festival is celebrated by Muslims.

• The **Christmas** festival is celebrated by Christians.

• Some towns have cultural festivals with dance and drum.

• We wear special clothes at a festival.

• We eat special food and share with others.

• Festivals show our culture.

• We respect other people's festivals.

**Let us talk**

1. What is a festival?

2. Name one festival in Nigeria.

3. What do we do at a festival?

**Words for my notebook**

• festival — a special time of celebration

• celebrate — to do something special and happy

• culture — the way of life of a people

**Worksheet**

1. A festival is a special time of ___.
   A) celebration B) sleep C) work D) crying

2. The ___ festival is celebrated by Muslims.
   A) Eid B) Easter C) New Yam D) Christmas

3. The ___ festival is celebrated by Christians.
   A) Christmas B) Eid C) Sallah D) New Yam

4. We wear special ___ at a festival.
   A) clothes B) shoes only C) bags D) books

5. Fill in: To do something special and happy is to _____.

6. Mention two festivals in Nigeria.

7. Write what we do at a festival.

**My own work**

• Draw people celebrating a festival.
