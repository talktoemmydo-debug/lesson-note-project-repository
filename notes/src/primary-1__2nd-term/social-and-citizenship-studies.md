# Social and Citizenship Studies — Primary 1 · Second Term

These are the notes for the second term of Primary 1. They follow the NERDC
Basic Education Curriculum (2025 revision) and the school's scheme of work.
The eight weeks below are the weeks that carry a written note; the remaining
weeks of the term are for revision and the end-of-term paper, so no note is
written for them.

*Depth: NERDC Primary 1 · Social and Citizenship Studies · Second Term W1-6, 8-9 (friends and neighbours; the extended family; community leaders; our country Nigeria; rights and duties of a child; honesty; obedience and respect; caring for public property).*

---

### WEEK 1 — FRIENDS AND NEIGHBOURS

**Main content**

• A **friend** is someone I like and play with.

• A **neighbour** is a person who lives near me.

• I have friends at home, school and the neighbourhood.

• Friends and neighbours help one another.

• We share and play together.

• We greet our neighbours.

• We do not fight or quarrel.

• We speak kindly to friends and neighbours.

• Good friends do good together.

• Friends and neighbours make life happy.

**Let us talk**

1. Who is a friend?

2. Who is a neighbour?

3. How do we treat our neighbours?

**Words for my notebook**

• friend — someone I like and play with

• neighbour — a person who lives near me

• share — to give part of what I have

**Worksheet**

1. A person who lives near me is my ___.
   A) neighbour B) stranger C) pet D) tool

2. A friend is someone I like and ___ with.
   A) play B) fight C) hide D) lose

3. Friends and neighbours ___ one another.
   A) help B) hit C) hate D) hide

4. We ___ our neighbours.
   A) greet B) chase C) fear D) forget

5. Fill in: To give part of what I have is to _____.

6. Mention two ways we treat friends well.

7. Write who a neighbour is.

**My own work**

• Draw me with a friend and a neighbour.

### WEEK 2 — THE EXTENDED FAMILY

**Main content**

• The **extended family** is the bigger family.

• It has parents, children, uncles, aunts and cousins.

• My father's brothers and sisters are my **uncles** and **aunts**.

• My uncle's children are my **cousins**.

• My **grandparents** are my parents' parents.

• The extended family helps one another.

• We visit our extended family.

• We greet and respect our elders.

• The extended family is part of who I am.

• A big family that loves is a strong family.

**Let us talk**

1. Who is in the extended family?

2. Who are my cousins?

3. How do we treat our elders?

**Words for my notebook**

• extended family — the bigger family with uncles, aunts and cousins

• uncle — a brother of my father or mother

• cousin — a child of my uncle or aunt

**Worksheet**

1. The bigger family with uncles and aunts is the ___ family.
   A) extended B) small C) school D) class

2. My uncle's children are my ___.
   A) cousins B) brothers C) parents D) neighbours

3. My grandparents are my parents' ___.
   A) parents B) children C) cousins D) friends

4. We greet and respect our ___.
   A) elders B) pets C) toys D) shoes

5. Fill in: A brother of my father is my _____.

6. Mention two members of the extended family.

7. Write who my cousins are.

**My own work**

• Draw my extended family together.

### WEEK 3 — COMMUNITY LEADERS

**Main content**

• A **leader** is a person who guides others.

• The **traditional ruler** leads the town.

• The **head teacher** leads the school.

• The **class captain** leads the class.

• A **religious leader** leads at the church or mosque.

• Leaders keep peace and order.

• Leaders settle quarrels.

• Leaders help the community to grow.

• We obey and respect our leaders.

• A good leader cares for the people.

**Let us talk**

1. Who is a leader?

2. Name one leader in your community.

3. What do leaders do?

**Words for my notebook**

• leader — a person who guides others

• traditional ruler — the leader of a town

• obey — to do what I am told

**Worksheet**

1. A person who guides others is a ___.
   A) leader B) stranger C) baby D) thief

2. The ___ leads the school.
   A) head teacher B) class captain C) doctor D) farmer

3. The ___ leads the town.
   A) traditional ruler B) pupil C) trader D) driver

4. Leaders keep peace and ___.
   A) order B) noise C) fight D) dust

5. Fill in: We obey and respect our _____.

6. Mention two leaders in the community.

7. Write what a leader does.

**My own work**

• Draw one leader in my community.

### WEEK 4 — OUR COUNTRY, NIGERIA

**Main content**

• A **country** is a land with its own people and government.

• My country is **Nigeria**.

• Nigeria is in **West Africa**.

• The capital of Nigeria is **Abuja**.

• Nigeria has many ethnic groups and languages.

• The Nigerian **flag** is green, white, green.

• The **naira** is our money.

• We sing the national **anthem**.

• Nigeria has many good people and places.

• I love my country, Nigeria.

**Let us talk**

1. What is my country?

2. What is the capital of Nigeria?

3. What colour is the Nigerian flag?

**Words for my notebook**

• country — a land with its own people and government

• Nigeria — my country, in West Africa

• capital — the chief city of a country

**Worksheet**

1. My country is ___.
   A) Nigeria B) Ghana C) Egypt D) Kenya

2. Nigeria is in ___ Africa.
   A) West B) East C) North D) South

3. The capital of Nigeria is ___.
   A) Abuja B) Lagos C) Kano D) Ibadan

4. The Nigerian flag is green, white and ___.
   A) green B) red C) blue D) black

5. Fill in: The money of Nigeria is the _____.

6. Mention two things about Nigeria.

7. Write the capital of Nigeria.

**My own work**

• Draw and colour the Nigerian flag.

### WEEK 5 — RIGHTS AND DUTIES OF A CHILD

**Main content**

• A **right** is something I ought to have.

• A child has the right to a **name**.

• A child has the right to **food**, **shelter** and **care**.

• A child has the right to **learn** and go to school.

• A **duty** is something I ought to do.

• My duty is to **obey** my parents and teachers.

• My duty is to **learn** well at school.

• My duty is to **help** at home.

• My duty is to be **honest** and kind.

• When I do my duty, I enjoy my rights.

**Let us talk**

1. What is a right?

2. Name one right of a child.

3. Name one duty of a child.

**Words for my notebook**

• right — something I ought to have

• duty — something I ought to do

• shelter — a place to live and be safe

**Worksheet**

1. Something I ought to have is a ___.
   A) right B) toy C) game D) song

2. Something I ought to do is a ___.
   A) duty B) gift C) prize D) treat

3. A child has the right to ___ and go to school.
   A) learn B) fight C) hide D) steal

4. My duty is to ___ my parents and teachers.
   A) obey B) ignore C) chase D) forget

5. Fill in: When I do my _____, I enjoy my rights.

6. Mention two rights of a child.

7. Write one duty of a child.

**My own work**

• Draw myself doing one duty at home.

### WEEK 6 — HONESTY

**Main content**

• **Honesty** means telling the truth.

• An honest person does not lie.

• An honest person does not steal.

• An honest person does not cheat.

• We tell the truth even when it is hard.

• We return what is not ours.

• We do not copy another's work.

• Honest people are trusted.

• Honesty makes a good name.

• God and people love an honest child.

**Let us talk**

**Words for my notebook**

7. Write what honesty means.

### WEEK 8 — OBEDIENCE AND RESPECT

**Main content**

• **Obedience** is doing what I am told.

• I obey my parents, teachers and leaders.

• **Respect** is treating others well.

• I respect my elders and my friends.

• I greet my elders with respect.

• I say please, sorry and thank you.

• I listen when a grown-up speaks.

• I do not talk back rudely.

• Obedience and respect keep peace.

• A child who obeys and respects is loved.

**Let us talk**

1. What is obedience?

2. What is respect?

3. How do we greet our elders?

**Words for my notebook**

• obedience — doing what I am told

• respect — treating others well

• elder — a person older than me

**Worksheet**

1. Doing what I am told is ___.
   A) obedience B) anger C) noise D) play

2. Treating others well is ___.
   A) respect B) fighting C) hiding D) losing

3. I obey my parents, teachers and ___.
   A) leaders B) thieves C) strangers D) nobody

4. I greet my elders with ___.
   A) respect B) noise C) anger D) haste

5. Fill in: A person older than me is an _____.

6. Mention two ways we show respect.

7. Write what obedience is.

**My own work**

• Draw myself greeting an elder with respect.

### WEEK 9 — CARING FOR PUBLIC PROPERTY

**Main content**

• **Public property** is a thing that belongs to everyone.

• The **school**, **road**, **bridge** and **park** are public property.

• The **street light** and **public tap** are public property.

• Public property is for all to use.

• We do not break or damage public property.

• We do not write on public walls.

• We keep public places clean.

• We report a broken public thing to a grown-up.

• Caring for public property saves money.

• Public property that is cared for lasts long.

**Let us talk**

1. What is public property?

2. Name one public property.

3. How do we care for public property?

**Words for my notebook**

• public property — a thing that belongs to everyone

• damage — to break or spoil

• care for — to look after well

**Worksheet**

1. A thing that belongs to everyone is ___ property.
   A) public B) private C) broken D) lost

2. The school, road and park are ___ property.
   A) public B) private C) my D) your

3. We do not ___ public property.
   A) damage B) clean C) use D) paint

4. We keep public places ___.
   A) clean B) dirty C) loud D) dark

5. Fill in: To break or spoil is to _____.

6. Mention two public properties.

7. Write how we care for public property.

**My own work**

• Draw one public property and keep it clean.
