# Prevocational Studies — Primary 1 · Second Term

These are the notes for the second term of Primary 1. They follow the NERDC
Basic Education Curriculum (2025 revision) and the school's scheme of work.
The eight weeks below are the weeks that carry a written note; the remaining
weeks of the term are for revision and the end-of-term paper, so no note is
written for them.

*Depth: NERDC Primary 1 · Pre-Vocational Studies · Second Term W1-6, 8-9 (planting a garden; caring for crops; animals and their young; preparing more foods; keeping the school clean; reusing waste; simple sewing; jobs in the community).*

---

### WEEK 1 — PLANTING A GARDEN

**You will learn to**

• say what a garden is

• plant a seed

• care for a young plant

**Things to know**

• A **garden** is a place where we grow plants.

• We can have a garden at home or at school.

• We dig the soil and make it soft.

• We put a **seed** in the soil.

• We cover the seed with soil.

• We water the seed every day.

• The seed grows into a **seedling**.

• A seedling is a young plant.

• We give the plant sunlight and water.

• Growing a garden gives us food and beauty.

**Let us talk**

1. What is a garden?

2. What do we put in the soil?

3. What does a seed grow into?

**Words for my notebook**

• garden — a place where we grow plants

• seed — what we plant to grow a new plant

• seedling — a young plant from a seed

**Worksheet**

1. A place where we grow plants is a ___.
   A) garden B) road C) roof D) desk

2. We put a ___ in the soil to grow a plant.
   A) seed B) stone C) nail D) cloth

3. A seed grows into a ___.
   A) seedling B) stone C) bird D) fish

4. We water the seed every ___.
   A) day B) year C) month only D) never

5. Fill in: A young plant from a seed is a _____.

6. Mention two things we do to plant a seed.

7. Write what a garden is.

**My own work**

• Draw a seed growing into a seedling.

### WEEK 2 — CARING FOR CROPS

**You will learn to**

• say how to care for crops

• weed and water

• protect crops

**Things to know**

• We **water** crops so they do not dry.

• We **weed** the farm, pulling out unwanted plants.

• A **weed** is a plant that grows where we do not want it.

• Weeds take food and water from the crop.

• We loosen the soil around the crop.

• We protect crops from animals and pests.

• We put manure on the soil to make it rich.

• We watch the crops as they grow.

• Good care makes crops grow well.

• We harvest crops when they are ripe.

**Let us talk**

1. What is a weed?

2. Why do we water crops?

3. How do we protect crops?

**Words for my notebook**

• weed — a plant that grows where we do not want it

• water — to give water to a plant

• harvest — to gather crops when ripe

**Worksheet**

1. A plant that grows where we do not want it is a ___.
   A) weed B) crop C) tree D) flower

2. We ___ crops so they do not dry.
   A) water B) hide C) burn D) cut

3. Weeds take food and ___ from the crop.
   A) water B) air only C) light D) sound

4. We harvest crops when they are ___.
   A) ripe B) green C) dry D) lost

5. Fill in: To pull out unwanted plants is to _____.

6. Mention two ways we care for crops.

7. Write what a weed is.

**My own work**

• Draw a person weeding a farm.

### WEEK 4 — PREPARING MORE FOODS

**You will learn to**

• prepare simple foods

• prepare a fruit plate

• keep food clean

**Things to know**

• We can prepare **fruits** like banana, orange and mango.

• We wash fruits before we eat them.

• We can peel and slice a fruit.

• We can make a **fruit plate** or salad.

• We can prepare a simple drink like water or juice.

• We wash our hands before touching food.

• We use clean plates and cups.

• We cover food to keep it safe.

• We eat fresh, well-prepared food.

• Preparing food is a useful skill.

**Let us talk**

**Words for my notebook**

• salad — a plate of mixed fruits or vegetables

### WEEK 8 — SIMPLE SEWING

**You will learn to**

• say what sewing is

• use a needle and thread

• mend a simple thing

**Things to know**

• **Sewing** is joining cloth with a needle and thread.

• We use a **needle** and **thread** to sew.

• We thread the needle.

• We make simple **stitches**.

• We can sew a button on a shirt.

• We can mend a small tear in a cloth.

• We can sew a simple bag.

• We use the needle with great care.

• We keep the needle away from little children.

• Sewing is a useful skill.

**Let us talk**

1. What is sewing?

2. What do we use to sew?

3. What can we mend?

**Words for my notebook**

• sewing — joining cloth with a needle and thread

• needle — a thin tool with an eye for thread

• stitch — one loop of thread in sewing

**Worksheet**

1. Sewing is joining cloth with a needle and ___.
   A) thread B) water C) glue D) stone

2. We use a ___ to sew.
   A) needle B) hammer C) spoon D) fork

3. We can sew a ___ on a shirt.
   A) button B) stone C) leaf D) nail

4. We use the needle with great ___.
   A) care B) haste C) anger D) noise

5. Fill in: One loop of thread in sewing is a _____.

6. Mention two things we can sew or mend.

7. Write what we use to sew.
