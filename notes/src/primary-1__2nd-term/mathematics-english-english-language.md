### WEEK 1 — LISTENING TO AND TELLING STORIES

**You will learn to**

• listen to a story with care

• tell a story in my own words

• say the parts of a story

**Things to know**

• A **story** tells us about people and events.

• We listen to a story with our ears and eyes.

• A story has a **beginning**, a **middle** and an **end**.

• We can tell a story in our own words.

• We say **who** is in the story.

• We say **what** happened.

• We say **where** it happened.

• Telling a story helps us to remember it.

• We speak clearly when we tell a story.

• Stories teach us and make us happy.

**Let us talk**

1. What are the parts of a story?

2. How do we listen to a story?

3. Tell a short story you know.

**Words for my notebook**

• story — a tale about people and events

• beginning — the first part of a story

• tell — to say a story in words

**Worksheet**

1. A story has a beginning, a middle and an ___.
   A) end B) start C) middle D) side

2. We listen to a story with our ___ and eyes.
   A) ears B) nose C) feet D) hands

3. We say who is in the story and what ___.
   A) happened B) slept C) broke D) hid

4. We tell a story in our own ___.
   A) words B) sleep C) tears D) silence

5. Fill in: The first part of a story is the _____.

6. Mention two parts of a story.

7. Write who is in a story you know.

**My own work**

• Draw one scene from a story I can tell.

### WEEK 2 — PHONICS PRACTICE: CONSONANT CLUSTERS AND WORD BUILDING

**You will learn to**

• read words with consonant clusters

• build words from sounds

• spell cluster words

**Things to know**

• A **consonant cluster** is two or three consonants together, each keeping its sound.

• **bl** as in **black**, **blue**.

• **st** as in **stop**, **step**.

• **tr** as in **tree**, **trip**.

• **sp** as in **spin**, **spot**.

• **gr** as in **green**, **grow**.

• We blend the sounds to read the word.

• bl-ack makes **black**. st-op makes **stop**.

• We build words by putting sounds together.

• Cluster words help us to read more.

**Let us talk**

1. What is a consonant cluster?

2. Name a word with st.

3. How do we build a word?

**Words for my notebook**

• consonant cluster — consonants together, each sound heard

• blend — to put sounds together to read

• build — to make a word from sounds

**Worksheet**

1. A consonant cluster is consonants said ___.
   A) together B) apart C) never D) slowly

2. bl is in ___.
   A) black B) ship C) chin D) this

3. st is in ___.
   A) stop B) chip C) that D) man

4. tr is in ___.
   A) tree B) shop C) fish D) pen

5. Fill in: gr is in _____.

6. Mention two words with consonant clusters.

7. Write two words with tr.

**My own work**

• Trace and write two words with st and two with bl.

### WEEK 4 — USING PLURALS OF NOUNS

**You will learn to**

• say what a plural is

• make the plural of a noun

• use plurals in a sentence

**Things to know**

• A **noun** is a naming word.

• The **plural** means more than one.

• We add **s** to make most plurals.

• One **cat**, two **cats**.

• One **book**, two **books**.

• One **dog**, two **dogs**.

• Some plurals change: one **child**, two **children**.

• One **foot**, two **feet**.

• The plural tells us there is more than one.

• We use plurals when we mean many.

**Let us talk**

1. What is a plural?

2. What is the plural of cat?

3. What is the plural of child?

**Words for my notebook**

• plural — more than one

• noun — a naming word

• children — the plural of child

**Worksheet**

1. The plural means ___ than one.
   A) more B) less C) no D) one

2. We add ___ to make most plurals.
   A) s B) a C) the D) ed

3. One cat, two ___.
   A) cats B) cat C) cates D) caties

4. One child, two ___.
   A) children B) childs C) childes D) child

5. Fill in: One foot, two _____.

6. Mention two plural words.

7. Write the plural of book.

**My own work**

• Draw two of a thing and write its plural.

### WEEK 8 — INTRODUCING ADVERBS

**You will learn to**

• say what an adverb is

• name adverbs

• use adverbs in a sentence

**Things to know**

• An **adverb** tells us more about an action.

• An adverb tells **how** we do something.

• She runs **quickly**. He walks **slowly**.

• The baby sleeps **softly**.

• **Quickly**, **slowly**, **loudly**, **softly** are adverbs.

• Many adverbs end in **-ly**.

• The adverb tells how the action is done.

• We use adverbs to make our talk clear.

• An adverb comes near the action word.

• Adverbs help us to describe actions.

**Let us talk**

1. What is an adverb?

2. Name one adverb.

3. Use an adverb in a sentence.

**Words for my notebook**

• adverb — a word that tells how an action is done

• quickly — at a fast speed

• slowly — at a slow speed

**Worksheet**

1. An adverb tells ___ we do something.
   A) how B) who C) where only D) when only

2. Which is an adverb?
   A) quickly B) boy C) run D) and

3. Many adverbs end in ___.
   A) -ly B) -ed C) -ing D) -s

4. She runs ___. Which word fits?
   A) quickly B) quick only C) boy D) table

5. Fill in: The baby sleeps _____. (softly/soft)

6. Mention two adverbs.

7. Write one adverb in a sentence.

**My own work**

• Draw someone doing an action and write how they do it.

### WEEK 9 — PHONICS: CONSONANT DIGRAPHS ch, sh, th, wh, ph

**You will learn to**

• know the digraphs ch, sh, th, wh, ph

• read words with these digraphs

• say the sound of each

**Things to know**

• A **digraph** is two letters that make one new sound.

• **ch** says /ch/ as in **chin**, **chip**, **church**.

• **sh** says /sh/ as in **ship**, **fish**, **shop**.

• **th** says /th/ as in **this**, **that**, **moth**.

• **wh** says /w/ as in **when**, **whale**, **wheel**.

• **ph** says /f/ as in **phone**, **photo**.

• The two letters work together as one sound.

• We blend the digraph with the rest of the word.

• sh-ip makes **ship**. ch-in makes **chin**.

• Digraphs help us to read more words.

**Let us talk**

1. What is a digraph?

2. Name a word with sh.

3. What sound does ph make?

**Words for my notebook**

• digraph — two letters making one new sound

• ch — /ch/ as in chin

• ph — /f/ as in phone

**Worksheet**

1. A digraph makes ___ new sound.
   A) one B) two C) three D) no

2. ch says /ch/ as in ___.
   A) chin B) ship C) this D) when

3. sh says /sh/ as in ___.
   A) ship B) chin C) that D) phone

4. ph says /f/ as in ___.
   A) phone B) ship C) this D) chin

5. Fill in: wh says /w/ as in _____.

6. Mention two words with digraphs.

7. Write two words with sh.

**My own work**

• Trace and write two words with ch and two with sh.
