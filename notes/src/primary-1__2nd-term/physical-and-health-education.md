# Physical and Health Education — Primary 1 · Second Term

These are the notes for the second term of Primary 1. They follow the NERDC
Basic Education Curriculum (2025 revision) and the school's scheme of work.
The eight weeks below are the weeks that carry a written note; the remaining
weeks of the term are for revision and the end-of-term paper, so no note is
written for them.

*Depth: NERDC Primary 1 · Physical and Health Education · Second Term W1-6, 8-9 (jumping; throwing and catching; ball games; gymnastics; rest and sleep; food and nutrition; safety in play; the value of exercise).*

---

### WEEK 1 — JUMPING

**Main content**

• **Jumping** is pushing the body off the ground.

• We can jump with **both feet** together.

• We can jump with **one foot**.

• The **long jump** is jumping as far as we can.

• The **high jump** is jumping as high as we can.

• We bend our knees before we jump.

• We swing our arms to help us jump.

• We land on the **balls** of our feet.

• We bend our knees to land softly.

• Jumping makes our legs strong.

**Let us talk**

1. What is jumping?

2. How do we land safely?

3. What is the long jump?

**Words for my notebook**

• jump — to push the body off the ground

• long jump — jumping as far as we can

• land — to come down after a jump

**Worksheet**

1. Jumping is pushing the body off the ___.
   A) ground B) water C) tree D) wall

2. We bend our ___ before we jump.
   A) knees B) ears C) eyes D) nose

3. We land on the ___ of our feet.
   A) balls B) heels C) sides D) toes only

4. Jumping as far as we can is the ___ jump.
   A) long B) high C) short D) slow

5. Fill in: We bend our knees to land _____.

6. Mention two kinds of jump.

7. Write how we land safely.

• Draw myself jumping.

### WEEK 2 — THROWING AND CATCHING

**Main content**

• **Throwing** is sending a ball away with the hand.

• **Catching** is holding a ball that comes to us.

• We throw with one hand or two hands.

• We throw to a friend or to a target.

• We watch the ball as it comes.

• We catch with both hands.

• We hold our hands ready to catch.

• We do not throw hard at a person's face.

• Throwing and catching need practice.

• Throwing and catching are used in many games.

**Let us talk**

1. What is throwing?

2. How do we catch a ball?

3. Why do we watch the ball?

**Words for my notebook**

• throw — to send a ball away with the hand

• catch — to hold a ball that comes to us

• target — the place we throw to

**Worksheet**

1. Throwing is sending a ball away with the ___.

   A) holding B) losing C) hiding D) breaking

3. We catch with ___ hands.
   A) both B) one only C) no D) dirty

4. We watch the ball as it ___.
   A) comes B) sleeps C) burns D) hides

5. Fill in: The place we throw to is the _____.

6. Mention two things we do with a ball.

7. Write how we catch a ball.

### WEEK 3 — BALL GAMES

**Main content**

• A **ball game** is a game played with a ball.

• **Football** is a game we play with the feet.

• We **kick** the ball with the foot.

• We **control** the ball so it stays near us.

• We pass the ball to a friend.

• Football is played by two teams.

• Each team tries to score a **goal**.

• We play by the rules.

• We play as a team and help one another.

• Ball games keep us fit and happy.

**Let us talk**

1. Name one ball game.

2. How do we kick a ball?

3. What is a team?

**Words for my notebook**

• ball game — a game played with a ball

• kick — to hit the ball with the foot

• team — a group that plays together

### WEEK 4 — GYMNASTICS AND MOVEMENT

**Main content**

• **Gymnastics** is moving the body in skillful ways.

• We can **roll** on the ground like a log.

• We can **balance** on one foot.

• We can **stretch** our arms up high.

• We can **bend** and **twist** our body.

• We can **hop** and **skip**.

• We move on a mat to be safe.

• Gymnastics makes the body strong and flexible.

• We do gymnastics with care.

• We warm up before we move.

**Let us talk**

1. What is gymnastics?

2. Name one movement we do.

3. Why do we use a mat?

**Words for my notebook**

• gymnastics — moving the body in skillful ways

• balance — to stay steady without falling

• roll — to turn the body over and over

### WEEK 5 — REST AND SLEEP

**Main content**

• **Rest** is taking it easy after work or play.

• We rest by sitting or lying down.

• **Sleep** is a deep rest at night.

• Sleep helps the body to grow and get strong.

• Children need much sleep.

• We sleep early and wake early.

• We sleep in a clean, quiet place.

• Rest and sleep keep us healthy.

• Too little sleep makes us tired and weak.

• We rest when we are tired.

**Let us talk**

1. What is rest?

2. Why do we sleep?

3. When do we sleep?

**Words for my notebook**

• rest — taking it easy after work or play

• sleep — a deep rest at night

• healthy — well and strong

**Worksheet**

   A) strong B) weak C) dirty D) sad

4. Too little sleep makes us tired and ___.
   A) weak B) strong C) tall D) happy

5. Fill in: A deep rest at night is _____.

6. Mention two ways we rest.

7. Write why we sleep.

### WEEK 6 — FOOD AND NUTRITION

**Main content**

• **Food** gives us energy and helps us grow.

• Good food makes the body strong.

• We eat **rice**, **yam**, **beans** and **pap**.

• We eat **fruits** and **vegetables**.

• We eat **fish**, **meat** and **eggs**.

• A **balanced diet** has all the food the body needs.

• We drink clean **water** every day.

• We wash our hands before eating.

• Bad or dirty food can make us sick.

• Good food and exercise keep us healthy.

### WEEK 8 — SAFETY IN PLAY

**Main content**

• We play to be happy and strong.

• We play in a **safe place**, not on the road.

• We do not push or hit our friends.

• We do not play with sharp or broken things.

• We wear the right clothes and shoes for play.

• We warm up before we play.

• We do not play too rough.

• We tell a grown-up if someone is hurt.

• We stop and take a rest if we feel weak.

• Safe play keeps us from harm.

**Let us talk**

1. Where do we play safely?

2. Name one danger in play.

3. What do we do if someone is hurt?

**Words for my notebook**

• safety — keeping away from harm

• rough play — play that can hurt

• warm up — to prepare the body before play

**Worksheet**

### WEEK 9 — THE VALUE OF EXERCISE

**Main content**

• **Exercise** is activity that moves the body.

• Running, jumping and playing are exercise.

• Exercise makes the **heart** strong.

• Exercise makes the **muscles** and **bones** strong.

• Exercise helps us to grow well.

• Exercise keeps us from sickness.

• Exercise makes us happy and fresh.

• We exercise every day.

• We warm up before and rest after exercise.

• A body that exercises is a healthy body.

**Let us talk**

1. What is exercise?

2. Name one good thing exercise does.

3. When do we exercise?

**Words for my notebook**

• exercise — activity that moves the body

• muscle — the part of the body that helps us move

• healthy — well and strong
