# Yoruba — Primary 1 · Second Term

These are the notes for the second term of Primary 1. They follow the NERDC
Basic Education Curriculum (2025 revision) and the school's scheme of work.
The nine weeks below are the weeks that carry a written note; the remaining
weeks of the term are for revision and the end-of-term paper, so no note is
written for them.

*Depth: NERDC Primary 1 · Yoruba · Second Term W1-6, 8-9, 11 (nọ́mbà 11-20; aṣọ; ilé-ẹ̀kọ́; ọjà; ìlú; iṣẹ́; oju ọjọ́; èso; ohun èlò ilé).*

---

### WEEK 1 — NỌ́MBÀ 11-20 (NUMBERS 11-20)

**You will learn to**

• count from 11 to 20 in Yoruba

• say the Yoruba word for each number

• count things around me

**Things to know**

• **Mọ́kànlá** means eleven (11).

• **Èjìlá** means twelve (12).

• **Ẹ̀tàlá** means thirteen (13).

• **Ẹ̀rìnlá** means fourteen (14).

• **Ẹ̀ẹ́dógún** means fifteen (15).

• **Ẹ̀rìndínlógún** means sixteen (16).

• **Ètàdínlógún** means seventeen (17).

• **Ìjìnlá** or **ìdínlógún** means eighteen (18).

• **Ogún** means twenty (20).

• We count on from ten to twenty.

**Let us talk**

1. How do you say eleven in Yoruba?

2. What is "ogún" in English?

3. Count from 11 to 15 in Yoruba.

**Words for my notebook**

• mọ́kànlá — eleven

• èjìlá — twelve

• ogún — twenty

**Worksheet**

1. "Mọ́kànlá" means ___.
   A) eleven B) twelve C) ten D) twenty

2. "Èjìlá" means ___.
   A) twelve B) eleven C) thirteen D) two

3. "Ogún" means ___.
   A) twenty B) ten C) twelve D) two

4. "Ẹ̀tàlá" means ___.
   A) thirteen B) fourteen C) twelve D) three

5. Fill in: Fifteen in Yoruba is _____.

6. Mention two numbers between 11 and 20 in Yoruba.

7. Write the Yoruba word for twenty.

**My own work**

• Trace and write the numbers 11 to 15 in Yoruba.

### WEEK 2 — AṢỌ (CLOTHES)

• **Bàtà** means shoes.

• **Fìlà** means a cap.

• **Àwò̀n** means a gown or dress.

• We wear clean clothes.

• We wash our clothes.

• We wear the right clothes for the weather.

**Let us talk**

**Words for my notebook**

• fìlà — a cap

### WEEK 4 — ỌJÀ (MARKET)

**You will learn to**

• **Olútàjà** means a seller.

• **Oníbàárà** means a buyer or customer.

• We buy food, clothes and other things.

• **Ẹ̀fọ́** means vegetables.

• **Eran** means meat.
