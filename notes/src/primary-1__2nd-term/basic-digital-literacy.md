# Basic Digital Literacy — Primary 1 · Second Term

These are the notes for the second term of Primary 1. They follow the NERDC
Basic Education Curriculum (2025 revision) and the school's scheme of work.
The eight weeks below are the weeks that carry a written note; the remaining
weeks of the term are for revision and the end-of-term paper, so no note is
written for them.

*Depth: NERDC Primary 1 · Basic Digital Literacy · Second Term W1-6, 8-9 (the desktop and icons; the mouse; the keyboard; typing; opening and closing; drawing; learning with the computer; safe habits).*

---

### WEEK 1 — THE DESKTOP AND ICONS

**Main content**

• The first screen we see is the **desktop**.

• The desktop has a **background** picture.

• Small pictures on the desktop are called **icons**.

• An **icon** stands for a program or a file.

• We click an icon to open it.

• The bar at the bottom is the **taskbar**.

• The small arrow we move is the **pointer**.

• The desktop shows what the computer can do.

• We keep the desktop tidy.

• The screen shows us our work.

**Let us talk**

1. What is the desktop?

2. What is an icon?

3. What do we call the small arrow?

**Words for my notebook**

• desktop — the first screen we see

• icon — a small picture that stands for a program

• pointer — the small arrow we move

**Worksheet**

1. The first screen we see is the ___.
   A) desktop B) keyboard C) mouse D) printer

2. A small picture that stands for a program is an ___.
   A) icon B) key C) wire D) screen

3. The small arrow we move is the ___.
   A) pointer B) icon C) taskbar D) button

4. We click an icon to ___ it.
   A) open B) close C) hide D) break

5. Fill in: The bar at the bottom of the screen is the _____.

6. Mention two things on the desktop.

7. Write what an icon is.

**My own work**

• Draw a computer screen with two icons.

### WEEK 2 — USING THE MOUSE

**Main content**

• The **mouse** moves the pointer on the screen.

• We hold the mouse gently with one hand.

• Moving the mouse moves the **pointer**.

• A **click** is pressing the left button once.

• A **double-click** is pressing the left button twice fast.

• We click to choose an item.

• **Drag** means holding the button and moving.

• We drag to move a picture.

• The right button opens a small menu.

• We use the mouse with care.

**Let us talk**

1. What does the mouse move?

2. What is a click?

3. What does drag mean?

**Words for my notebook**

• mouse — a device that moves the pointer

• click — to press the left button once

• drag — to hold and move

**Worksheet**

1. The mouse moves the ___ on the screen.
   A) pointer B) icon C) key D) wire

2. Pressing the left button once is a ___.
   A) click B) drag C) type D) save

3. Pressing the left button twice fast is a ___.
   A) double-click B) drag C) scroll D) shut

4. To move a picture we ___ it.
   A) drag B) sing C) hide D) cut

5. Fill in: We hold the mouse with one _____.

6. Mention two things we do with a mouse.

7. Write what a click is.

**My own work**

• Draw a mouse and the pointer it moves.

### WEEK 3 — THE KEYBOARD: LETTER AND NUMBER KEYS

**Main content**

• The **keyboard** has keys we press to type.

• The **letter keys** are the alphabet, A to Z.

• The **number keys** are 0 to 9.

• The **space bar** makes a space between words.

• The **enter key** moves to a new line.

• The **backspace key** rubs out a letter.

• The **shift key** helps us to type a capital letter.

• We press a key gently with one finger.

• The keyboard helps us to write on the computer.

• We keep the keyboard clean and dry.

**Let us talk**

1. What are the letter keys?

2. Which key makes a space?

3. What does the backspace key do?

**Words for my notebook**

• keyboard — the keys we press to type

• space bar — the key that makes a space

• enter key — the key that moves to a new line

**Worksheet**

1. The keys A to Z are the ___ keys.
   A) letter B) number C) function D) arrow

2. The key that makes a space is the ___.
   A) space bar B) enter key C) shift key D) backspace

3. The key that rubs out a letter is the ___.
   A) backspace B) space bar C) enter D) shift

4. The number keys are ___.
   A) 0 to 9 B) A to Z C) F1 to F12 D) red to blue

5. Fill in: We press a key gently with one _____.

6. Mention two special keys on the keyboard.

7. Write what the space bar does.

**My own work**

• Draw a keyboard and colour the letter keys.

### WEEK 4 — TYPING SIMPLE WORDS

**Main content**

• We type by pressing the letter keys.

• We look at the screen as we type.

• We type our name with the letter keys.

• We press the **space bar** between words.

• We press **enter** to start a new line.

• We use **backspace** to correct a mistake.

• We hold the **shift** key for a capital letter.

• We type slowly and carefully.

• Typing helps us to write on the computer.

• We sit well when we type.

**Let us talk**

1. How do we type a word?

2. Which key starts a new line?

3. How do we type a capital letter?

**Words for my notebook**

• type — to write with the keyboard

• capital letter — a big letter, typed with shift

• correct — to fix a mistake

**Worksheet**

1. We type by pressing the ___ keys.
   A) letter B) number C) arrow D) function

2. We press ___ between words.
   A) space bar B) enter C) shift D) backspace

3. To start a new line we press ___.
   A) enter B) space bar C) shift D) escape

4. We type a capital letter with the ___ key.
   A) shift B) enter C) space D) backspace

5. Fill in: To fix a mistake we use _____.

6. Mention two keys we use when typing.

7. Write your first name as you would type it.

### WEEK 5 — OPENING AND CLOSING A PROGRAM

**Main content**

• We **open** a program by clicking its icon.

• A program opens in a **window**.

• We work inside the window.

• We **save** our work so we do not lose it.

• We **close** a program when we finish.

• The small **x** at the corner closes a window.

• We save before we close.

• We do not switch off while the computer is working.

• We open and close with care.

• Saving keeps our work safe.

**Let us talk**

1. How do we open a program?

2. Why do we save our work?

3. How do we close a window?

**Words for my notebook**

• open — to start a program

• save — to keep our work safe

• close — to finish a program

**Worksheet**

1. We open a program by clicking its ___.
   A) icon B) wire C) key D) plug

2. A program opens in a ___.
   A) window B) box C) bag D) door

3. We ___ our work so we do not lose it.
   A) save B) hide C) cut D) lose

4. The small ___ at the corner closes a window.
   A) x B) o C) + D) −

5. Fill in: We save our work before we _____.

6. Mention two things we do with a program.

7. Write why we save our work.

**My own work**

### WEEK 6 — DRAWING WITH THE COMPUTER

**Main content**

• A **drawing program** lets us paint on the screen.

• The **pencil** tool draws a thin line.

• The **brush** tool draws a thick line.

• The **eraser** tool rubs out.

• The **fill** tool colours a shape.

• We choose a **colour** from the colour box.

• We draw shapes like a circle and a square.

• We can save our drawing.

• Drawing on the computer is fun and clean.

• We can print our drawing.

**Let us talk**

3. How do we choose a colour?

**Words for my notebook**

• drawing program — a program for painting on the screen

• brush — a tool that draws a thick line

• fill — a tool that colours a shape

**Worksheet**

### WEEK 8 — LEARNING WITH THE COMPUTER

• We can play **learning games** with numbers and letters.

### WEEK 9 — SAFE HABITS WITH THE COMPUTER

**Main content**

• We use the computer with a **grown-up** near.

• We do not eat or drink near the computer.

• We keep our hands **clean and dry**.

• We press keys **gently**.

• We do not pull the wires.

• We sit **well** and not too close to the screen.

• We rest our eyes from the screen.

• We do not talk to strangers online.

• We tell a grown-up if something looks wrong.

• Good habits keep us and the computer safe.

**Let us talk**

1. Why do we not eat near the computer?

2. How do we sit when using the computer?

3. What do we do if something looks wrong online?

**Words for my notebook**

• safely — in a way that keeps us from harm

• gently — softly, not hard

• stranger — a person we do not know

**Worksheet**

1. We use the computer with a ___ near.
   A) grown-up B) stranger C) pet D) baby

2. We keep our hands clean and ___.
   A) dry B) wet C) dirty D) oily

3. We press keys ___.
   A) gently B) hard C) fast only D) with a stick

4. We do not talk to ___ online.
   A) strangers B) teachers C) parents D) friends

5. Fill in: In a way that keeps us from harm is _____.

6. Mention two safe habits with the computer.

7. Write what we do if something looks wrong online.

**My own work**

• Draw myself sitting well at the computer.
