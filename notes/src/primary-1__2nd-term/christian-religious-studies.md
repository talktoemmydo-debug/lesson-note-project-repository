# Christian Religious Studies — Primary 1 · Second Term

These are the notes for the second term of Primary 1. They follow the NERDC
Basic Education Curriculum (2025 revision) and the school's scheme of work.
The eight weeks below are the weeks that carry a written note; the remaining
weeks of the term are for revision and the end-of-term paper, so no note is
written for them.

*Depth: NERDC Primary 1 · Christian Religious Studies · Second Term W1-6, 8-9 (the birth of Jesus; the shepherds and the wise men; Jesus as a boy; the baptism of Jesus; the temptation; Jesus calls the first disciples; water into wine; Jesus loves the children).*

---

### WEEK 1 — THE BIRTH OF JESUS

**Main content**

• An angel told **Mary** that she would have a baby.

• The baby's name would be **Jesus**.

• Mary and **Joseph** travelled to **Bethlehem**.

• Jesus was born in a **manger** in Bethlehem.

• A **manger** is a box where animals eat.

• There was no room for them in the inn.

• Jesus is the **Son of God**.

• God sent Jesus to the world out of love.

• We thank God for the birth of Jesus.

• Christmas is the time we remember the birth of Jesus.

**Let us talk**

1. Who told Mary about the baby?

2. Where was Jesus born?

3. What is a manger?

**Words for my notebook**

• Jesus — the Son of God, born in Bethlehem

• manger — a box where animals eat

• Bethlehem — the town where Jesus was born

**Worksheet**

1. An angel told ___ she would have a baby.
   A) Mary B) Martha C) Ruth D) Esther

2. Jesus was born in ___.
   A) Bethlehem B) Jerusalem C) Egypt D) Nazareth only

3. Jesus was born in a ___.
   A) manger B) palace C) tent D) boat

4. Jesus is the ___ of God.
   A) Son B) father C) brother D) friend only

5. Fill in: Mary and Joseph travelled to _____.

6. Mention two people in the birth story of Jesus.

7. Write where Jesus was born.

**My own work**

• Draw the baby Jesus in the manger.

### WEEK 2 — THE SHEPHERDS AND THE WISE MEN

**Main content**

• **Shepherds** were watching their sheep at night.

• An **angel** appeared and told them Jesus was born.

• The shepherds were afraid, then glad.

• The shepherds went to see the baby Jesus.

• **Wise men** came from the east.

• A **star** guided the wise men.

• The wise men brought **gifts**: gold, frankincense and myrrh.

• The wise men worshipped Jesus.

• We can give our best to Jesus.

• God told everyone that Jesus was born.

**Let us talk**

1. Who told the shepherds the good news?

2. What guided the wise men?

3. Name one gift the wise men brought.

**Words for my notebook**

• shepherds — men who look after sheep

• wise men — men who came from the east to see Jesus

• gifts — things we give, like the gold and myrrh

**Worksheet**

1. The ___ were watching their sheep at night.
   A) shepherds B) wise men C) soldiers D) kings

2. A ___ guided the wise men.
   A) star B) cloud C) lamp D) bird

3. The wise men brought gold, frankincense and ___.
   A) myrrh B) water C) bread D) stone

4. The wise men came from the ___.
   A) east B) sea C) north only D) market

5. Fill in: The shepherds went to see the baby _____.

6. Mention two gifts the wise men brought.

7. Write what guided the wise men.

**My own work**

• Draw the star and the wise men.

### WEEK 3 — JESUS AS A BOY

**Main content**

• Jesus grew up in **Nazareth**.

• Jesus was a good and obedient boy.

• Jesus helped Joseph and Mary.

• Jesus learned to work with wood.

• Jesus went with his family to the **temple** in Jerusalem.

• At twelve, Jesus stayed in the temple to talk with the teachers.

• Jesus listened and asked good questions.

• The teachers were amazed at Jesus.

• Jesus obeyed his mother and father.

• Jesus grew in wisdom and in love with God and people.

**Let us talk**

1. Where did Jesus grow up?

2. What did Jesus do at the temple?

3. How was Jesus as a boy?

**Words for my notebook**

• Nazareth — the town where Jesus grew up

• temple — the house of God in Jerusalem

• obey — to do what we are told

**Worksheet**

1. Jesus grew up in ___.
   A) Nazareth B) Bethlehem C) Egypt D) Rome

2. Jesus was a good and ___ boy.
   A) obedient B) lazy C) rude D) angry

3. At twelve, Jesus stayed in the ___ to talk with the teachers.
   A) temple B) market C) boat D) field

4. Jesus helped Joseph and ___.
   A) Mary B) Martha C) Ruth D) Esther

5. Fill in: Jesus obeyed his mother and _____.

6. Mention two things Jesus did as a boy.

7. Write where Jesus grew up.

**My own work**

• Draw Jesus helping at home in Nazareth.

### WEEK 4 — THE BAPTISM OF JESUS

**Main content**

• **John the Baptist** baptised people in the river.

• Jesus came to John to be baptised.

• John baptised Jesus in the **River Jordan**.

• When Jesus came up, the **heavens opened**.

• The **Holy Spirit** came down like a dove.

• A **voice** from heaven said, "This is my beloved Son."

• God the Father was pleased with Jesus.

• Baptism shows we belong to God.

• Jesus showed us to obey God.

• We remember the baptism of Jesus.

**Let us talk**

1. Who baptised Jesus?

2. Where was Jesus baptised?

3. What came down like a dove?

**Words for my notebook**

• baptise — to wash with water as a sign of belonging to God

• Holy Spirit — the Spirit of God, like a dove

• River Jordan — the river where Jesus was baptised

**Worksheet**

1. ___ baptised Jesus.
   A) John the Baptist B) Peter C) Paul D) Joseph

2. Jesus was baptised in the River ___.
   A) Jordan B) Nile C) sea D) lake

3. The Holy Spirit came down like a ___.
   A) dove B) fish C) lamb D) bird only

4. A voice from ___ said, "This is my beloved Son."
   A) heaven B) the sea C) the tree D) the ground

5. Fill in: Baptism shows we belong to _____.

6. Mention two things that happened at the baptism of Jesus.

7. Write who baptised Jesus.

**My own work**

• Draw the dove coming down at the baptism.

### WEEK 5 — THE TEMPTATION OF JESUS

**Main content**

• Jesus went into the **wilderness** and did not eat for forty days.

• The **devil** came to tempt Jesus.

• The devil said, "Turn these stones into bread."

• Jesus said, "Man does not live by bread alone."

• The devil told Jesus to jump from the temple.

• Jesus said, "Do not put the Lord your God to the test."

• The devil showed Jesus all the kingdoms.

• Jesus said, "Worship the Lord your God only."

• Jesus used the **word of God** to answer.

• The devil went away, and angels came to help Jesus.

**Let us talk**

1. Where did the devil tempt Jesus?

2. How did Jesus answer the devil?

3. What can we use to resist doing wrong?

**Words for my notebook**

• tempt — to try to make someone do wrong

• wilderness — a dry, empty place

• word of God — the Bible, which Jesus quoted

**Worksheet**

1. The devil tempted Jesus in the ___.
   A) wilderness B) temple C) sea D) market

2. Jesus did not eat for ___ days.
   A) forty B) seven C) twelve D) three

3. Jesus answered the devil with the ___.
   A) word of God B) help of man C) power of gold D) might of kings

4. The devil told Jesus to turn stones into ___.
   A) bread B) water C) gold D) sand

5. Fill in: To try to make someone do wrong is to _____.

6. Mention two ways the devil tempted Jesus.

7. Write what Jesus used to answer the devil.

**My own work**

• Draw Jesus in the wilderness.

### WEEK 6 — JESUS CALLS THE FIRST DISCIPLES

**Main content**

• A **disciple** is a person who follows and learns from Jesus.

• Jesus called **Simon Peter** and **Andrew** by the sea.

• They were fishermen, casting their nets.

• Jesus said, "Follow me, and I will make you fishers of men."

• Jesus called **James** and **John**, the sons of Zebedee.

• They left their nets and followed Jesus.

• Jesus chose **twelve** disciples to be with him.

• The disciples left their work to follow Jesus.

• We too can follow Jesus.

• Following Jesus means doing what he says.

**Let us talk**

1. What is a disciple?

2. Name two disciples Jesus called.

3. What were Peter and Andrew doing?

**Words for my notebook**

• disciple — one who follows and learns from Jesus

• Peter — a fisherman Jesus called

• follow — to go after and obey

**Worksheet**

1. A ___ follows and learns from Jesus.
   A) disciple B) stranger C) soldier D) king

2. Jesus called Peter and Andrew by the ___.
   A) sea B) temple C) mountain D) market

3. Peter and Andrew were ___.
   A) fishermen B) farmers C) teachers D) builders

4. Jesus chose ___ disciples.
   A) twelve B) seven C) five D) three

5. Fill in: Jesus said he would make them fishers of _____.

6. Mention two disciples Jesus called.

7. Write what a disciple is.

**My own work**

• Draw Jesus calling the fishermen.

### WEEK 8 — JESUS TURNS WATER INTO WINE

**Main content**

• Jesus and his mother went to a **wedding** at **Cana**.

• The disciples were there too.

• The wine for the guests ran out.

• Mary told Jesus, "They have no wine."

• Jesus told the servants to fill six big **jars** with water.

• The servants filled the jars to the top.

• Jesus turned the **water into wine**.

• The master of the feast tasted the good wine.

• This was the **first miracle** Jesus did.

• The disciples believed in Jesus.

**Let us talk**

1. Where was the wedding?

2. What did Jesus turn the water into?

3. Who told Jesus the wine had run out?

**Words for my notebook**

• Cana — the town of the wedding

• miracle — a wonderful thing only God can do

• wine — the drink Jesus made from water

**Worksheet**

1. The wedding was at ___.
   A) Cana B) Bethlehem C) Egypt D) Rome

2. Jesus turned water into ___.
   A) wine B) milk C) oil D) honey

3. ___ told Jesus the wine had run out.
   A) Mary B) Martha C) Ruth D) Esther

4. This was the ___ miracle Jesus did.
   A) first B) second C) last D) third

5. Fill in: A wonderful thing only God can do is a _____.

6. Mention two people at the wedding.

7. Write what Jesus turned the water into.

**My own work**

• Draw the six jars at the wedding.

### WEEK 9 — JESUS LOVES THE CHILDREN

**Main content**

• People brought their **children** to Jesus.

• The disciples tried to send the children away.

• Jesus said, "Let the little children come to me."

• Jesus said, "Do not stop them."

• Jesus **blessed** the children.

• Jesus took the children in his arms.

• Jesus said the kingdom of God belongs to such as these.

• Jesus loves **all** children.

• We can come to Jesus just as we are.

• We should love and care for one another.

**Let us talk**

1. Who brought the children to Jesus?

2. What did Jesus say about the children?

3. How did Jesus treat the children?

**Words for my notebook**

• bless — to ask God to do good for someone

• welcome — to receive with love

• kingdom of God — where God rules

**Worksheet**

1. People brought their ___ to Jesus.
   A) children B) money C) gold D) stones

2. The disciples tried to send the children ___.
   A) away B) home C) to school D) to bed

3. Jesus said, "Let the little children ___ to me."
   A) come B) run C) hide D) go

4. Jesus ___ the children.
   A) blessed B) scolded C) chased D) ignored

5. Fill in: Jesus loves all _____.

6. Mention two things Jesus did for the children.

7. Write what Jesus said about the children.

**My own work**

• Draw Jesus with the children.
