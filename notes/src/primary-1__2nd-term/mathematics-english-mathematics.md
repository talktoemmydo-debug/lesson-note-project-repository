### WEEK 1 — MULTIPLICATION (PART 1)

**Main content**

• **Multiplication** means adding the same number again and again.

• The times sign **×** means multiply.

• 2 × 3 means 2 added three times: 2 + 2 + 2 = 6.

• 2 × 3 = 6.

• We can multiply with sticks or stones in groups.

• 3 × 2 = 6.

• 5 × 2 = 10.

• Multiplication is a fast way to add.

• We count in groups to multiply.

• The answer of multiplication is the product.

**Let us talk**

1. What does the times sign mean?

2. What is 2 × 3?

3. How is multiplication like adding?

**Words for my notebook**

• multiplication — adding the same number again and again

• times sign — × , means multiply

• product — the answer of multiplication

**Worksheet**

1. Multiplication means adding the same number again and ___.
   A) again B) once C) never D) twice only

2. The times sign is ___.
   A) × B) + C) − D) =

3. 2 × 3 = ___.
   A) 6 B) 5 C) 8 D) 3

4. 5 × 2 = ___.
   A) 10 B) 7 C) 12 D) 5

5. Fill in: The answer of multiplication is the _____.

6. Mention two multiplication sums.

7. Write 3 × 2 and its answer.

**My own work**

• Draw 3 groups of 2 stones and count them all.

### WEEK 2 — MULTIPLICATION (PART 2)

**Main content**

• We can multiply bigger numbers.

• The **2 times table**: 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12.

• The **3 times table**: 3, 6, 9, 12, 15.

• The **5 times table**: 5, 10, 15, 20, 25.

• 4 × 3 = 12.

• 5 × 5 = 25.

• We skip-count by twos, threes and fives.

• Knowing the times tables helps us to multiply fast.

• Multiplication helps us in everyday life.

• We write our sums neatly.

3. What is 5 × 5?

**Words for my notebook**

### WEEK 3 — OPEN SENTENCES

**Main content**

• An **open sentence** has a missing number.

• We write the missing number as a **box** □ or a letter.

• 2 + □ = 5. The missing number is 3.

• □ + 4 = 7. The missing number is 3.

• 6 − □ = 2. The missing number is 4.

• We find the number that makes the sentence true.

• An open sentence is like a small puzzle.

• We use adding or subtracting to find the missing number.

• Open sentences help us to think.

• We check our answer by putting it back.

**Let us talk**

1. What is an open sentence?

2. What is the missing number in 2 + □ = 5?

3. How do we find the missing number?

**Words for my notebook**

• open sentence — a sum with a missing number

• missing number — the number we must find

• true — correct, as it should be

**Worksheet**

1. An open sentence has a ___ number.
   A) missing B) wrong C) loud D) dark

2. In 2 + □ = 5, the missing number is ___.
   A) 3 B) 2 C) 5 D) 7

3. In □ + 4 = 7, the missing number is ___.
   A) 3 B) 4 C) 7 D) 11

4. In 6 − □ = 2, the missing number is ___.
   A) 4 B) 6 C) 2 D) 8

5. Fill in: We find the number that makes the sentence _____.

6. Mention two open sentences.

7. Write the missing number in 5 + □ = 9.

**My own work**

• Draw and solve 4 + □ = 8.

### WEEK 4 — MONEY (PART 1)

**Main content**

• **Money** is what we use to buy things.

• Nigerian money is the **naira** and **kobo**.

• We have **coins** and **notes**.

• A coin can be 50 kobo or 1 naira.

• A note can be 5, 10, 20 or 50 naira.

• We count money to know how much we have.

• We pay money to buy things.

• We keep money safe.

• Money helps us to trade.

• We do not waste money.

**Let us talk**

2. Name one Nigerian coin or note.

3. Why do we count money?

**Words for my notebook**

• money — what we use to buy

• naira — the Nigerian money unit

• kobo — the small part of a naira

### WEEK 5 — MONEY (PART 2)

**Main content**

• We can **add** money to find the total.

• 2 naira + 3 naira = 5 naira.

• We add the naira and the kobo.

• When we pay too much, the seller gives **change**.

• Change is the money given back.

• If I pay 10 naira for a 6 naira sweet, the change is 4 naira.

• We count the change to be sure.

• We spend money wisely.

• We save money for later.

• Money problems use adding and subtracting.

**Let us talk**

2. What is change?

**Words for my notebook**

• change — the money given back

• save — to keep money for later

### WEEK 8 — TIME (PART 1)

**Main content**

• A **clock** tells us the time.

• The **short hand** tells the hour.

• The **long hand** tells the minutes.

• When the long hand is on 12, it is the hour.

• When the short hand is on 3 and the long hand on 12, it is **3 o'clock**.

• There are **12** hours on the clock face.

• There are **seven** days in a week.

• We do things at different times.

• We go to school in the morning.

• Knowing time helps us not to be late.

**Let us talk**

1. What does the short hand tell?

2. What is 3 o'clock?

3. How many days are in a week?

**Words for my notebook**

• clock — a thing that tells time

• hour — the time the short hand shows

• o'clock — the time on the hour

**Worksheet**

5. Fill in: The time on the hour is _____.
