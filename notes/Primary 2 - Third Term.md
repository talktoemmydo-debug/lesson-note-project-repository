# Basic Science

These are the notes for the third term of Primary 2. They follow the NERDC
Basic Education Curriculum (2025 revision) and the school's scheme of work.
The eight weeks below are the weeks that carry a written note; the remaining
weeks of the term are for revision and the end-of-term paper, so no note is
written for them.

*Depth: NERDC Primary 2 · Basic Science · Third Term W1-6, 8, 9 (change in nature; change in plants; change in animals; water in our environment; the mouth, teeth and tongue; the teeth; rocks; properties of living and non-living things).*


### WEEK 1 — CHANGE IN NATURE

**You will learn to**

• say what change is

• name changes in nature

• notice change around us

**Things to know**

• A **change** is when something becomes different.

• Nature **changes** every day.

• The **morning** changes into **afternoon**.

• The **day** changes into **night**.

• The **weather** changes from **sunny** to **rainy**.

• The **seasons** change through the year.

• A **seed** changes into a **plant**.

• A **baby** changes into a **child**.

• Change happens all around us.

• We can watch and learn from change.

**Let us talk**

1. What is change?

2. Name one change in nature.

3. What changes in the sky each day?

**Words for my notebook**

• change — when something becomes different

• nature — the world of plants, animals, sky and land

• season — a time of the year

**Worksheet**

1. When something becomes different, that is a ___.
   A) change B) stone C) tree D) cloud

2. The day changes into ___.
   A) night B) stone C) tree D) cloud

3. The weather changes from sunny to ___.
   A) rainy B) dry C) hard D) dark

4. A seed changes into a ___.
   A) plant B) stone C) cloud D) star

5. Fill in: A time of the year is a _____.

6. Mention two changes you see in nature.

7. Write what change is.

**My own work**

• Draw and colour day and night.

### WEEK 2 — CHANGE IN PLANTS

**You will learn to**

• say how plants change

• name the stages of a plant

• care for plants

**Things to know**

• Plants **change** as they grow.

• A **seed** is planted in the **soil**.

• The seed takes in **water**.

• A small **shoot** comes out of the seed.

• The shoot grows **leaves**.

• The plant grows **taller**.

• The plant grows **flowers**.

• The flowers become **fruit**.

• The fruit has new **seeds**.

• We water plants so they grow well.

**Let us talk**

1. Where do we plant a seed?

2. What comes out of the seed first?

3. What do flowers become?

**Words for my notebook**

• seed — what a plant grows from

• shoot — the first small growth

• flower — the part that becomes fruit

**Worksheet**

1. We plant a seed in the ___.
   A) soil B) sky C) fire D) stone

2. A small ___ comes out of the seed.
   A) shoot B) stone C) cloud D) star

3. The plant grows ___ after the shoot.
   A) leaves B) stones C) clouds D) stars

4. Flowers become ___.
   A) fruit B) stones C) clouds D) stars

5. Fill in: The first small growth is a _____.

6. Mention two things a plant needs to grow.

7. Write how a plant changes.

**My own work**

• Draw and colour a seed, a shoot and a flower.

### WEEK 4 — WATER IN OUR ENVIRONMENT

**You will learn to**

• say where water is

• name uses of water

• keep water clean

**Things to know**

• **Water** is all around us.

• Water is in the **river**, **stream** and **lake**.

• Water is in the **sea** and **ocean**.

• Water falls as **rain**.

• We get water from the **tap** and **well**.

• We **drink** water every day.

• We **bathe** and **wash** with water.

• We **cook** with water.

• Plants and animals **need** water.

• We keep water **clean**.

**Let us talk**

1. Name two places where we find water.

2. Name two uses of water.

3. Why do we keep water clean?

**Words for my notebook**

• water — the clear liquid we drink

• river — a large stream of water

• rain — water that falls from the sky

### WEEK 5 — THE MOUTH, TEETH AND TONGUE

**You will learn to**

• name the parts of the mouth

• say what each part does

• care for the mouth

**Things to know**

• The **mouth** helps us eat and speak.

• Inside the mouth are the **teeth**.

• The **teeth** chew our food.

• The **tongue** tastes our food.

• The tongue helps us **speak**.

• The tongue can taste **sweet**, **sour**, **salt** and **bitter**.

• The **lips** close the mouth.

• The **gums** hold the teeth.

• We clean our mouth every day.

• A clean mouth keeps us healthy.

**Let us talk**

### WEEK 6 — THE TEETH

**You will learn to**

• name the kinds of teeth

• say what teeth do

• care for the teeth

**Things to know**

• We have **two sets** of teeth.

• The first set are the **milk teeth**.

• The second set are the **adult teeth**.

• **Front teeth** cut our food.

• **Back teeth** chew and grind our food.

• Teeth help us **speak** clearly.

• We **brush** our teeth every morning and night.

• We use a **toothbrush** and **toothpaste**.

• We do not eat too many **sweets**.

• Clean teeth keep us healthy.

**Let us talk**

1. What are the first teeth called?

2. What do front teeth do?

3. When do we brush our teeth?

**Words for my notebook**

• milk teeth — the first teeth

• adult teeth — the second teeth

• brush — to clean the teeth

**Worksheet**

   A) Back B) Front C) Side D) Top

   A) toothpaste B) sand C) oil D) ink

5. Fill in: The second set of teeth are the _____ teeth.

6. Mention two ways to care for the teeth.

7. Write what teeth help us do.

### WEEK 8 — NON-LIVING THINGS: ROCKS

**You will learn to**

• say what a rock is

• name kinds of rocks

• tell rocks from living things

**Things to know**

• A **rock** is a non-living thing.

• Rocks are **hard** and **solid**.

• Rocks are found on the **ground** and in the **ground**.

• Some rocks are **big**; some are **small**.

• Some rocks are **smooth**; some are **rough**.

• Rocks do **not** grow.

• Rocks do **not** eat or breathe.

• Rocks do **not** have young ones.

• We use rocks to **build**.

• We use small rocks for **paths**.

**Let us talk**

1. What is a rock?

2. Name one thing rocks do not do.

3. Name one use of rocks.

**Words for my notebook**

• rock — a hard non-living thing

• non-living — not alive

• rough — not smooth

**Worksheet**

1. A rock is a ___ thing.
   A) non-living B) living C) growing D) eating

2. Rocks are ___ and solid.
   A) hard B) soft C) wet D) warm

3. Rocks do not ___.
   A) grow B) stay C) sit D) wait

4. We use rocks to ___.
   A) build B) eat C) drink D) sing

5. Fill in: Not alive is _____.

6. Mention two things rocks do not do.

7. Write what a rock is.

**My own work**

• Draw and colour a big rock and a small rock.

### WEEK 9 — LIVING AND NON-LIVING THINGS

**You will learn to**

• tell living from non-living

• name properties of each

• give examples

**Things to know**

• **Living things** are alive.

• Living things **grow**.

• Living things **eat** and **drink**.

• Living things **breathe**.

• Living things **move**.

• Living things have **young ones**.

• **Non-living things** are not alive.

• Non-living things do **not** grow or eat.

• Non-living things do **not** breathe.

• Examples: **plants**, **animals** and **people** are living; **rocks**, **water** and **chairs** are non-living.

**Let us talk**

1. Name two things living things do.

2. Name one non-living thing.

3. How are living and non-living things different?

**Words for my notebook**

• living thing — something that is alive

• non-living thing — something that is not alive

• breathe — to take in air

**Worksheet**

1. Living things ___.
   A) grow B) stay still C) sleep only D) do nothing

2. Living things ___ and drink.
   A) eat B) burn C) break D) hide

3. Living things ___.
   A) breathe B) melt C) rust D) fade

4. A ___ is a non-living thing.
   A) rock B) tree C) dog D) child

5. Fill in: To take in air is to _____.

6. Mention two living things.

7. Write the difference between living and non-living things.

**My own work**

• Draw and colour one living thing and one non-living thing.
