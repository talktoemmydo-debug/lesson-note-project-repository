Scheme of Work (10 pillars)

General Knowledge

Source: General-Knowledge-Scheme-of-Work.html

Design

Purpose: a single, complete General Knowledge stream that builds a pupil's world awareness year after year so that, by the end of Primary 4, he or she is fully equipped for the General Paper / General Knowledge sections of the Common Entrance Examination (NCEE), state placement tests and private secondary school entrance examinations.. Alignment: the NCEE General Paper (the highest-scoring paper — 60 of 140 marks) tests General Knowledge, Current Affairs, Social Studies, Basic Science, Civic Education, Nigerian History and Geography. Every term in this scheme is mapped to those seven domains, plus Verbal and Quantitative Reasoning skills.. Spiral model: ten pillars are revisited each year at a higher level — Myself & Family · Nigeria & Nationhood · Geography (Nigeria, Africa, World) · Government & Civics · History & Heritage · Science & Environment · Technology & Innovation · Sports & Entertainment · Money & Trade · Current Affairs & Everyday Knowledge.. Global standard: the structure mirrors Cambridge Primary Global Perspectives strands (health & wellbeing, looking after planet Earth, working with other countries, values and beliefs, the world of work, sport and recreation) and the internationally recognised General Knowledge pillars (static facts, history, geography, civics, science, current affairs).. Current affairs: each term carries a Current Affairs block. Facts change, so the scheme fixes the AREAS to teach, while the Appendix (Current Affairs Snapshot, dated August 2026) gives the anchoring facts to teach now. Schools must refresh the snapshot each academic year.. Week-9 structure: teaching weeks are 1–6 and 8–9 of every term; Week 7 is Mid-Term Break, Week 10 Revision, Week 11 Examination and Week 12 Closing/Awards. Teaching never goes past Week 9, exactly as in the rest of the school's compressed scheme.
